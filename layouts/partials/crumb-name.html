{{- /* Short name for a page in a breadcrumb trail. nav_title wins when a page sets one. Otherwise the title up to its first " | ", because titles are written for the search result and carry a price or strapline after the pipe ("Leeds Tutors | GCSE & A-Level from £37/hr"), which Google shows word for word in the breadcrumb under the result. */ -}} {{- $name := .Params.nav_title | default .LinkTitle -}} {{- return strings.TrimSpace (index (split $name " | ") 0) -}}
