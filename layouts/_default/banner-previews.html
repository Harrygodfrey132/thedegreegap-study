What's wired up

  • All four banners click through to /referred-by-school-guide/ with rel="sponsored", per Google's paid-link guidance.
  • All four banners are static per partner request (no GIFs). We can test an animated GIF version later if click-through-rate warrants it.
  • Every key message visible at a glance, designed for scan-reading on partner sites.
  • Banners now carry the full The Degree Gap wordmark logo (not the favicon) for brand awareness.
  • For delivery, banners will be exported as PNG files per School Guide's ad system, well under the 1000kb cap.
