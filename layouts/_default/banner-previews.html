What's wired up

  • Click target on all three: /referred-by-school-guide/. Swap per partner when each placement goes live.
  • Animated banners (728 × 90 and 320 × 100) stop after 3 rotations and land on the first message, per IAB spec.
  • Skyscraper is static for a sidebar context, every key message visible at a glance.
  • All three respect prefers-reduced-motion: animations off, first frame retained.
  • Built in HTML/CSS, can ship as HTML5 or be exported as GIF (well under the 1000kb cap either way).
  • Cream banner against the navy header gives strong contrast; the same cream against the white body needs the border + shadow you can see on the skyscraper to hold its edge.
  • The blue background colour here is a placeholder for the School Guide navy; if Victoria sends the exact hex we'll swap it.
