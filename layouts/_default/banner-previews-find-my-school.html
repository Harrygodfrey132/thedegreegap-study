FindMySchool banner creatives and partnership breakdown

Six units built from the supplied mockup, recoloured to the real brand burgundy and cut to fixed IAB sizes so they drop into standard ad slots. Hover any banner to see the click state. The whole banner is one clickable link.

Evaluation of the supplied mockup

What works and has been kept: the pale burgundy ground reads as distinct against a mostly-white host site without shouting. "Grades not where they should be?" is a good parent-pain opener and sits right in our voice. The pill CTA matches the banner family already running with School Guide.

What had to be fixed before it could ship:

  • It was not a link. The CTA was a <button> with an onclick handler left over from the tool that generated it. No href, no destination, no tracking. Now a real anchor carrying FindMySchool UTMs.
  • Wrong burgundy. The mockup used #6B1F2E. Brand burgundy is #800020, which is visibly warmer and more saturated. Corrected throughout.
  • Undefined CSS variables. var(--surface-1) and var(--font-voice) do not exist outside the generating tool, so on FindMySchool's site the banner would have rendered with a transparent background and a default system font.
  • Missing icon font. The book and arrow glyphs were Tabler icons (class="ti ti-book-2"). FindMySchool will not be loading that font, so both would have rendered as empty boxes. Replaced with inline SVG.
  • No trust signal at all. This is the biggest one. The 5.0 rating, the {{ $reviews }} Google reviews and the Prestige award are our strongest conversion levers and none of them appeared. All three are now in, sized to fit each unit.
  • No fixed dimensions. The unit was fluid width. Ad slots are fixed sizes, so it has been cut to 728×90, 300×250, 300×600, 160×600 and 320×100, plus a fluid native version for the editorial placement.
  • House style. "A-level" corrected to "A-Level".

PARTNERSHIP PROPOSAL · CONDENSED

The Degree Gap × FindMySchool

A performance partnership for GCSE and A-Level families, paid per converted customer. Prepared for FindMySchool (Leovate Ltd), August 2026.

The offer, in one line

FindMySchool earns £75 for every referred enquiry that becomes a paying customer. Nothing is charged for impressions, clicks or enquiries that do not convert. Creative is supplied by us and adapted per page, subject to FindMySchool's design approval.

Why the fit works

  • Intent. Parents comparing league tables have exam performance front of mind. Tutoring is the adjacent action.
  • Audience. State schools are 74% of school profile visits, and those families are the core tutoring market.
  • Timing. Traffic peaks at spring allocation and August results, which is exactly when tutoring demand spikes.
  • Geography. London is 35% of sixth form page visits, where tutor availability and willingness to pay are both highest.

Check the conservative row before sending. The base case and strong rows both reconcile against 20,000 page views: 0.8% of 20,000 is 160 clicks, 12% of those is 19 leads, which matches the stated 15–20. The strong row lands on 45, matching 40–45. The conservative row does not: 0.2% of 20,000 is 40 clicks and 8% of those is 3, not the 5–8 shown. It would need roughly 35,000 to 50,000 page views to produce that range. Either the row is using a different traffic base or the figure is overstated, and it is the one number a partner is most likely to check.

Worth knowing too: a 0.8% base-case CTR is well above typical display performance, where 0.05% to 0.1% is normal and contextual placements rarely clear 0.5%. The numbers are defensible as a target for well-matched native units, but they are the optimistic end. The 60-day pilot is what settles it.

Commercial terms

  • £75 per converted customer. No charge for impressions, clicks or unconverted enquiries.
  • 90-day attribution. Anyone who first arrives via FindMySchool tracking and books within 90 days counts as referred.
  • Independent visibility. Leads land on a dedicated page with FindMySchool UTMs and a unique form endpoint, so FindMySchool sees every lead it sends without relying on us to report it.
  • Monthly conversion report against that lead list, with reasonable audit rights on request.

Open items before this goes out

  • The results-day window has effectively closed. A-Level results were 13 August and GCSE results are 20 August. "Launched before GCSE results day" now means live within six days, including FindMySchool's design approval and ad trafficking. Either move the pilot start to the September and October catch-up window, which is still strong for tutoring demand, or scope a much smaller results-week test on one placement.
  • The dedicated landing page does not exist yet. The proposal promises referred-by-find-my-school with its own form endpoint. These banners currently point at /book-a-call/ carrying the right UTMs, so nothing is broken, but the unique endpoint needs building before launch or the attribution promise cannot be kept.
  • Section 6, the campaign calendar, did not come through either. Same as the figures table.
  • Contact block is unfilled in the proposal footer: name, email and phone are still placeholders.
