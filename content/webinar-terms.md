_Last updated 2 November 2026._

These terms and conditions ("Terms") cover your registration for, and attendance at, any free online webinar run by The Degree Gap ("we", "us", "our") for students, parents and guardians, and schools (each "a Webinar"). They also explain how we use the personal information you give us.

You accept these Terms by ticking the box to accept them and registering. If you do not agree, please do not register.

Accepting these Terms does **not** sign you up to our newsletter or to any marketing. That is a separate choice, made with its own optional box on the registration form (see section 6).

## In short

- **Everyone** who registers gets the emails needed for the Webinar: the joining link, a reminder and the replay.
- **Our weekly newsletter**, which includes invitations to future free webinars, is only for people who tick the newsletter box. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link.
- **Students** only ever give us an email address. We never ask a student for a phone number, and we never call or text a student. Students under 16 never receive the newsletter.
- **Parents and guardians** may give us a phone number if they want to. It is optional. We use it to send a reminder before the Webinar starts. If you also tick the box saying you are interested in academic support, we will call you afterwards and may text or WhatsApp you about tutoring.
- You can stop hearing from us at any time (see section 8).

The Degree Gap is the data controller responsible for the personal information you give us when you register.

For any question about these Terms, a Webinar or your personal information, email [admin@thedegreegap.com](mailto:admin@thedegreegap.com).

2.1 A Webinar is a free, live online session for secondary school and sixth form students, their parents and guardians, and school and college staff. We make a recording ("the replay") and any slides available to registrants afterwards.

2.2 A Webinar gives general information and guidance only. It is not financial, legal, medical or other professional advice, and it does not replace advice from a student's school, college, exam board or a qualified adviser. Any decision you make after attending is your own.

2.3 We may change the date, time, format, speakers or content of a Webinar, or cancel it. If we cancel, we will tell registrants by email.

## 3. Who can register

3.1 Parents and guardians, school and college staff, and students aged 13 or over may register.

3.2 If you register as a parent or guardian, you confirm that you are 18 or over.

3.3 If you are a student under 13, please ask a parent or guardian to register for you.

3.4 You confirm that the details you give are accurate and that you are entitled to give any contact details you enter.

3.5 We may refuse, cancel or remove any registration at our discretion.

## 4. The personal information we collect

When you register, we collect:

- your name;
- your email address;
- whether you are registering as a parent or guardian, a student, or a member of school or college staff;
- the year group of the student the registration relates to (we do not ask school staff for this);
- whether you ticked the box saying you are interested in academic support;
- whether you ticked the box to receive our weekly newsletter;
- if you register as a parent or guardian, your phone number, but only if you choose to give it; and
- limited technical and analytics data about your visit (see section 11).

A phone number is never required to register. We only ask a parent or guardian for one. We never ask a student or a member of school staff for a phone number.

## 6. The messages you will receive

6.1 **Webinar emails (everyone).** After you register, we email you the joining link before the Webinar and a reminder on the day. Afterwards, we email you the replay, any slides and follow-up resources. These emails are about the Webinar you registered for, not marketing.

6.2 **Our weekly newsletter (only if you tick the box).** The registration form has a separate, optional box to receive our newsletter. It is unticked unless you tick it, and you can register for the Webinar without it. If you tick it, we add you to our email list and send you the newsletter once a week. It includes free resources, guidance and invitations to our future free webinars. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. We do not offer the newsletter to students under 16, and we never add them to it.

6.3 **Phone, text and WhatsApp (parents and guardians only).** If you register as a parent or guardian and give us a phone number:

- we may send you a reminder by text or WhatsApp shortly before the Webinar starts; and
- only if you also ticked the box saying you are interested in academic support, we will call you after the Webinar to talk about tutoring for your child, and we may follow up by text or WhatsApp.

If you did not tick the academic support box, we will not call or message you about tutoring. You can tell us to stop calling or messaging you at any time, and every text tells you how to opt out (see section 8).

6.4 **Students.** Students only ever give us an email address. We never ask a student for a phone number, and we never call, text or WhatsApp a student. A student who ticks the academic support box may receive an email from us about it, and nothing else.

## 7. Consent

7.1 Your consent to our weekly newsletter is given by ticking the newsletter box. Your consent to calls and messages about tutoring is given by ticking the academic support box and giving your phone number. Neither is required to register for a Webinar.

7.2 You can withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing it does not affect anything we sent before you withdrew it.

7.3 We never sell your personal information, and we never pass it to anyone else for their own marketing.

## 8. How to stop hearing from us

You can stop any of our messages at any time:

- **Newsletter and marketing emails:** click the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email.
- **Texts:** reply **STOP**.
- **WhatsApp:** reply **STOP**, or tell us in the chat.
- **Calls:** tell us on the call that you would rather not be called again.
- **Anything else:** email [admin@thedegreegap.com](mailto:admin@thedegreegap.com).

We act on your request promptly. When you unsubscribe, we keep your email address or phone number on a "do not contact" list, so that we do not contact you again by mistake. Unsubscribing from the newsletter does not stop emails about a Webinar you have registered for; if you want those to stop too, email us and we will remove your registration.
