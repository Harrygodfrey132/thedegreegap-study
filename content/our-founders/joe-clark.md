I'm Joe, one of the two people behind The Degree Gap. Harry Godfrey and I grew up together, so when we say we've been doing this side by side for a long time, we mean it.

I was tutored at school too. Like Harry, I had two tutors, and like Harry, one of them was excellent and the other was only okay. You don't forget a contrast like that. It's the reason we put so much time into matching.

I did well across the board at GCSE. At A-Level we both had Economics tutoring, and we both came out with A\*AA after being predicted lower. I'd put a big part of that down to having the right person in the room.

I went on to study Business Management and Marketing at Pearson College London, with internships in sales and marketing along the way. At university I got back into tutoring, this time teaching Business, and found I loved it. After a spell working in finance and recruitment, Harry and I came back to education and started The Degree Gap together.

## What I do now

A big part of my job is talking to parents. Every family has a free call with Harry or me before any tutor is suggested, and on that call I'm trying to get a feel for your child: what's gone wrong, what they're like, and who they'd actually listen to. Then we send 2 or 3 tutor profiles within 24 hours.

Harry and I interview every tutor ourselves before they join the platform. Knowing the subject gets them through the door. How they explain it, and how they are with young people, is what decides it.

I also spend time in schools running workshops, including our Dragon's Den revision sessions, which are open to any pupil. And I write for the site, mostly about the decisions parents get stuck on: when to start tutoring, how to pick a tutor, and which A-Levels to choose.
