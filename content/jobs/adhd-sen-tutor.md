## Overview

We are hiring experienced ADHD & SEN tutors to teach one to one online. Students with ADHD, dyslexia or other additional needs usually do not need easier work. They need the same work taught in a way that fits how they process it.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for ADHD & SEN continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Adapting explanation and pacing to how the student actually learns
- Teaching exam subjects to full standard rather than reducing expectations
- Working with access arrangements where they are in place
- Building routines and strategies that hold up outside sessions

## What we look for

You have real experience with additional needs, not only patience. We ask about specific approaches at interview.

Alongside that, every ADHD & SEN tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching ADHD & SEN
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach ADHD & SEN specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
