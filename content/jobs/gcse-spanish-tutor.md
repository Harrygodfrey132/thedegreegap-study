## Overview

We are hiring experienced GCSE Spanish tutors to teach one to one online. GCSE Spanish students often have reasonable vocabulary and lose marks on tense accuracy and the speaking exam.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for GCSE Spanish continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Preparing the speaking exam, including role play and photo card
- Teaching tense accuracy, which separates the middle and top bands
- Building vocabulary that is actually usable under exam conditions
- Working translation and the writing tasks

## What we look for

You can build confidence and accuracy together rather than trading one for the other.

Alongside that, every GCSE Spanish tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching GCSE Spanish at this level
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach GCSE Spanish specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
