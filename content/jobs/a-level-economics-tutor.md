## Overview

We are hiring experienced A-Level Economics tutors to teach one to one online. A-Level Economics rewards the essay and the diagram working together. Students who can explain a concept in conversation often cannot get it onto the page in the form the examiner wants.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for A-Level Economics continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Teaching essay structure, including evaluation and judgement, which carry the higher marks
- Working diagrams as part of the argument rather than decoration
- Teaching data response technique against the specification's assessment objectives
- Covering both micro and macro to the same standard

## What we look for

You can teach judgement. Descriptive answers hit a ceiling quickly at this level.

Alongside that, every A-Level Economics tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching A-Level Economics at this level
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach A-Level Economics specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
