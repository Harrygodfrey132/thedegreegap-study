## Overview

We are hiring experienced A-Level Biology tutors to teach one to one online. A-Level Biology is a volume problem. The content does not stop, and students who coped at GCSE by revising late find that approach stops working in Year 12.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for A-Level Biology continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Teaching the specification at the depth the papers actually ask for
- Building a working system for retaining content across two years
- Teaching the statistics and data analysis that appear in the papers
- Supporting the practical endorsement alongside written preparation

## What we look for

You can teach students how to hold two years of content, not only what is in it.

Alongside that, every A-Level Biology tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching A-Level Biology at this level
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach A-Level Biology specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
