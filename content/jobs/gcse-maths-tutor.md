## Overview

We are hiring experienced GCSE Maths tutors to teach one to one online. GCSE Maths splits into foundation and higher tier, and a good deal of our work is students sitting the wrong one, or sitting the right one with gaps from Year 8 still open underneath.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for GCSE Maths continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Teaching across both tiers, and being honest with a family when the tier looks wrong for the student
- Rebuilding number and algebra fluency before exam technique, because the marks rarely come back without it
- Working the non-calculator paper as its own skill rather than assuming it follows from the rest
- Teaching students to show method, since most papers award marks for working even when the answer is wrong

## What we look for

You can find the actual gap. Most students who come to us have been told to practise more papers, which does very little when the problem sits three years further back.

Alongside that, every GCSE Maths tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching GCSE Maths at this level
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach GCSE Maths specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
