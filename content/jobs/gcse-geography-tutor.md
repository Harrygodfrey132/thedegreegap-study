## Overview

We are hiring experienced GCSE Geography tutors to teach one to one online. GCSE Geography turns on case studies. Students who have learned them as facts rather than as evidence for a specific question lose marks in the longer answers.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for GCSE Geography continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Teaching case studies as evidence to be deployed, not paragraphs to be recited
- Working the extended answers where most of the available marks sit
- Teaching map, graph and data skills, which appear throughout the papers
- Preparing for the fieldwork questions on the student's specification

## What we look for

You can teach a student to answer the question asked, using the case study rather than reciting it.

Alongside that, every GCSE Geography tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching GCSE Geography at this level
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach GCSE Geography specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
