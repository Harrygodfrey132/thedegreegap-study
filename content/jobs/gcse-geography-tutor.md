## About the role

The Degree Gap is recruiting experienced GCSE Geography tutors for online one-to-one teaching.

GCSE Geography assesses the applied use of case studies. Students who have learned them as blocks of content rather than as evidence underperform on extended responses.

Students are matched to tutors by one of the co-founders following a consultation with the family. You will receive the student's current attainment, target grade and relevant background before the first session. We recruit for GCSE Geography on a rolling basis.

## Responsibilities

- Teach case studies as evidence applied to a specific question
- Develop extended response technique, where the majority of marks sit
- Deliver cartographic, graphical and data skills
- Prepare students for the fieldwork assessment

## Requirements

Candidates must be able to teach applied use of case study material rather than recall.

- A minimum of two years' teaching or tutoring experience
- Demonstrable experience teaching GCSE Geography at this level
- An enhanced DBS certificate, verified before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable internet connection and a quiet teaching environment

## Terms

The role is self-employed and part-time, with hours set by the tutor. Students are allocated according to your stated availability and subject coverage. Sessions are scheduled and paid through the platform, so tutors do not invoice families directly and are not required to advertise or source their own students. A named contact is available for scheduling and safeguarding matters.

## Selection process

All applicants are interviewed by a co-founder before appointment. The interview covers subject knowledge, teaching approach, and experience of supporting students working below their target grade. We appoint a small proportion of applicants.
