## About the role

The Degree Gap is recruiting experienced GCSE Combined Science tutors for online one-to-one teaching.

Combined Science covers biology, chemistry and physics within a single qualification, and referred students typically require support across all three.

Students are matched to tutors by one of the co-founders following a consultation with the family. You will receive the student's current attainment, target grade and relevant background before the first session. We recruit for GCSE Combined Science on a rolling basis.

## Responsibilities

- Teach biology, chemistry and physics to GCSE Combined Science standard
- Deliver the required practicals and the written questions arising from them
- Teach the mathematical content within the chemistry and physics papers
- Manage revision across three disciplines

## Requirements

Candidates must be genuinely competent across all three sciences. Single subject specialists are placed on the relevant separate science role.

- A minimum of two years' teaching or tutoring experience
- Demonstrable experience teaching GCSE Combined Science at this level
- An enhanced DBS certificate, verified before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable internet connection and a quiet teaching environment

## Terms

The role is self-employed and part-time, with hours set by the tutor. Students are allocated according to your stated availability and subject coverage. Sessions are scheduled and paid through the platform, so tutors do not invoice families directly and are not required to advertise or source their own students. A named contact is available for scheduling and safeguarding matters.

## Selection process

All applicants are interviewed by a co-founder before appointment. The interview covers subject knowledge, teaching approach, and experience of supporting students working below their target grade. We appoint a small proportion of applicants.

## How to apply

Contact us by WhatsApp or email with a summary of your teaching experience and the subjects and levels you cover.
