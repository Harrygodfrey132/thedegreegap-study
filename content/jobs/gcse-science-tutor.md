## Overview

We are hiring experienced GCSE Combined Science tutors to teach one to one online. Combined Science is three subjects on one timetable, and students often need the same tutor across all three. It is a demanding brief and we recruit for it separately.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for GCSE Combined Science continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Teaching biology, chemistry and physics to GCSE standard across the combined specification
- Handling the required practicals, which come up in the written papers
- Teaching the maths content in the physics and chemistry papers, which catches out students who are weaker at maths
- Managing revision across three subjects without letting one quietly drop

## What we look for

You are genuinely comfortable in all three. We would rather place a specialist in one subject than a generalist who is thin in two.

Alongside that, every GCSE Combined Science tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching GCSE Combined Science at this level
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach GCSE Combined Science specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
