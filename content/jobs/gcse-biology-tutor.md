## About the role

The Degree Gap is recruiting experienced GCSE Biology tutors for online one-to-one teaching.

GCSE Biology is frequently treated as a recall subject, and marks are most often lost on application questions set in unfamiliar contexts.

Students are matched to tutors by one of the co-founders following a consultation with the family. You will receive the student's current attainment, target grade and relevant background before the first session. We recruit for GCSE Biology on a rolling basis.

## Responsibilities

- Deliver the specification content alongside the required practicals
- Develop application technique for unfamiliar experimental contexts
- Teach extended response questions, which are assessed on structure
- Cover the mathematical and graphical skills assessed in the papers

## Requirements

Candidates must be able to move students from recall to application.

- A minimum of two years' teaching or tutoring experience
- Demonstrable experience teaching GCSE Biology at this level
- An enhanced DBS certificate, verified before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable internet connection and a quiet teaching environment

## Terms

The role is self-employed and part-time, with hours set by the tutor. Students are allocated according to your stated availability and subject coverage. Sessions are scheduled and paid through the platform, so tutors do not invoice families directly and are not required to advertise or source their own students. A named contact is available for scheduling and safeguarding matters.

## Selection process

All applicants are interviewed by a co-founder before appointment. The interview covers subject knowledge, teaching approach, and experience of supporting students working below their target grade. We appoint a small proportion of applicants.

## How to apply

Contact us by WhatsApp or email with a summary of your teaching experience and the subjects and levels you cover.
