## Overview

We are hiring experienced GCSE Biology tutors to teach one to one online. GCSE Biology looks like the memorisable science and then students lose marks on application questions about experiments they have never seen.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for GCSE Biology continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Teaching the specification content alongside the required practicals
- Working application questions that put familiar biology in an unfamiliar context
- Teaching the extended six-mark answers, which need structure rather than more facts
- Covering the maths and graph skills that appear in the papers

## What we look for

You can move a student from recall to application. Knowing the content is rarely the thing holding a grade back.

Alongside that, every GCSE Biology tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching GCSE Biology at this level
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach GCSE Biology specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
