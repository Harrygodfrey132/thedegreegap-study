## How applying works

1. **Send one application.** A short summary of your teaching experience and the subjects and levels you cover. One application covers every subject.
2. **Interview.** We check your subject knowledge, including the specification and the mark schemes, and verify your enhanced DBS certificate before any student is assigned.
3. **Matching.** A co-founder matches each student to a tutor after a consultation with the family. All lessons are online, and you set your own hours.
