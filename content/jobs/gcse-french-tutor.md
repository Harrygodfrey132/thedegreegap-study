## Overview

We are hiring experienced GCSE French tutors to teach one to one online. GCSE French is four skills assessed separately, and the speaking exam is where confident students most often underperform.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for GCSE French continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Preparing the speaking exam, including role play and photo card
- Teaching writing to the mark scheme, where range and accuracy are both credited
- Building listening technique, which is a skill rather than a vocabulary problem
- Working translation in both directions

## What we look for

You can rehearse the speaking exam properly and calm a student who freezes in it.

Alongside that, every GCSE French tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching GCSE French at this level
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach GCSE French specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
