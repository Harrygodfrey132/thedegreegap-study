## About the role

The Degree Gap is recruiting experienced A-Level Geography tutors for online one-to-one teaching.

A-Level Geography combines extended written assessment with an independent investigation, and the NEA is the component for which students most often require structured support.

Students are matched to tutors by one of the co-founders following a consultation with the family. You will receive the student's current attainment, target grade and relevant background before the first session. We recruit for A-Level Geography on a rolling basis.

## Responsibilities

- Teach essay technique to A-Level standard, including evaluation and synthesis
- Support the NEA from question formulation through to analysis and write-up
- Deliver quantitative and qualitative data skills
- Cover both physical and human components

## Requirements

Candidates must be able to supervise independent research appropriately, without producing the work.

- A minimum of two years' teaching or tutoring experience
- Demonstrable experience teaching A-Level Geography at this level
- An enhanced DBS certificate, verified before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable internet connection and a quiet teaching environment

## Terms

The role is self-employed and part-time, with hours set by the tutor. Students are allocated according to your stated availability and subject coverage. Sessions are scheduled and paid through the platform, so tutors do not invoice families directly and are not required to advertise or source their own students. A named contact is available for scheduling and safeguarding matters.

## Selection process

All applicants are interviewed by a co-founder before appointment. The interview covers subject knowledge, teaching approach, and experience of supporting students working below their target grade. We appoint a small proportion of applicants.

## How to apply

Contact us by WhatsApp or email with a summary of your teaching experience and the subjects and levels you cover.
