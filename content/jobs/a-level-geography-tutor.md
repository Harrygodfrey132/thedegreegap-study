## Overview

We are hiring experienced A-Level Geography tutors to teach one to one online. A-Level Geography pairs demanding essays with an independent investigation, and the NEA is where students most often need structured support.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for A-Level Geography continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Teaching essay technique with evaluation and synthesis at A-Level standard
- Supporting the NEA from question design through to analysis and write-up
- Working quantitative and qualitative data skills
- Covering both physical and human topics to the same depth

## What we look for

You can support an independent investigation without doing it for the student.

Alongside that, every A-Level Geography tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching A-Level Geography at this level
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach A-Level Geography specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
