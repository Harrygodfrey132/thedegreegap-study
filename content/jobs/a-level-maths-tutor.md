## Overview

We are hiring experienced A-Level Maths tutors to teach one to one online. Most A-Level Maths students arrive having done well at GCSE and hit the step up in Year 12. Pure sits alongside statistics and mechanics, and it is common for one of the three to quietly fall behind.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for A-Level Maths continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Teaching pure, statistics and mechanics, or being clear at interview about which you do not cover
- Closing the GCSE-to-A-Level gap in algebra, which is behind most Year 12 struggles
- Working through proof and reasoning questions, where students lose marks for jumps in logic
- Preparing students who are also sitting Further Maths, where pacing is different

## What we look for

You can teach the step up itself. A student who was a grade 8 at GCSE and is now getting Es is not lacking ability, and needs a tutor who can diagnose why.

Alongside that, every A-Level Maths tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching A-Level Maths at this level
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach A-Level Maths specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
