## About the role

The Degree Gap is recruiting experienced A-Level Physics tutors for online one-to-one teaching.

A-Level Physics assumes a level of mathematical fluency that many students, particularly those not taking A-Level Maths concurrently, have not yet reached.

Students are matched to tutors by one of the co-founders following a consultation with the family. You will receive the student's current attainment, target grade and relevant background before the first session. We recruit for A-Level Physics on a rolling basis.

## Responsibilities

- Develop the mathematical competence the specification assumes
- Teach problem solving in unfamiliar contexts
- Deliver the required practicals and associated analysis
- Cover the optional topic on the student's specification

## Requirements

Candidates must be able to teach the mathematical content without disengaging the student.

- A minimum of two years' teaching or tutoring experience
- Demonstrable experience teaching A-Level Physics at this level
- An enhanced DBS certificate, verified before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable internet connection and a quiet teaching environment

## Terms

The role is self-employed and part-time, with hours set by the tutor. Students are allocated according to your stated availability and subject coverage. Sessions are scheduled and paid through the platform, so tutors do not invoice families directly and are not required to advertise or source their own students. A named contact is available for scheduling and safeguarding matters.

## Selection process

All applicants are interviewed by a co-founder before appointment. The interview covers subject knowledge, teaching approach, and experience of supporting students working below their target grade. We appoint a small proportion of applicants.

## How to apply

Contact us by WhatsApp or email with a summary of your teaching experience and the subjects and levels you cover.
