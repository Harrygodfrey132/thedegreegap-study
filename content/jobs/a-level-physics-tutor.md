## Overview

We are hiring experienced A-Level Physics tutors to teach one to one online. A-Level Physics assumes a level of mathematical fluency that many students have not reached, particularly those not taking A-Level Maths alongside it.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for A-Level Physics continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Rebuilding the mathematical toolkit the course assumes
- Teaching problem-solving on unfamiliar contexts rather than recognised question types
- Working through the required practicals and the analysis questions built on them
- Covering the optional topic on the student's specification

## What we look for

You can teach the maths without making the student feel they are in the wrong subject.

Alongside that, every A-Level Physics tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching A-Level Physics at this level
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach A-Level Physics specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
