## About the role

The Degree Gap is recruiting experienced GCSE History tutors for online one-to-one teaching.

GCSE History assesses written response and source handling rather than knowledge alone, and specifications differ substantially between boards.

Students are matched to tutors by one of the co-founders following a consultation with the family. You will receive the student's current attainment, target grade and relevant background before the first session. We recruit for GCSE History on a rolling basis.

## Responsibilities

- Teach source analysis and provenance as a repeatable method
- Develop essay technique against the specific mark scheme
- Deliver the depth and breadth studies on the student's specification
- Establish secure recall of chronology and detail

## Requirements

Candidates must be familiar with the board specification the student is entered for.

- A minimum of two years' teaching or tutoring experience
- Demonstrable experience teaching GCSE History at this level
- An enhanced DBS certificate, verified before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable internet connection and a quiet teaching environment

## Terms

The role is self-employed and part-time, with hours set by the tutor. Students are allocated according to your stated availability and subject coverage. Sessions are scheduled and paid through the platform, so tutors do not invoice families directly and are not required to advertise or source their own students. A named contact is available for scheduling and safeguarding matters.

## Selection process

All applicants are interviewed by a co-founder before appointment. The interview covers subject knowledge, teaching approach, and experience of supporting students working below their target grade. We appoint a small proportion of applicants.
