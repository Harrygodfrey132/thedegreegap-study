## Overview

We are hiring experienced GCSE History tutors to teach one to one online. GCSE History is a knowledge subject assessed as a writing subject. Students who know the period well still lose marks on source questions and structured essays.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for GCSE History continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Teaching source analysis and provenance as a repeatable method
- Working the essay questions against the specific mark scheme wording
- Covering the depth and breadth studies on the student's specification
- Building recall of dates and detail that survives exam pressure

## What we look for

You know the specifications differ substantially by board and can teach the one the student is sitting.

Alongside that, every GCSE History tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching GCSE History at this level
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach GCSE History specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
