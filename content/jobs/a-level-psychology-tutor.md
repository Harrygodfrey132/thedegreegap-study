## The role

We are taking on A-Level Psychology tutors, and we are fussy about who we say yes to.

A-Level Psychology students usually know the studies and still lose marks, because a large share of the paper is evaluation rather than recall.

Joe speaks to the family before you ever hear a name, so you start knowing the predicted grade, the mock that came back lower than everyone hoped, and what the school has already tried. You are not opening an email from a stranger and guessing.

## What the teaching looks like

- Teaching evaluation as a structured skill rather than a list of criticisms
- Working research methods and the statistics that go with them, which is the most commonly weak area
- Preparing for extended answers where the marks sit in the argument
- Covering the optional topics on the student's specification

## Who we take on

You can teach evaluation explicitly. Students who describe well and evaluate poorly are the majority of the referrals we get.

Beyond that, two years of teaching or tutoring behind you, real experience with A-Level Psychology at this level rather than the subject in general, and an enhanced DBS. We check the DBS before you meet a student, so it is worth having it to hand. You will also need the right to work in the UK, somewhere quiet to teach from and a connection that does not drop halfway through a session.

## How it works week to week

You tell us when you are free and how many students you want. Nothing lands in your diary that you have not agreed to take, you are not advertising yourself anywhere, and you are not chasing families for money. If a student goes quiet or something awkward comes up, you ring us and we deal with it.

## Talk to us

Harry or Joe will interview you before anything else happens. We turn down most people who apply, so expect a real conversation: what you know, how you teach A-Level Psychology, and what you do with a student who has decided they are no good at it.

Message us on WhatsApp or send an email telling us what you have taught. That is the whole application.
