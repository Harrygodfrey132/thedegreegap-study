## Overview

We are hiring experienced A-Level Psychology tutors to teach one to one online. A-Level Psychology students usually know the studies and still lose marks, because a large share of the paper is evaluation rather than recall.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for A-Level Psychology continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Teaching evaluation as a structured skill rather than a list of criticisms
- Working research methods and the statistics that go with them, which is the most commonly weak area
- Preparing for extended answers where the marks sit in the argument
- Covering the optional topics on the student's specification

## What we look for

You can teach evaluation explicitly. Students who describe well and evaluate poorly are the majority of the referrals we get.

Alongside that, every A-Level Psychology tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching A-Level Psychology at this level
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach A-Level Psychology specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
