## Overview

We are hiring experienced A-Level History tutors to teach one to one online. A-Level History asks for an argument sustained across an essay and supported with precise evidence. Students often bring plenty of knowledge and no line.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for A-Level History continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Teaching argument construction and sustained judgement
- Working with historiography where the specification requires it
- Supporting the coursework component, which many students underestimate
- Teaching source evaluation at A-Level depth

## What we look for

You can teach a student to take a position and hold it for two sides of paper.

Alongside that, every A-Level History tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching A-Level History at this level
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach A-Level History specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
