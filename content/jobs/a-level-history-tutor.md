## About the role

The Degree Gap is recruiting experienced A-Level History tutors for online one-to-one teaching.

A-Level History requires sustained argument supported by precise evidence, alongside a coursework component that students commonly underestimate.

Students are matched to tutors by one of the co-founders following a consultation with the family. You will receive the student's current attainment, target grade and relevant background before the first session. We recruit for A-Level History on a rolling basis.

## Responsibilities

- Develop sustained argument and supported judgement
- Deliver historiography where required by the specification
- Supervise and support the coursework component
- Teach source evaluation to A-Level standard

## Requirements

Candidates must be able to teach a student to establish and sustain a position across an extended essay.

- A minimum of two years' teaching or tutoring experience
- Demonstrable experience teaching A-Level History at this level
- An enhanced DBS certificate, verified before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable internet connection and a quiet teaching environment

## Terms

The role is self-employed and part-time, with hours set by the tutor. Students are allocated according to your stated availability and subject coverage. Sessions are scheduled and paid through the platform, so tutors do not invoice families directly and are not required to advertise or source their own students. A named contact is available for scheduling and safeguarding matters.

## Selection process

All applicants are interviewed by a co-founder before appointment. The interview covers subject knowledge, teaching approach, and experience of supporting students working below their target grade. We appoint a small proportion of applicants.
