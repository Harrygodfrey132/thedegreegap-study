## About the role

The Degree Gap is recruiting experienced Reading tutors for online one-to-one teaching.

Reading support is an early intervention role rather than examination tutoring, and typically involves younger children who have lost confidence.

Students are matched to tutors by one of the co-founders following a consultation with the family. You will receive the student's current attainment, target grade and relevant background before the first session. We recruit for Reading on a rolling basis.

## Responsibilities

- Assess whether difficulty lies in decoding, fluency or comprehension
- Deliver phonics and decoding instruction where required
- Develop comprehension and inference alongside fluency
- Rebuild the child's confidence in reading aloud

## Requirements

Candidates must have prior experience teaching early reading.

- A minimum of two years' teaching or tutoring experience
- Demonstrable experience teaching Reading
- An enhanced DBS certificate, verified before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable internet connection and a quiet teaching environment

## Terms

The role is self-employed and part-time, with hours set by the tutor. Students are allocated according to your stated availability and subject coverage. Sessions are scheduled and paid through the platform, so tutors do not invoice families directly and are not required to advertise or source their own students. A named contact is available for scheduling and safeguarding matters.

## Selection process

All applicants are interviewed by a co-founder before appointment. The interview covers subject knowledge, teaching approach, and experience of supporting students working below their target grade. We appoint a small proportion of applicants.
