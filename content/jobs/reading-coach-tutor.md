## Overview

We are hiring experienced Reading tutors to teach one to one online. Reading support is early intervention work. It is not exam tutoring and needs a different skill set, usually with younger children who have lost confidence.

Students are matched to you by Harry or Joe after a consultation with the family, so you begin with a clear brief rather than a name and an email address. This is an ongoing role and we recruit for Reading continuously.

## What the teaching involves

- Assessing where fluency is breaking down, whether decoding, pace or comprehension
- Teaching phonics and decoding where that is the gap
- Building comprehension and inference alongside fluency
- Rebuilding a child's willingness to read aloud

## What we look for

You have taught early reading before and can work with a child who has decided they are bad at it.

Alongside that, every Reading tutor needs:

- Two or more years of teaching or tutoring experience
- Direct experience teaching Reading
- An enhanced DBS check, which we verify before any student is assigned
- The right to work in the UK
- A reliable connection and a quiet space to teach from

## How the work reaches you

You set your own availability and take on as many or as few students as suits you. There is no advertising, cold outreach or chasing families for work, and sessions are booked and paid through the platform rather than invoiced directly. You have a named contact here when something needs sorting out.

## How we hire

Every tutor is interviewed by one of the co-founders before joining. We are selective and turn down most applicants. The interview covers your subject knowledge, how you teach Reading specifically, and how you handle a student who is behind and has lost confidence.
