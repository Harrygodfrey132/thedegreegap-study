## What the UCAT looks like now

The UCAT changed in 2025, and a lot of older advice online hasn't caught up. Abstract Reasoning was removed, so there are now three scored cognitive subtests plus the Situational Judgement Test (SJT):

| Subtest | Questions | Time | Scored |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verbal Reasoning | 44 | 22 minutes | 300 to 900 |
| Decision Making | 35 | 37 minutes | 300 to 900 |
| Quantitative Reasoning | 36 | 26 minutes | 300 to 900 |
| Situational Judgement | 69 | 26 minutes | Band 1 (best) to Band 4 |

The cognitive total runs from 900 to 2700, not the old 3600. The whole test takes just under two hours on a computer at a Pearson VUE centre. Any book, course or question bank from before 2025 is preparing your child for a different exam, so check the date on anything you buy.

## What a good UCAT score looks like in 2026

In 2026, 42,359 candidates sat the UCAT, up from 41,354 the year before. The final mean total was 1918, according to the UCAT Consortium's test statistics. Here's where each decile started:

| Decile | Total score (2026) |
|---|---|
| 9th (top 10%) | 2250 |
| 8th | 2140 |
| 7th | 2050 |
| 6th | 1980 |
| 5th (median) | 1920 |
| 4th | 1850 |
| 3rd | 1780 |

For the SJT, 15% of candidates reached Band 1, 40% Band 2, 34% Band 3 and 11% Band 4. Verbal Reasoning was again the lowest-scoring subtest, with a mean of 602, against 640 for Decision Making and 675 for Quantitative Reasoning.

One detail is worth knowing. The interim mean, based on students who tested by mid-September, was 1970. The final mean, once late testers were included, fell to 1918. The same pattern happened in 2025. Students who book later tend to be less prepared, so aim for a date in July or early August.

## How universities use the score

This is the part most families miss. A score of 2100 is strong everywhere, but how much it helps depends on how each medical school uses it. Broadly, schools fall into three groups:

- **Ranking schools** shortlist mainly on UCAT. Bristol, for example, has used UCAT alone to shortlist for interview.
- **Composite schools** combine UCAT with GCSEs and other factors. Birmingham has weighted academic results at 45%, UCAT at 40% and contextual factors at 15%.
- **Threshold schools** set a minimum, then judge applications on other criteria. Sunderland, for example, has looked for scores in the top eight deciles.

Because your child gets their score on the day, they can choose their four medical schools afterwards. A student with 2250 should apply where UCAT carries the most weight. A student with 1850 and excellent GCSEs should look hard at composite schools. That decision alone can matter more than another 50 points. Admissions criteria change each year, so always check each medical school's current page.

## Key dates for UCAT 2027 (2028 entry)

The UCAT Consortium usually publishes the next year's dates in January. For guidance, this is how the 2026 cycle ran:

| Step | 2026 date |
|---|---|
| Account registration, bursary and access arrangements open | 20 May |
| Booking opens | 23 June |
| Testing starts | 13 July |
| Access arrangements deadline | 10 September |
| Booking deadline | 16 September |
| Last test day and bursary deadline | 24 September |
| UCAS deadline for Medicine | 15 October |
| Results sent to universities | Early November |

Expect a similar pattern in 2027. The test costs £70 in the UK and £115 abroad, and the UCAT bursary waives the fee completely for eligible UK candidates. Your child can sit the UCAT only once per cycle, so the date they choose matters.

## Does your child actually need a UCAT tutor?

Not always, and a good tutor will tell you that. The UCAT Consortium provides free official practice tests, and many students score well with those plus a decent question bank and disciplined timed practice.

Tutoring tends to earn its cost in five situations:

- **Stuck in one subtest.** Your child's mock scores have plateaued in one area, most often Verbal Reasoning or Decision Making, after several weeks of practice.
- **Timing, not knowledge.** They can answer the questions but run out of time. This is the most common problem, and the most fixable with coaching.
- **SJT Band 3 or 4 in mocks.** Some medical schools screen out Band 4, and the SJT rewards a specific way of thinking about professional behaviour.
- **Little time.** Their A-Level load leaves only a few weeks, so the preparation needs to be targeted.
- **No school support.** Their school doesn't run UCAT preparation or has few medicine applicants.

If none of these applies, start with the free official material and a question bank, and review after a month of practice.

## What UCAT tutoring costs in the UK

Prices vary widely. These are typical published rates from UK providers in 2026:

| Provider type | Typical price |
|---|---|
| Online self-study courses | Often under £10 an hour of content |
| Specialist medicine tutoring companies (packages) | About £30 to £50 an hour |
| General tutoring agencies | From about £60 an hour |
| Premium specialist packages | Several hundred pounds to over £2,000 for 10 to 30 hours |

More hours aren't automatically better. A focused block of six to ten sessions, aimed at one or two weak subtests and timed practice, usually does more than a 30-hour package spread thinly across everything.

## A 12-week UCAT plan for spring and summer

This plan assumes a test date in late July or early August.

**Weeks 1 to 2: diagnose.** Sit one full official practice test under timed conditions. Record the score for each subtest and the SJT band. That tells you where the work is.

**Weeks 3 to 8: build skills one subtest at a time.** Spend most time on the weakest subtest. For Verbal Reasoning, that means practising fast reading for the claim being tested. For Decision Making, it means logic puzzles, Venn diagrams and probability. For Quantitative Reasoning, it means speed with the on-screen calculator.

**Weeks 9 to 12: full mocks, every week.** Do at least one full timed mock a week and review every wrong answer. Practise moving on from a hard question quickly, because every question carries the same mark.

**Throughout:** do 20 minutes of SJT practice a few times a week, using the GMC's guidance on good medical practice as the reference point.

## How to choose a UCAT tutor

Ask these questions before you book:

- **Did they sit the current format?** A tutor who sat the UCAT before 2025 prepared for Abstract Reasoning. Look for a recent, top-decile score.
- **Do they start with a diagnostic?** Good tutors look at your child's subtest scores before teaching anything.
- **Do they know how universities use the score?** They should help your child choose where to apply once the score is in.
- **Will they say when to stop?** A good tutor will tell you when your child doesn't need more sessions.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is UCAT tutoring worth it?

It can be, if your child is stuck in one subtest, keeps running out of time, or scores Band 3 or 4 in SJT mocks. Many students do well with the free official practice tests and a question bank, so start there and decide after a month.

### How much does a UCAT tutor cost?

Most UK providers charge about £30 to £60 an hour, often sold in packages. Premium packages of 10 to 30 hours can cost from several hundred pounds to over £2,000.

### What is a good UCAT score in 2026?

The 2026 median was 1920 out of 2700. A score of 2050 put a candidate in the top 30%, and 2250 in the top 10%. What counts as good depends on how the medical schools your child applies to use the score.

### When is the UCAT in 2027?

Official dates are usually published in January. In 2026, booking opened on 23 June and testing ran from 13 July to 24 September, so expect a similar window in summer 2027.

### When should my child start preparing for the UCAT?

Most students need 8 to 12 weeks of regular practice. For a test in late July, that means starting in April or May, alongside A-Levels.

### Can you resit the UCAT?

Not in the same year. Candidates can sit the UCAT once per testing cycle, and the score is only valid for that year's application.
