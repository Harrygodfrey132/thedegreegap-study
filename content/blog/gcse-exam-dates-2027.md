The summer 2027 GCSE exams run from **Monday 10 May to Friday 18 June 2027**, and results come out on **Thursday 19 August 2027**. All the main exam boards (AQA, Pearson Edexcel, OCR and WJEC Eduqas) work to the same JCQ timetable, so most subjects fall on the same day whichever board your child's school uses.

This guide gives every core subject date, the deadlines that matter before May, and what the shape of the timetable means for revision. If you'd like help turning it into a plan for your child, [book a free 30-minute call](/book-a-call/).

## Key GCSE dates for 2027 at a glance

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| Wednesday 13 January 2027 | November 2026 resit results |
| Sunday 31 January 2027 | Deadline for modified papers (large print or Braille) |
| Sunday 21 February 2027 | Exam entry deadline for schools |
| Sunday 21 March 2027 | Deadline for other access arrangements, such as extra time or a reader |
| Monday 10 May 2027 | First GCSE exam |
| 31 May to 4 June 2027 | Half term, with no exams |
| Friday 18 June 2027 | Last GCSE exam |
| Wednesday 23 June 2027 | Contingency day |
| Thursday 19 August 2027 | GCSE results day |

Schools handle exam entries and access arrangements, but it's worth knowing the deadlines. If your child might need extra time, speak to the school's SENCo now, because the evidence takes time to gather.

## Core subject exam dates 2027

These dates are the same for AQA, Edexcel and OCR unless noted. Combined Science students sit their biology, chemistry and physics papers on the same days as the separate sciences.

| Subject | Paper 1 | Paper 2 | Paper 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maths | Friday 14 May | Thursday 27 May | Monday 14 June |
| English Literature | Wednesday 12 May | Wednesday 19 May | |
| English Language | Monday 24 May | Tuesday 8 June | |
| Biology | Tuesday 11 May | Thursday 10 June | |
| Chemistry | Monday 17 May | Tuesday 15 June | |
| Physics | Tuesday 25 May | Friday 18 June | |

Maths Paper 1 is the non-calculator paper for AQA and Edexcel. OCR puts its non-calculator paper second, on 27 May.

| Subject | Main exam dates |

| Geography | Tuesday 18 May, Wednesday 26 May and Thursday 10 June |

These are Pearson Edexcel's dates; AQA and OCR papers in these subjects mostly fall on the same days, but check your child's own timetable. Languages also have speaking assessments, which schools schedule separately, so ask the school for those dates. Always check your child's own timetable for morning or afternoon sessions.

## The shape of the 2027 exam season

The dates matter less than the pattern they make. Laid out week by week, the core and popular option papers look like this:

| Week | Dates | Main papers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 to 14 May | Computer Science 1, Biology 1, English Literature 1, Business 1, RS 1, Maths 1 |
| 2 | 17 to 21 May | Chemistry 1, Geography 1, English Literature 2, Computer Science 2, History 1, Business 2, RS 2 |
| 3 | 24 to 28 May | English Language 1, Physics 1, Geography 2, Maths 2, History 2 |
| Half term | 31 May to 4 June | No exams |
| 4 | 7 to 11 June | English Language 2, History 3, Biology 2, Geography 3 |
| 5 | 14 to 18 June | Maths 3, Chemistry 2, Physics 2 |

Three things stand out for revision.

**The first three weeks are the heaviest.** Every core subject has its first paper between 10 and 25 May. Your child needs to have revised all of it before the season starts, not "get to physics later".

**Half term is a gift.** A full week with no exams sits right before English Language Paper 2 and the last maths and science papers. Use it for timed past papers, not a break from revision.

**Science finishes last.** Chemistry Paper 2 is on 15 June and Physics Paper 2 on 18 June. That means science paper 2 content can keep being revised through early June, but it also means a long season for triple science students, who sit six science papers across six weeks.

## Formula and equation sheets in 2027

Yes, they're staying. Ofqual has confirmed that GCSE Maths, Physics and Combined Science students will get formula and equation sheets in their 2027 exams. Your child still needs to know how to use every formula, but they don't have to memorise them.

## What if your child is ill on exam day?

Tell the school straight away. Students who are ill or affected by something serious can apply for special consideration through the school, which may adjust the mark slightly. The contingency day on 23 June is only used if a national or major local disruption stops an exam going ahead. It isn't a resit day for individual students, but every student must stay available until it has passed, so don't book a holiday before 24 June.

## A revision countdown from now

From mid-November there are about 25 weeks until the first exam. A realistic plan for most Year 11s:

- **November to December:** mocks, then use the results to find the two or three weakest subjects. Our [GCSE mocks guide](/gcse-mocks/) explains how.
- **January to February half term:** fill the biggest gaps, especially in maths and English.
- **March to Easter:** move from learning content to exam questions and past papers.
- **Easter to 10 May:** full timed papers, with the first-week subjects first.
- **During the exams:** use gaps between papers and half term to prepare for the next paper, not the last one.

## Frequently asked questions

### When do GCSEs start in 2027?

The first GCSE exams are on Monday 10 May 2027. All the main exam boards follow the same JCQ timetable.

### When do GCSEs finish in 2027?

The last GCSE exam is on Friday 18 June 2027. Students must stay available until the contingency day on Wednesday 23 June.

### When is GCSE results day 2027?

Thursday 19 August 2027. A-Level results come out the week before.

### What is the GCSE contingency day?

It's a reserve date, Wednesday 23 June 2027, used only if a national or major local disruption stops an exam going ahead. It isn't for individual students who miss an exam.

### Will there be formula sheets in the 2027 GCSEs?

Yes. Students taking GCSE Maths, Physics and Combined Science in 2027 will get formula and equation sheets in the exam, and Ofqual has confirmed they will continue after 2027 for the current specifications.

### Are there any exams during May half term?

No. There are no GCSE exams between 31 May and 4 June 2027, which makes that week valuable for final revision.
