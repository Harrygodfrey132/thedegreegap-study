The UCAS deadline for most 2027 courses is **6pm (UK time) on Wednesday 13 January 2027**. If your child's personal statement still isn't finished, that sounds like plenty of time. It usually isn't, because the real deadline is the one your child's school sets, and Christmas takes two weeks out of the middle.

This guide sets out the dates that matter, explains what happens if you miss them, and gives a week-by-week plan to get the statement finished well before the school breaks up. If you'd like an expert to read the draft, [book a free 30-minute call](/book-a-call/).

## The UCAS dates that matter for 2027 entry

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| 15 October 2026 | Deadline for Oxford, Cambridge, Medicine, Dentistry and Veterinary courses (passed) |
| Your school's date | Internal deadline, usually in November or December, so the school can write the reference |
| 13 January 2027, 6pm | Equal consideration deadline for most other courses |
| 25 February 2027 | UCAS Extra opens for students with five choices and no offers |
| 30 June 2027, 6pm | Last date to apply before applications go straight into Clearing |
| 2 July 2027 | Clearing opens |
| Mid-August 2027 | A-Level results day |

## What "equal consideration" actually means

Universities must consider every application that arrives by 6pm on 13 January on the same basis. After that, they don't have to. UCAS will still pass a late application on until 30 June, but popular courses may already be full, and some universities rarely look at late applications at all.

The flip side is also worth knowing. A statement sent in December isn't judged more favourably than one sent on 12 January. Your child may hear back sooner, but there's no prize for being first. The goal is a finished, strong statement, sent comfortably before the deadline.

## Why your real deadline is earlier

Three things pull the deadline forward.

- **The reference.** A teacher has to write it, and the school adds [predicted grades](/blog/predicted-grades-how-they-work/) before it sends the application. Schools set internal deadlines to make that possible.
- **Christmas.** Most schools break up in the week before Christmas and return in early January. Teachers who check statements and references aren't available over the break.
- **The last-minute queue.** Every year, some students submit on 13 January and hit problems with their account, a referee or a missing detail. Leaving a few days spare removes that risk.

In practice, aim to have the statement finished by the time the school breaks up.

## The personal statement in 2027: three questions

Since 2026 entry, the personal statement is no longer one long essay. Your child answers three questions:

1. Why do you want to study this course or subject?
2. How have your qualifications and studies helped you to prepare for this course or subject?
3. What else have you done to prepare outside of education, and why are these experiences useful?

The three answers share a 4,000-character limit, including spaces, and each must be at least 350 characters. Universities see the answers separately, but admissions staff read them together as one statement, and the same statement goes to all five choices. Our guide to [the new UCAS personal statement](/blog/ucas-2027-personal-statement-and-autumn-deadlines/) goes through each question in detail.

## A five-week plan to finish before the school breaks up

This plan starts in mid-November and finishes on Friday 18 December, leaving the Christmas break as a buffer.

**Week 1 (16 to 22 November): settle the choices and gather evidence.** Confirm the five courses, since one statement has to work for all of them. Then list everything your child could write about: books, lessons, projects, work experience, part-time jobs and responsibilities at home.

**Week 2 (23 to 29 November): write a rough first draft of all three answers.** Don't worry about length. The aim is to get every idea down, with one specific example per point.

**Week 3 (30 November to 6 December): get one expert read.** Ask one person who knows the subject to read it, such as a subject teacher or a mentor. Several readers with different opinions usually make a statement worse, not better.

**Week 4 (7 to 13 December): cut to fit.** Bring it under 4,000 characters, check each answer is over 350, and remove anything that repeats. Question 2 usually deserves the most space for academic courses.

**Week 5 (14 to 18 December): final read and hand-in.** Read it aloud, check every fact, and give it to the school in time for the reference.

## The mistakes that cost the most

These are the patterns we see most often in statements for 2027 entry:

- **A generic opening to Question 1.** Saying you're passionate about a subject tells the reader nothing. One specific moment, book or problem that made your child curious does far more.
- **Listing grades in Question 2.** The grades are already on the application. Admissions tutors want to know what your child learned and how it connects to the course.
- **A list of activities in Question 3.** Five activities with no reflection are weaker than two with a clear line on what each taught them.
- **Using the same example twice.** With only 4,000 characters, every example should do one job, in one answer.
- **Naming one university.** The statement goes to all five choices, so it should never mention a single university.
- **Copied or AI-written text.** UCAS checks statements for similarity, and admissions tutors read thousands. A statement that sounds like everyone else's doesn't stand out.

## Missed the deadline?

It isn't the end of the road. Your child can still apply until 6pm on 30 June 2027, as long as the course is still accepting applications, so check each course page first. Students who use all five choices and get no offers can add more through UCAS Extra from 25 February. After 30 June, applications go into Clearing, which opens on 2 July.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the UCAS deadline for 2027 entry?

For most courses it's 6pm UK time on Wednesday 13 January 2027. Oxford, Cambridge, Medicine, Dentistry and Veterinary courses had an earlier deadline of 15 October 2026.

### What time is the UCAS deadline?

6pm UK time. An application that arrives at 6.01pm isn't covered by equal consideration, so don't leave it to the final hour.

### Can you apply after the UCAS deadline?

Yes. UCAS accepts applications until 6pm on 30 June 2027, but universities don't have to consider them and popular courses may be full. After 30 June, applications go straight into Clearing.

### Is my school's deadline the same as the UCAS deadline?

No. Schools set an earlier internal deadline, often in November or December, so teachers have time to write the reference. Check the school's date, because that's the one your child actually has to meet.

### Does applying early to UCAS help?

Not for courses with the January deadline. Universities must consider every application received by 13 January equally. Applying earlier may mean a faster decision, but it doesn't improve your child's chances.

### How long is the UCAS personal statement?

Up to 4,000 characters, including spaces, across three questions, with at least 350 characters for each answer. That's roughly 550 to 650 words in total.
