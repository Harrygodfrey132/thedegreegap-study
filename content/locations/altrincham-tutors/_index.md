faq_1_q: "Do you help with the Trafford test as well?"
faq_1_a: "Yes, though GCSE and A-Level are most of what we do. Trafford's the only Greater Manchester borough still running an entrance test. For the Altrincham and Sale grammars it's a shared test: two papers of around an hour each, sat once in the September of Year 6, covering verbal reasoning, non-verbal reasoning and maths. Loreto Grammar and St Ambrose set their own exams. We match children in Year 5 and Year 6 with tutors who know those formats, and within a couple of lessons the tutor can usually tell whether your child needs reasoning practice or some Year 4 maths that never quite landed."
faq_2_q: "My child wants to apply to a different sixth form at 16. Can you help?"
faq_2_a: "Yes, and it comes up a lot in Altrincham. The grammar sixth forms take students from other schools into Year 12, and each one sets its own bar. Altrincham Grammar School for Boys looks for a minimum average across your child's best eight GCSEs, plus a grade 6 in each subject carried on. Altrincham Grammar School for Girls asks for grade 7s in the subjects taken at A-Level, and Loreto Grammar has recently had twenty places for children coming in from other schools. So the job's a narrow one. We'll read this year's requirements with you, work out which grades are short, and point the lessons there."
