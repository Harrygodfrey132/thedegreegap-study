  - q: "Does my child need a tutor for Maths, or will more revision do?"
    a: "Look at the working on a recent paper, not the grade on the front. If it's tidy but the marks run out towards the back, that's technique. It's the quickest thing a tutor can fix, because the Maths is there and only the layout's missing. Crossed-out or blank working on the longer questions is different: that's a gap in the method. More revision on its own rarely helps there, because your child ends up practising what they can already do. And if the mocks are near the top on all three papers, a few weeks on the two question types still slipping may be plenty."
  - q: "Should my child sit higher or foundation tier Maths in Altrincham?"
    a: "It depends on how your child's doing in Year 10, not on what the school usually does. The tier is which set of papers they sit. Higher goes up to a 9, but there's very little safety net if your child falls below a 4. Foundation stops at a 5, and far more of its marks are within reach. Plenty of Altrincham children sit higher, and for a borderline 5 or 6 in Year 10 that's usually right, with focused work on the topics that decide it. On a 3 or 4, higher can cost the pass, and a steady 4 or 5 opens most of Trafford College's courses and the apprenticeship routes. The first lesson helps show which, and it's a decision to make with your child's school."
