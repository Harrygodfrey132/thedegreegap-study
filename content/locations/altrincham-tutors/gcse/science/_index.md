  - q: "Why is my child struggling with GCSE Science when they revise so much?"
    a: "Usually because the revision practises remembering, and the paper tests method. Science revision drifts towards what feels like learning: definitions, flashcards. Those are the questions where the marks go, and re-reading won't fix them. The other common cause is the six-mark answers, where your child knows the content and writes it in the wrong order. A first lesson with a marked paper tells us which it is, and each needs a different plan."
