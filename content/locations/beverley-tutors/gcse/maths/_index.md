  - q: "Should my child fear the non-calculator paper more than the other two?"
    a: "Not on its own. AQA, Edexcel and OCR each set three equally weighted papers, one without a calculator and two with, so two of every three marks come from calculator papers. Eduqas sets two papers instead. Fractions and algebra get exposed early on the non-calculator paper, but plenty of marks also slip in the long problems near the end of the final calculator paper. A tutor can usually tell from one marked mock which of the two is hurting your child more."
  - q: "Why does my child find Maths so hard when they work so hard at it?"
    a: "Usually because one early method never quite settled, not because of effort. One-to-one, the tutor can. That's often where the first lesson goes: finding the step that broke, then rebuilding forward from it."
