  - q: "My child reads all the time. So why is GCSE English going badly?"
    a: "Because reading well and writing about reading against the clock are different skills. A keen reader often sees what a writer is doing but hasn't learned to say how, with the right term and a quote to back it up. Others have never been shown how to plan an essay, so every answer starts strong and drifts. The tutor's first job is working out which it is, usually from one marked essay."
