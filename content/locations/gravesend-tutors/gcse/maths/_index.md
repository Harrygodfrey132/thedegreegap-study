  - q: "Should my child be entered for Foundation or Higher Maths?"
    a: "It depends where they're working now and what they need next. Foundation tops out at a grade 5, so it rules out anyone who needs a 6 or more for sixth form. Higher covers grades 4 to 9, with an allowed 3 just below, so a child at a shaky 4 can come away with less than they'd get on Foundation. A tutor's diagnostic in Year 10 gives you a clearer picture before the school decides, and if Higher is the aim, work starts on the algebra Foundation leaves out."
  - q: "How do I find out which Maths questions are costing my child marks?"
    a: "Look at a marked mock question by question rather than at the grade. Marks lost early in the paper usually point to number or algebra slips. Marks lost on the long questions near the end, especially the ones that say 'show that', point to setting out rather than knowledge. If the working is blank, it's often confidence with a method. The tutor goes through a paper like this in the first lesson, and you'll hear what they found."
