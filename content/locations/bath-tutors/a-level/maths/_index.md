  - q: "What is the difference between Edexcel, AQA and OCR A-Level Maths, and does it matter?"

Edexcel puts Statistics and Mechanics together in Paper 3 with two Pure papers before it, AQA spreads Pure across all three papers with Mechanics on one and Statistics on another, and OCR, including the MEI route, does its own split with a different large data set. Question wording and the amount of structure given in a multi-step problem also differ. A tutor who knows the wrong board is practising the wrong paper. We confirm the exam board on the consultation call and match to it."
