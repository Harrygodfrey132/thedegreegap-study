  - q: "Should my child take foundation or higher tier GCSE Maths?"
    a: "It depends on where the marks are now, not where the family hopes they'll finish. Foundation stops at a 5 and makes far more of the available marks reachable. A Bath student at a borderline 5 or 6 in the Year 10 exams is usually right to stay on higher with focused work on the reasoning questions. At a 3 or 4, foundation lowers the risk. A Year 10 diagnostic turns that into a decision rather than a guess."
