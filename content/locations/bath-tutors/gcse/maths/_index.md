  - q: "How much homework will my child get between Maths lessons?"
    a: "About an hour a week, and no more than ninety minutes. It should be past-paper questions on that week's topic, not a chapter of the textbook. If your child's working on proof and circle theorems, that's usually two or three sets of questions. The reasons go in full sentences, so the tutor can mark the words as well as the numbers. Past ninety minutes it stops helping, and a tired child just practises the mistakes. The tutor marks it before the next lesson, and that's where the pattern shows."
  - q: "Should my child be sitting higher or foundation Maths?"
    a: "Go by where your child's marks are now, not where you'd love them to finish. Higher tier is the harder set of papers. It goes up to a 9, but its lowest grade is a 4, or a 3 if they only just miss. So a bad day can leave very little. Foundation stops at a 5, with far more of the marks in reach. On a borderline 5 or 6 in Year 10, staying on higher is usually right, with some work on the reasoning questions. On a 3 or 4, foundation is safer. A proper check in Year 10 turns it into a decision, not a guess."
