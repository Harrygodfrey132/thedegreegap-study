faq_2_q: "My child didn't pass the 11+. Will that hold them back at GCSE?"
faq_2_a: "No. The 11+ is a test taken at ten, and it decided a school place, nothing more. GCSEs are the same exams at every school, marked the same way, and plenty of students at the upper schools go on to excellent grades and strong sixth forms. What we do see is children who've quietly decided they're 'not the clever one'. A tutor on their side, one-to-one, is often the thing that starts to change that."
