faq_2_q: "My child didn't pass the 11+. Will that hold them back at GCSE?"
faq_2_a: "No. The 11+ is a test taken at ten, and it decided a school place, nothing more. A GCSE is marked the same way whichever school your child is at, and plenty of children at the upper schools go on to excellent grades and strong sixth forms. What we do see is children who've quietly decided they're 'not the clever one'. A tutor who's on their side, one-to-one, is often what starts to shift that."
