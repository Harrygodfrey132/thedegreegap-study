faq_1_q: "How does the 32-point total at King Edward VI College work, and which subject should a tutor start with?"
faq_1_a: "The college adds up your child's best six GCSE grades, a grade 6 counting as 6 points and so on, and currently asks for at least 32. Its own example is 7, 6, 5, 5, 5 and 4. Then courses add their own conditions, like a 7 in Maths for A-Level Maths or a 6 in English Literature for English Literature. So a tutor starts with any subject the chosen course names, then the one nearest the next grade. The rules can change, so read the course pages together each autumn."
