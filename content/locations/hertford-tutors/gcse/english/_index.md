  - q: "Does it matter whether my child sits AQA or Edexcel English Language?"
    a: "Yes, for the detail of the practice. Both boards test reading and writing over two papers, but the questions are built differently. AQA pairs a fiction extract with a creative writing task on one paper, then two non-fiction sources with a viewpoint piece on the other. Edexcel uses a 19th-century fiction extract on its first paper and modern non-fiction on its second, with its own writing tasks. Your child's school chooses the board, so we'll check it on the call and pick a tutor who knows it well."

And if the marks are already strong and steady, some timed practice at home may be all they need. We'll say so."
