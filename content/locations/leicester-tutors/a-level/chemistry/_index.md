  - q: "How can I tell which parts of A-Level Chemistry my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Sort a marked paper into three piles: calculation questions, organic mechanism questions and everything else. Almost every student is clearly worse at one of the three. Calculation losses usually mean a maths gap wearing a Chemistry label. Losses on unfamiliar molecules mean mechanisms were learned as pictures. Vague inorganic answers mean recall that was never precise. Fifteen minutes of that beats a term of general revision."
