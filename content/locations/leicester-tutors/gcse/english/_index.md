  - q: "What is the hardest part of GCSE English for Leicester students?"
    a: "Closed-book quotation recall, followed by the comparison question. Recall is a memory problem wearing an analysis problem's clothes, and students who understand a text completely can still freeze without it in front of them. Comparison is hard because it asks for a genuinely different paragraph shape, one that holds two texts at once rather than walking through them in turn. Both respond quickly to targeted work, which is why they are usually where a tutor starts."
  - q: "When is the best time to start English tutoring in Year 11?"
    a: "September to February, before the mocks that inform sixth form and college conversations. An autumn start gives a full term of weekly marked writing before anything is decided, which is the mechanism this subject actually improves by. April and May starts still help and we take them often, but by then the sensible plan is narrower: pick the paper losing the most marks and drill that rather than trying to rebuild everything."
  - q: "Can an English tutor help a child with dyslexia or processing differences?"

Mention it on the consultation call and it shapes who we suggest."
