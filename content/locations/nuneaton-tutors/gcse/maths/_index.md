    a: "Usually because class practice comes in tidy blocks and tests don't. After a lesson on simultaneous equations, every question is simultaneous equations. In a test, your child has to recognise which method a question wants, and often combine two. If a method was only half secure, that's where it shows. A tutor mixes questions up on purpose, so your child practises choosing the method as well as using it."
