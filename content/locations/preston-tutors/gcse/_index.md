faq_1_q: "My child has a conditional offer from a Preston college. When should we start GCSE tutoring?"
faq_1_a: "As soon as you know which grade on the offer is furthest away. Offers often arrive in the winter of Year 11, which still leaves a few months of weekly lessons before May. Earlier is easier, though. A Year 10 start gives the tutor time to fix the older gaps properly instead of patching them."
faq_2_q: "What does a GCSE tutor actually do in the weeks before the mocks?"
faq_2_a: "Mostly past papers, timed and marked against the real mark scheme. The tutor watches where your child runs out of time or loses method marks, and spends the next lesson on that. Between lessons there's a short set of questions on the weak topic. The aim is for nothing in the mock hall to feel new."
faq_3_q: "Can a GCSE tutor help with English Language and English Literature?"
faq_3_a: "Yes, and they need different kinds of help. Language is about reading unseen texts quickly and writing well against the clock. Literature is about knowing the set texts and having quotes ready to use. A tutor who knows your child's board will split the time between the two depending on which is further behind."
