  - q: "My child dreads the non-calculator paper. Is that where the marks go?"
    a: "Often, but check the mocks before assuming. On AQA, Edexcel and OCR there are three papers, one without a calculator and two with, and all three papers count equally. Eduqas sets two. The non-calculator paper rewards quick, accurate number work and algebra, so slips with fractions and negatives cost the most there. On the calculator papers, it's the longer, wordier problems near the end. A tutor works out early on which paper is dropping more marks."
