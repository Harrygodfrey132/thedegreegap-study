  - q: "Combined Science only gives one double grade. How do I know which science is the problem?"
    a: "Look at the papers rather than the final grade. On Combined Science the papers are normally split by science, so a recent set of mocks puts Biology, Chemistry and Physics side by side. On triple, the three grades tell you straight away. If one science is clearly behind, that's where the tutor starts. If they're all a little low, the trouble is more often a type of question than a subject, and the first lesson will show which. Anything you've noticed at home, like Physics homework that takes twice as long, is worth mentioning on the call."

And if your child is already doing well across the board, some timed practice papers may be enough. We'll give you a straight answer on the call, even if it's that they don't need us."
