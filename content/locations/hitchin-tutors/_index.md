faq_1_q: "How does the Hitchin sixth form consortium work?"
faq_1_a: "Hitchin Boys', Hitchin Girls' and The Priory plan their post-16 subjects together, so a student can take a course that is only taught at one or two of the three. Classes mix students from all three schools. The consortium sets a standard entry requirement of five GCSEs at grades 9 to 4, including English and Maths at grade 4 or above, with an average points score of at least 4, and most courses add their own requirement on top. Check this year's course list rather than last year's."
faq_2_q: "Is everything really online, and what do the sessions run on?"
faq_2_a: "Yes, every session. They run on Lessonspace, a shared whiteboard where your child and the tutor both write, draw and annotate on the same page, and each lesson is recorded so it can be watched back before a test. There is nothing to download and no special kit beyond a laptop. Families across Hitchin, Letchworth and the surrounding villages use the same set-up, and after each lesson you get a short note on what was covered."
faq_3_q: "My child is well behind. Is it too late to help?"
faq_3_a: "Almost never, although it changes what the first term looks like. When a gap reaches back a year or two, the tutor traces your child's mistakes backwards until they find the point where the understanding ran out, which is often further back than the school report suggests. The plan builds forward from there, running alongside school rather than repeating it. It takes more weeks than polishing exam technique, and we will tell you which of the two we think is going on."
faq_4_q: "What does it cost, and is there a contract?"
faq_4_a: "From £37 an hour, depending on how experienced the tutor is, with the rate agreed before anything is booked. That price includes the live online lesson, the tutor's preparation, the practice set afterwards and the note home. There is no contract, joining fee or materials charge. You pay one session at a time, so families can pause for a holiday or stop once a grade feels secure, and come back before mocks if they want."
