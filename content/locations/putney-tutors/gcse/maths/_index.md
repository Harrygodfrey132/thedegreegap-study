  - q: "Is International GCSE Maths harder than GCSE Maths?"
    a: "Different rather than harder. Edexcel's International GCSE, the one Putney High teaches, has two two-hour papers with a calculator in both, and brings in some calculus and functions that a GCSE leaves for A-Level. A GCSE has three shorter papers, one without a calculator, so mental and written arithmetic count for more. Both are graded 9 to 1. The tutor needs to know which one your child sits, and that's the first thing we ask on the call."

Putney High expects every student to sit Higher. Elsewhere the school decides, and a tutor's view of your child's recent papers can help that conversation."
