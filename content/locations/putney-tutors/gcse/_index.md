faq_1_q: "Is International GCSE Maths harder than the ordinary GCSE?"
faq_1_a: "Not harder, just different, and both are graded 9 to 1. Edexcel's International GCSE, which Putney High teaches, has two two-hour papers with a calculator allowed in both, and adds a little calculus and work on functions. A GCSE has three shorter papers, the first without a calculator, so arithmetic done by hand counts for more. Neither is the easy option. What matters is that the tutor knows the one your child sits, so it's the first thing we ask about."
