  - q: "How can I tell which Biology topics are costing my child marks?"
    a: "Look at a recent marked paper by question type rather than topic. If the short recall questions are mostly right but the long answers and data questions are half-marked, the gap is applying knowledge, not knowing it. If marks go missing on one topic wherever it appears, that topic needs rebuilding. The tutor does this sorting in the first lesson, and you'll hear what they found."
