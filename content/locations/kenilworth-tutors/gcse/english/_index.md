  - q: "Does my child need a tutor for English, or would more revision do?"
    a: "Look at a recent marked paper. If the reading answers retell the text instead of explaining it, or the writing task trails off before the end, more revision won't fix it, because neither is about knowing content. That's technique, and it's where a tutor helps most. If the marks are fine and your child just wants practice, a few past papers at home might be enough."
  - q: "What actually happens in an English lesson?"
    a: "More writing than most children expect. Often it starts with last week's homework paragraph up on the whiteboard, rewritten together line by line. Then the tutor teaches one skill, like comparing two poems or opening a story so the reader wants to carry on, and your child tries it against the clock. Every lesson is recorded, so the explanation's there to watch again before the next essay."
