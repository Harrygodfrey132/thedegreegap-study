  - q: "Foundation or Higher in Science: how do we decide?"
    a: "It depends where your child is now. Higher papers go up to a grade 9 and expect confident work on the harder topics, while Foundation papers top out at a 5. If your child's hovering around a 4 or 5 in Year 10, a tutor can look at the mock with you and help the decision rest on evidence. The school makes the final call on entries, so it's worth raising it early."
  - q: "AQA, Edexcel, OCR: does the board change much in Science?"
    a: "More than people think. AQA, Edexcel and OCR cover a lot of the same science, but they word questions differently and expect different practicals. AQA calls them required practicals, Edexcel calls them core practicals, and OCR has its own practical activity groups. Kenilworth School and the schools around it choose their own boards, so check your child's exercise books or ask on the call, and we'll match a tutor who knows that board."
