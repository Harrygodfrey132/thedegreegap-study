  - q: "How can I tell which A-Level Maths topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Look at where the working stops rather than at the topic headings. Full working with an arithmetic slip near the end is a fluency issue and the cheapest to fix. Working that starts confidently and is abandoned three lines in usually means the wrong method was chosen, which is the most common Year 13 problem and the least often diagnosed. A blank space means the question was not recognised at all. Those three need different plans, and a single marked mock paper separates them quickly."
