    a: "Three blocks in the second year account for most of the lost marks. Transition metals is first, because it reads as content and is examined as reasoning about colour, shape and ligand substitution. Electrochemistry is second, where electrode potentials have to be combined and a direction predicted rather than a definition recalled. Spectroscopy is third and the least forgiving, because a mass spectrum and an NMR trace have to be turned into a structure with no partial credit for knowing the theory."
