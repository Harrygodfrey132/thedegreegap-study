faqs:
  - q: "What trips Liverpool teenagers up most in GCSE Science?"
    a: "The longer written answers, by quite a way. The six-mark questions ask your child to explain something in a logical order. Most children lose marks not because they don't know the science, but because they write down everything they can remember and hope the marks are in there somewhere. Next come the required practicals, which lots of children do once in a lab and never look at again. Then the maths inside Physics. In our experience, those three explain most of the gap between a grade 4 and a grade 6."
  - q: "Do we need a separate tutor for Biology, Chemistry and Physics?"
    a: "Not always. It depends where the marks are going. If your child's fairly steady across Combined Science, one good tutor for all three keeps life simple. But if one subject is clearly the weak spot, we'll suggest a tutor who teaches that subject and knows its practicals and long answers inside out. That's better than spreading one person thin. Plenty of Liverpool families start with one subject and add another later."

The last quarter of an hour is exam questions on that topic, marked against the mark scheme. That way your child sees exactly where each mark comes from, and why."
