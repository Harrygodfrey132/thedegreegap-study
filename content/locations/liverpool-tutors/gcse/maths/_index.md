  - q: "Should my child focus on the calculator or the non-calculator paper?"
    a: "Both, because they carry equal weight, so the real question is which one is losing more marks. The non-calculator paper rewards arithmetic fluency and clean algebraic manipulation. The calculator papers reward accuracy across multi-step work and the patience to read a long question properly. Most students leak marks in different places on each. A tutor's first diagnostic is often just marking one of each and seeing which pattern looks worse."
  - q: "Should my child sit foundation or higher tier GCSE Maths?"
    a: "Higher tier runs from grade 4 up to 9, foundation caps at grade 5. If your child is sitting around a borderline 5 in Year 10 mocks, higher tier is usually the right call but it needs targeted preparation, because the back third of the paper is where those students lose ground. At a 3 or a low 4, foundation lowers the risk of walking out with very little. It's a decision worth making with a marked paper in front of you rather than on a feeling."
