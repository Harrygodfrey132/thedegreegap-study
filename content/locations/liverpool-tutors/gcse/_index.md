faq_2_a: "Yes, and that's often where the progress begins. Plenty of teenagers have quietly decided they're 'no good at Maths' or 'rubbish at English'. The tutor sets work your child can actually finish, then builds on those small wins until that belief starts to shift. And you'll usually hear it in how they talk about the subject before you see it in a grade."
