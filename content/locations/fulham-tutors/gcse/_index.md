faq_1_q: "My daughter is at Lady Margaret and wants to stay on for the sixth form. When does she apply?"
faq_1_a: "Earlier than many parents expect. For 2027 entry, Lady Margaret's own Year 11s applied by early November, and girls from other schools have until late January, so the application goes in long before any results. Enrolment then happens on GCSE results day, once the grades are in. Each A-Level has its own requirement in the sixth form prospectus, so check the ones for her subjects. A tutor can then put the months that are left into whichever subject is furthest off."
faq_2_q: "Why start GCSE tutoring in Year 10 rather than waiting for the mocks?"
faq_2_a: "Because Year 10 is when gaps are cheapest to fix. A big share of the course is taught that year, and each topic gets built on later, so a shaky one, like algebraic fractions or balancing equations, tends to resurface in every Year 11 mock. In Year 10 a tutor can go back over it calmly, with no exam the following week. It also means your child reaches the autumn of Year 11, when sixth form applications go in, with predicted grades that reflect what they can really do."
