  - q: "How can my child get better at the spectroscopy questions in A-Level Chemistry?"
    a: "By practising them in a fixed order until it's automatic. Start from the molecular formula, then use infrared to spot the main bonds, the mass spectrum for the molecular ion, and NMR for the number of environments and what sits next to what. Write each conclusion down before drawing a structure, then check the structure against every piece of evidence. Past paper questions are the best practice, because they mix spectroscopy with the organic reactions your child already knows."
