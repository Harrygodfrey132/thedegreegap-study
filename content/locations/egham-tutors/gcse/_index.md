With a term or less, they get choosy: past papers for your child's board, the question types losing the most marks, and timing on the day. It's not the ideal plan, and nobody can promise a grade. But a few focused weeks can still move a mark, and for a nervous Year 11, knowing exactly what to practise is a relief in itself."
