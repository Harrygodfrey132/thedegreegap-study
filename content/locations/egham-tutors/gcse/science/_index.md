  - q: "How do I work out which bits of Science are costing my child marks?"
    a: "Ask to see a marked mock and look at where the marks went, not just the total. AQA papers mix short recall questions with longer ones: six-mark answers, practical questions and multi-step calculations. If the short answers are fine and the long ones are thin, it's usually exam technique. If marks are lost all across one science, say Chemistry, there's probably a content gap, often from Year 9. A tutor maps this properly in the first lesson, with your child explaining their answers as they go."
