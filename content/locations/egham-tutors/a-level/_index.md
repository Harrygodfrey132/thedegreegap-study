faq_1_q: "My child got good GCSEs but is struggling a term into A-Levels. Is that normal?"
faq_1_a: "Very common, and rarely a sign they've picked the wrong subjects. A-Level questions ask your child to apply ideas to situations they haven't seen, where GCSE often rewarded remembering them. Revision habits that worked in Year 11 stop working. The first marked assessments of Year 12 tend to show it, and an E at that stage usually says more about technique than ability. A tutor helps your child change how they revise as well as getting to grips with the topic."
