  - q: "How can I tell where my child is losing Economics marks?"
    a: "Ask to see a marked paper and look at where the red pen is. If the short data questions are dropping marks, it's usually not using the figures or misreading a percentage. If the long essays are, it's normally evaluation: a point made, then nothing weighing it up. Most students have one bigger leak than the other. A tutor spots it in the first lesson and puts the early weeks there."
