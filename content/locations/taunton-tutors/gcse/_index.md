faq_2_q: "Can one tutor cover English Language and English Literature together?"
faq_2_a: "Yes, and it's common to need both, because they're marked quite differently. Language rewards reading an unseen extract closely and writing well against the clock. Literature wants an argument about set texts, backed by quotations your child can explain. The grades matter separately at 16, too: Huish asks for a 5 in English Language for A-Level Economics and Biology, and a 5 in English Literature for A-Level English Literature."
