faq_1_q: "My child is moving to a new sixth form in September. Should we start tutoring straight away?"
faq_1_a: "Not necessarily on day one, but don't wait for the Year 12 mocks either. The first half term somewhere new is a lot to take in: new faces, new teachers, and A-Level content that moves faster than anything at GCSE. A sensible moment is when the first marked assessments come back, often around October half term, because by then you can see which subject is wobbling. If your child already knows Maths or Chemistry will be the hard one, starting in September is fine too."

faq_3_q: "Do your tutors know the exam boards Ilford sixth forms use?"
faq_3_a: "Yes, and we check board by board rather than assuming. Sixth forms choose a board for each subject, so your child could sit AQA Biology, OCR Chemistry and Edexcel Maths in the same summer. Those courses share most of their content but word their questions differently and reward slightly different things, above all in the longer answers. Tell us the boards on the call. If you're unsure, your child's subject teacher will know, or it's printed on their past papers."
