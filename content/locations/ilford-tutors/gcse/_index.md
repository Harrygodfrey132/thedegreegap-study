faq_1_a: "Look at the papers, not the group chat. A recent mock or end-of-topic test tells you far more than what other children say they got, because it shows which questions lost the marks. In the first lesson the tutor goes through one with your child and separates careless slips from real gaps. Quite often a child who feels miles behind is a handful of topics away from where they want to be. Sometimes the gap is bigger. Then you'll know that too, along with a plan to close it."
