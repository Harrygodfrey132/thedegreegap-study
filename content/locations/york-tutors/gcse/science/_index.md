    a: "Combined is worth two GCSEs graded from 9-9 down, Triple is worth three separate grades. Triple carries more content and more exam time, so it suits a student comfortable across all three who wants the Sciences to lead somewhere. Combined is not a lesser route and does not close A-Level Sciences at most sixth forms, though entry requirements vary by school and are worth checking on the current page. What matters more is whether the weakest of the three is being carried by the other two."
