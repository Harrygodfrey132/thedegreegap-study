faq_1_q: "My daughter got great GCSEs. Why is A-Level suddenly so hard?"
faq_1_a: "Because it really is a different kind of subject, not because she's stopped trying. GCSE questions mostly tell students which method to use. A-Level questions often don't, so the skill is choosing the method, then explaining it in writing. Students who've always found school straightforward can hit this in their first term and lose confidence fast. A tutor works on questions she hasn't seen before until picking the method becomes a habit, which is usually the part missing."
