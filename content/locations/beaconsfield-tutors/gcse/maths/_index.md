  - q: "How can I tell which Maths topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Look at where on the paper the marks go. Early questions are mostly number and simple algebra, the middle brings ratio, geometry and graphs, and the last third is long multi-step problems. If your child loses marks all the way through, there's usually a gap from earlier years. If the damage is mostly at the end, it's more about exam technique and knowing where to start. A tutor sorts a recent mock this way in the first lesson."

The school makes the final decision, and a tutor's view of the working gives you something solid to bring to that conversation."
