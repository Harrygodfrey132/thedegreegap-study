  - q: "Why is my child struggling with GCSE Maths?"
    a: "Nearly always because of something that happened well before the topic that triggered the phone call. Maths stacks. Fractions hold up algebra, algebra holds up rearranging, and rearranging holds up most of Year 11. A student who never properly got negatives in Year 8 can follow a lesson perfectly and still fall apart on a question needing three of those skills at once. So the tutor works backwards through wrong answers until the understanding stops, and that point usually sits a couple of years behind the class."
