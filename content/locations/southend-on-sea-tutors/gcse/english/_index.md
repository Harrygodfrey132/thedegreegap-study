  - q: "How much GCSE English homework should my child do between sessions?"
    a: "Sixty to ninety minutes between weekly sessions, and the shape matters more than the total. One essay written to the clock without notes teaches more than three hours of annotating. Then a rewrite of the weakest paragraph once the tutor has marked it. Past ninety minutes the returns drop off sharply, and in a subject this tied up with confidence, a student who feels buried stops writing altogether. Short, timed, marked, repeated."
