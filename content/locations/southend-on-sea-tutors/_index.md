faq_3_q: "Can you help a student who is resitting GCSE Maths or English?"
faq_3_a: "Yes, and a resit usually needs a different plan from the first attempt. Something went wrong the first time, and going through the whole specification again rarely finds it. So the tutor starts with the paper your child actually sat, sorts the lost marks into content gaps and exam-day habits, then spends the hours where they will move the most. Timelines around the November window are tight and tutors can work quickly when they need to. If the target looks unrealistic we will say so."
