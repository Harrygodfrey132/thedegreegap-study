  - q: "Higher or Foundation in Science: how do we know which is right?"
    a: "Your child's school makes the call, usually from mock results, but it helps to know what's at stake. Higher papers lead to grades 4 to 9, with a 3 possible for a narrow miss. Foundation tops out at a 5, so if your child hopes to take a science at A-Level in Abingdon, ask the sixth form what grade it wants before the tier is settled.
