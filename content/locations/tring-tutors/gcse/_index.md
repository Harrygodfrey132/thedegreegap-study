faq_2_q: "Tring School sits OCR for GCSE Maths but AQA for English and Science. Does the board matter for a tutor?"
faq_2_a: "It does. The content overlaps a lot, but the papers don't look the same. OCR numbers its Higher Maths papers 4 to 6 and puts the non-calculator one in the middle, while AQA's GCSE starts with it. Question styles and mark schemes differ too, so practising another board's papers can waste time. We confirm each subject's board on the call, or you'll find it printed on the front of a mock paper."
