  - q: "How can I tell which A-Level Maths topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Look at where the working stops rather than at topic headings. Full working with a sign error near the end is accuracy and the cheapest to fix. Working abandoned three lines in usually means the wrong method was chosen. A blank space means the question was not recognised at all. Those three look identical on a report and a single marked mock separates them quickly."
