  - q: "How do you work out which Maths topics my child is actually missing?"
    a: "By reading the working, not the score. The first lesson is a check-up: recent questions get sorted on the shared whiteboard into the ones your child couldn't start and the ones they started and got stuck on. Those two mean opposite things. A blank is usually a topic that didn't go in the first time, perhaps missed or rushed in a busy term. It tends to rebuild quickly once it's named, because there's nothing to unlearn. A stall is about confidence with the method, which needs practice rather than teaching. Most children have some of both, and the balance decides the term."
  - q: "Is Year 10 too early to start Maths tutoring?"
    a: "No. It's often the best time. In Year 10 there's room to find the real gap and rebuild it properly rather than patch it. That takes a few weeks then. That conversation is much easier with evidence of what your child can do now, rather than what a Year 8 assessment said. Starting in Year 11 is fine too. The plan's just tighter."
