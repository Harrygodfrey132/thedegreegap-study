faqs:
  - q: "Which GCSE Maths topics catch Newbury students out the most?"
    a: "The non-calculator paper, more than any single topic. On AQA, which St Bart's uses, Paper 1 has no calculator, and that's where fractions, surds, percentage change by hand and multi-step ratio questions cost the most. Algebra comes next: rearranging formulas, and expanding brackets with a negative inside. Further up the Higher paper, circle theorems and iterative methods decide the top marks. For a child working at a 4 or 5 it's usually the number work underneath, and the tutor finds out which in the first lesson."
  - q: "Is it the calculator papers or Paper 1 my child should practise most?"
    a: "Neither on its own: all three papers count equally, so the non-calculator paper is a third of the grade and the two calculator papers make up the rest. Paper 1 rewards quick, accurate arithmetic and clean algebra. The calculator papers reward problem-solving and checking long calculations, and marks often slip near the end of Paper 3, when your child is tired. The first paper your child does with the tutor shows which one is costing more, and the time goes there first."
