faqs:
  - q: "How do I know if my child needs a Maths tutor or just more revision?"
    a: "Read the script rather than the grade. Clean working with small errors and unfinished questions at the end points to accuracy and pacing, and more timed practice marked honestly may be enough on its own. Working that stops partway, or questions never started, points to method gaps, and that is where a tutor is genuinely faster than anything a student can do alone."
  - q: "Why is my child's Maths predicted grade lower than I expected?"
    a: "Predictions come from mock performance plus teacher judgement, and at A-Level the usual gap is between following a method and selecting one. A student who works fluently when the topic is named may have no idea where to start when it is not. The script shows this clearly: blank questions on covered topics mean selection, not knowledge. Reading it is the only way to know which conversation to have with school."
  - q: "Can my child have Maths tutoring during the school holidays?"
    a: "Yes, and the holidays suit the rebuilding work well, because a weak strand benefits from consecutive days rather than an hour a week spread thin. Everything runs online, so families away from home keep their slot. Plenty use the summer between Year 12 and Year 13, when the new content has not yet started competing for attention."
