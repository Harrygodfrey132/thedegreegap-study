faqs:
  - q: "How can I tell whether it's a tutor my child needs, or just more revision?"
    a: "Look at the marked paper, not the grade. If the working's clean, with small slips and a few unfinished questions at the end, it's probably accuracy and pacing. More timed practice, marked honestly, may be enough on its own. If the working stops partway, or questions were never started, that points to gaps in method. That's where a tutor is much quicker than anything your child can do alone."
  - q: "My child's Maths prediction is lower than we expected. Why?"
    a: "Predicted grades come from mocks plus the teacher's judgement, and at A-Level the usual gap is between following a method and choosing one. Your child may work fluently when the topic's named, and have no idea where to start when it isn't. The marked paper shows this clearly. Blank questions on topics they've covered mean the problem is choosing the method, not knowing it. Reading the script tells you which conversation to have with school."
