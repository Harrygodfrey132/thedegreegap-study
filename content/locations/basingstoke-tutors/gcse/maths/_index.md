  - q: "Should my child take foundation or higher tier GCSE Maths?"
    a: "Higher tier caps at grade 9 and floors at a 4, sometimes a 3. Foundation caps at 5 and floors at 1. A student sitting around a 4 or 5 in Year 10 mocks is usually better served by higher tier with targeted work on the early and middle questions, because that is where grades 4 to 6 actually live and the hardest questions can be left alone. Where a student is well below that, foundation aimed at securing a strong 5 is often the better plan. The decision should follow a diagnostic rather than one mock."
  - q: "Does my child need a tutor or just more revision?"
    a: "Look at the working on a recent paper. Blank space on whole question types is a missing topic, and revision does not fix it because there is nothing there to revise. Clean working that loses marks near the end is exam technique, which is the fastest thing a tutor fixes. Crossed-out attempts on multi-step questions is method confidence, which needs practice with someone watching. The three look identical on a report and need entirely different terms of work, which is why the diagnosis is worth more than the first month of lessons."
