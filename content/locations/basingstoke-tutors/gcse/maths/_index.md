  - q: "Could more revision sort it, or does my child need a tutor?"
    a: "Look at the working on a recent paper, and it'll usually tell you. Blank space on whole types of question means a missing topic, and revision can't fix that, because there's nothing there to revise. Neat working that loses marks near the end is exam technique, which a tutor can sort quickest. Crossed-out attempts on longer questions show shaky confidence with method, and that needs practice with someone watching. On a report, all three look the same. So working out which it is matters more than the first month of lessons."
