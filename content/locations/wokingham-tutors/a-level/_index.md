faq_2_q: "Do Year 12 grades really count towards UCAS predictions?"
faq_2_a: "Usually, yes. Predicted grades tend to be set in the autumn of Year 13, and teachers lean on the Year 12 summer exams and the work leading up to them. So a weak summer can follow your child onto their UCAS form. A tutor works on the subject that's dragging early enough for the improvement to show before predictions are made. It's your child's work that changes, though, not the prediction itself."
