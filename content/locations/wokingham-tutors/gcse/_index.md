faq_2_q: "My child is at The Holt and their friend is at The Emmbrook. Why do their Maths papers look different?"
faq_2_a: "Because schools choose their own exam boards. The Holt has published Edexcel for GCSE Maths, and The Emmbrook uses AQA. Both set three papers, one without a calculator, and a 6 means the same on either. But the wording and the way marks are spread through a question differ. That's why we ask about the board for each subject before suggesting anyone. The board's name is usually on the cover of any past paper your child brings home."
faq_3_q: "How can a GCSE tutor help my child get ready for the Year 11 mocks?"
faq_3_a: "Mostly by practising papers the way they'll be marked. In the weeks before mocks, the tutor works through past questions on the topics your child finds hardest, then marks them against the scheme so your child can see where each mark went. Timing gets practised too, since lots of students know enough and still run out of time. Afterwards, the marked mock papers become the plan for the next few weeks."
