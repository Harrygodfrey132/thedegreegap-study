  - q: "My child reads all the time. Why are they struggling with GCSE English?"
    a: "Because enjoying a book and writing an exam answer about it are different skills. A keen reader often knows exactly what a poem means and writes a paragraph explaining it, which earns little. The marks are for how the writer creates the effect: a word choice, a shift in tone, the shape of a stanza. Once your child sees what the mark scheme rewards, their reading usually becomes an advantage instead of a distraction."
