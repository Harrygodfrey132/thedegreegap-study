  - q: "Should my child sit the higher or foundation tier in GCSE Science?"
    a: "It depends on where they are by the end of Year 10. Higher tier covers grades 4 to 9, with a 3 allowed in some cases, while foundation tops out at a 5. A child sitting around a 5 or 6 in Year 10 tests usually belongs on higher, with some targeted work on the harder questions. If your child is nearer a 3 or 4, foundation can be the less risky route. A tutor's view, from real papers, helps you make that call with the school."
