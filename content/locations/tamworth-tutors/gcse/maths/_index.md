  - q: "Does my child need a calculator paper tutor or a non-calculator one?"
    a: "The same tutor, but the two papers fail for different reasons and a good diagnostic separates them. Marks lost on the non-calculator paper are usually arithmetic fluency and algebraic manipulation, things that need short daily practice rather than teaching. Marks lost on the calculator papers are more often multi-step reasoning, where the student knows every individual step and cannot see the order. Bring both papers to the first session if you have them, because a student who is fine on one and struggling on the other tells us more than a single overall grade does."

The one thing that changes the answer is what the mock says: a mock that is well below the target usually means something structural, and structural takes a term."
