faq_1_q: "My son has his eye on GSK or Airbus. Can a tutor help with that?"
faq_1_a: "Yes, and on the technical routes it's usually his Maths grade that decides whether he can apply. Each employer publishes its own entry requirements, and they change between schemes and years. So check the current listing, not what a friend was told last year. If he's just under the line, that's a short, focused piece of work, not a full programme. The application often includes an essay or a test too, and that's worth preparing for."
faq_2_q: "Our son's predicted a D but needs a B. Is that realistic?"
faq_2_a: "It can be, and a recent paper will tell you more than the prediction does. If he mostly knows the content and the marks are going on method and unfinished questions, two grades over a year is a realistic aim. If big parts of the course never went in, it'll take longer than a term, and the target might need to shift. We'd rather tell you that on the call than take the booking and disappoint you in March."
