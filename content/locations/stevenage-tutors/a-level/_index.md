faq_1_q: "Can a tutor help with a GSK or Airbus degree apprenticeship application?"
faq_1_a: "Yes, and Maths is usually the grade that decides eligibility on the technical routes. The Stevenage employers publish their own entry requirements, those vary by scheme and shift between years, so work from the current listing rather than from what someone was told last year. Where a student sits just under the line, that is a short and specific piece of work rather than a full programme, and the application itself often carries an essay or a test worth preparing for."
faq_2_q: "My son is predicted a D and needs a B. Is that realistic?"
faq_2_a: "It depends entirely on where the marks are going, and that is answerable from a paper rather than from the prediction. If the content is broadly there and the losses are in method, timing and unfinished questions, two bands across a year is a normal outcome. If large parts of the specification were never secure, the honest answer is that it will take longer than one term and may mean a narrower target. We would rather say that on the call than take the booking and disappoint you in March."
