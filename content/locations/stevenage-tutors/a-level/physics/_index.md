angle_body_1: "A mechanics question describes a situation in words: a block on an incline, two masses over a pulley, a projectile launched at an angle. The mathematics required is rarely difficult. What defeats students is trying to hold the situation in their head while manipulating symbols, because a physical arrangement with several forces acting in different directions is genuinely hard to keep straight without a picture. The working then goes wrong not because the algebra is beyond them but because it is being done blind, and a sign error two lines in ruins everything after it."
angle_body_2: "The fix is a habit rather than a technique: draw it, mark every force with an arrow, resolve into components in writing, and only then write an equation. Students resist because drawing feels like time lost when a clock is running, and it is the fastest route through the question. The first session works a mechanics problem with your child drawing before computing, which usually makes the difference obvious.
