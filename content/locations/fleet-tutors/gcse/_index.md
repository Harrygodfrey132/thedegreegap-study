faq_1_q: "Which GCSE grades do colleges near Fleet ask for?"
faq_1_a: "Each college sets its own entry line, and then some courses add a grade of their own. To join The Sixth Form College Farnborough your child needs at least five 4s, and most who take A-Levels there have mainly 5s or higher. A-Level English Language there, for example, asks for at least a 5 in both English GCSEs. Yateley School's sixth form normally looks for five GCSEs at 5 or above for A-Levels. These can change from year to year, so it's worth checking this year's guide."
faq_2_q: "Is it worth starting in Year 10, or can it wait until Year 11?"
faq_2_a: "Year 10 if you can, though Year 11 isn't too late. In Fleet, Year 11 brings the college application on top of the mocks, so evenings get crowded. Starting in Year 10 gives a tutor room to fix something like algebra or essay planning properly, with no exam close by. But if your child's already in Year 11, it's still worth starting. The lessons will just put more of their time into exam questions and timing."
faq_3_q: "Can one tutor cover English Language and English Literature?"
faq_3_a: "Usually, yes, though the two GCSEs test quite different things. Language is about extracts your child hasn't seen before and a piece of their own writing, so method and timing matter most. Literature is the set texts: knowing them well, with quotations that come back under pressure. It's common for one tutor to teach both, and we'll check they know your child's board and books. If one paper is the real worry, the lessons can lean that way."
