  - q: "Should my child worry more about the calculator or non-calculator paper?"
    a: "AQA, Edexcel and OCR all set three papers, one non-calculator and two calculator, and all three count equally. So the calculator papers carry two-thirds of the marks between them. On the non-calculator paper, it's usually the early questions that cost marks: fractions, negatives and long multiplication. On the calculator papers it's more often the longer problems near the end. Eduqas sets two papers instead of three. The first lesson usually shows which paper is costing your child more."
  - q: "Can I tell at home which Maths topics are letting my child down?"
    a: "Often, yes. Sit down with their last mock and a highlighter, and mark every question where marks went. Bunched at the back of the paper? That usually points to timing and nerves on the long problems. Spread all the way through, starting early? Then something older is wobbling, often a Year 8 or 9 topic like fractions or rearranging. Bring that mock to the call if you can. It saves a lot of guessing."
