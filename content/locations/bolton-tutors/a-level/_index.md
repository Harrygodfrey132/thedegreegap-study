faq_1_q: "How does A-Level tutoring help with the predicted grade UCAS sees?"
faq_1_a: "The prediction comes out of Year 12 work and the first Year 13 mock, and it goes out with the application in the autumn. So the useful window opens a year before the exams do. Tutors read the actual scripts rather than the grade on the front, find where the marks go missing, and rebuild that under timed conditions while there is still time for it to show. What a teacher decides stays with the teacher. What they are looking at can change."
