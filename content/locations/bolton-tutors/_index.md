faq_1_q: "How do sixth-form applications work in Bolton, and can a tutor help with them?"
faq_1_a: "Most Year 11s here apply during the autumn, and each provider runs its own process with its own published requirements. Canon Slade and Thornleigh take applications directly, and The Sixth Form Bolton sets a deadline in January. A tutor helps by working to those dates rather than to June, which in practice means treating the autumn mock as the paper that counts. On the first call we go through what your child's actual shortlist asks for this year."
faq_2_q: "What platform do the sessions run on?"
faq_2_a: "Lessonspace. It is a shared whiteboard built for one-to-one teaching, so the tutor and your child write on the same page rather than talk at each other, and every session is recorded to watch again. Most Bolton students are comfortable with it inside the first lesson and nothing needs installing. Because every session is online, the match gets made on the specification your child sits, not on who happens to live nearby."
faq_3_q: "Can you help a student who has fallen a long way behind?"
faq_3_a: "Yes, and it is a fair chunk of what we do. Some students arrive with gaps going back two years, which is more common than parents think and is not a verdict on anybody. The first session works backwards through their own wrong answers until the understanding runs out, and that point is usually earlier than anyone guessed. The plan then rebuilds forward alongside school instead of repeating the current topic louder."
faq_4_q: "Do your tutors cover the exam boards Bolton schools use?"
faq_4_a: "Yes. AQA, Edexcel, OCR and WJEC, along with the international specifications some independent schools teach. We confirm the board and the tier before proposing anyone, because boards word questions differently, examine practicals differently and print different formulae in the booklet. A tutor who already knows your child's specification is useful from the first ten minutes. One who is learning it alongside your child is not."
