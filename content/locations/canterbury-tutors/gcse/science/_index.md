    a: "The questions that ask a student to apply something rather than recall it, and they sit in the same places every year. Required practicals examined as method, with a question about variables or error rather than about the steps. Data questions where a gradient has to be read and interpreted. Six-mark answers that need a chain of reasoning rather than a list. None of these is about ability. They respond to being taught directly, which is why they shift fast once someone actually works on them."
  - q: "Should my child take foundation or higher tier, and does Triple or Combined matter more?"
    a: "Higher tier caps at grade 9 and floors around a 4, while foundation caps at 5. For Science the tier question often settles itself, and the decision that matters more is Triple versus Combined. Combined counts as two GCSEs across all three sciences at less depth. Triple counts as three and is occasionally written into sixth form Science requirements. If your child is in Year 9 and Sciences are a possible A-Level, that conversation is worth having now."
