faq_1_q: "My son's offer needs an A in one subject and he's on a B. What would a tutor actually do?"
faq_1_a: "Start with that one subject. The tutor reads his last few essays or papers, marks them the way the exam board would and finds the kind of question that's costing him. Then it's practice on that question type every week, against the clock. Nobody can promise a grade. But timed past papers, marked properly, are most of the work at this stage. And you'll know what's being worked on each week."
