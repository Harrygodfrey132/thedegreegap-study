faqs:
  - q: "What is the hardest part of GCSE Maths for Bristol students?"
    a: "The back third of the higher paper, almost every time. The topics that decide a grade 6 from a grade 7 upwards are algebraic manipulation inside longer questions, circle theorem proofs where the reasoning has to be written out and not just implied, iterative numerical methods, and the multi-stage problem solving that fills the end of Paper 3. What those have in common is that none of them tests a single fact. They test whether a student can hold three steps together under time, which is a different skill from knowing the topic."
  - q: "Should my child focus on the calculator or the non-calculator paper?"
    a: "Both carry equal weight, so the useful question is which one is losing more marks. The non-calculator paper rewards arithmetic fluency and clean algebraic manipulation. The calculator papers reward accuracy across multi-step work and the patience to read a long question properly before starting it. Most students leak marks in different places on each, and a tutor's first diagnostic is often simply marking one of each and comparing the two patterns."

The first session tells us which of the two we're dealing with, and we'll tell you honestly which it is."
