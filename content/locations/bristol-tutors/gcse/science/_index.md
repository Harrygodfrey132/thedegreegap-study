Plenty of Bristol families start with the subject that's hurting and add a second later if it's needed."
  - q: "My child is in Year 10. Is it too early to start Science tutoring?"
    a: "Year 10 is the best time to start, and more so for Science than most subjects. The content stacks, so a topic that doesn't land in Year 10 quietly undermines everything built on top of it in Year 11, and by then there's no room in the timetable to go back. Year 10 tutoring also looks different: more concept building, less exam drilling, and time to fix a required practical properly rather than memorising it in April. Starting early usually means fewer sessions overall, not more."
