  - q: "Does it matter whether my child's on AQA, Edexcel or OCR for English?"
    a: "Yes, more than most parents expect. The core skills are the same, but the papers are laid out differently, the wording in the mark schemes isn't the same, and schools choose different set texts. A tutor working to the wrong board can teach your child a good essay that still scores below what it's worth on the paper they'll actually sit. So we check the board and the set texts on the free call before we suggest anyone. Getting that wrong would waste the first month."
  - q: "How do I work out where my child is losing marks in English?"
    a: "Look at which questions lost marks on a recent mock, not just the total. If the reading answers hold up and the writing question is weak, it's almost always timing, not ability, and that's quick to work on. If marks drop away across the longer analysis answers, it's usually essay structure and how quotations get used. And if Literature's fine while Language dips, or the other way round, those are two separate problems. They need working on separately too."
