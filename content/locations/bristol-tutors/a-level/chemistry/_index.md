    a: "Group 2, and above all the solubility trends, because they run in opposite directions. The reactivity explanations cost marks separately, because they need shielding, distance and nuclear attraction in sequence."
  - q: "How can I tell which A-Level Chemistry topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Read a marked answer beside the scheme rather than looking at the total. If your child states a trend correctly and the scheme wanted a chain of reasoning they did not write, the gap is explanation structure and it closes quickly. If the trend itself is wrong, that is content, and with Group 2 it is usually the two solubility patterns merging. If calculations bleed marks everywhere, that is amount of substance."
