angle_body_1: "Group 2 is the column of the periodic table with magnesium, calcium and barium in it. It's often where your child first finds that learning a trend isn't the same as explaining it. Learn one pattern and it's easy to apply it, confidently, to the other. And the practical questions build on exactly that.
