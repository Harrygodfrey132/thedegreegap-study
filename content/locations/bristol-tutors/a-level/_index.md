faq_2_q: "My son says the jump to A-Level Physics is much steeper than GCSE. What does a tutor actually do?"
faq_2_a: "It usually isn't effort. A-Level Physics leans hard on the maths inside it and the multi-step problems GCSE never asked for. A Bristol A-Level Physics tutor diagnoses on a past paper, finds whether it's the mechanics, the fields work or the algebra letting him down, and drills the method and the required practicals until the marks come back."
faq_3_q: "How does A-Level tutoring actually move a UCAS predicted grade?"
faq_3_a: "The prediction comes from the school, built on Year 12 and early Year 13 performance. Tutoring works on that performance. A tutor marks recent essays and past papers the way an examiner would, finds where the depth or the AO3 evaluation is falling short, and drills it before the teacher sets the number. And the earlier that starts, the more room there is to move it."
