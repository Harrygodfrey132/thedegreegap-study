faqs:
  - q: "What is the hardest part of GCSE Science for Berkhamsted students?"
    a: "The Chemistry calculations, usually, followed by six-mark answers. Moles, concentrations and yield questions stack several steps, and a slip early on costs every mark after it. The long answers catch students who know the content but write it as a list rather than a linked explanation. Physics equations and unit conversions are a close third. Which one matters most varies by student, so the first session on a recent paper sorts out where your child's marks are going."
  - q: "Should my child sit foundation or higher tier GCSE Science?"
    a: "It depends on where they are working now and where they need to be. Higher tier gives access to grades 4 to 9, while foundation stops at a 5. If a student is sitting around a 5 in Year 10, higher is usually the right call with focused preparation on the harder questions. At a 3 or 4, foundation can be safer. Schools make the final decision, and a tutor's diagnostic in Year 10 gives you solid evidence to discuss with them."
  - q: "Does my child need a Science tutor or just more revision?"

If long questions are blank or crossed out, the method itself is not secure, and revision from notes rarely fixes that alone. If your child is already scoring well across all three sciences, targeted practice may be enough, and we will tell you so."
