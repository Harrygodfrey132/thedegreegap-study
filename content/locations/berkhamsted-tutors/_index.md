faq_1_a: "Grade 4 or above in English and Maths, plus an average of about grade 4 across six other GCSEs, going by the school's published admissions criteria. Then each A-Level has its own minimum on top, set out in the sixth form pack, and those are what catch most families out. It's the same bar whether your child's already at Ashlyns or joining from somewhere else. The application goes in during December of Year 11. Requirements do get revised, so check this year's pack before relying on any figure."

faq_3_q: "My child is a long way behind. Is it too late?"
faq_3_a: "Usually not, and we see it a lot. The tutor goes through a recent paper with your child, looks hard at the mistakes, and works back to the last thing that was really solid. So on the first call we'll say plainly which one we think your child needs, and roughly how many weeks it might take."
