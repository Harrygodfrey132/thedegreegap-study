faq_1_q: "Two subjects are fine and one is a disaster. Can we get help with just that one?"
faq_1_a: "Yes, usually, and it's often the best use of the time. An offer is three grades, so the weak subject is what holds the whole thing back, and it's the one taking up your child's evenings too. Sort it out and the other two often pick up without anyone touching them, because the time and headspace go back to them. We'd rather put one focused hour where it counts than spread three thinly."
faq_2_q: "Should my child drop to three subjects?"
faq_2_a: "It depends on what the courses they're after actually ask for. Check that before anything's dropped, because some courses name particular subjects. If a fourth subject isn't needed and it's costing grades in the other three, dropping it is often sensible. If it's one they need, it's better to get help with it than to drop it. It's worth talking to the sixth form about it as well as to us."
