faq_1_q: "Two subjects are fine and one is a disaster. Do we get help for just that one?"
faq_1_a: "Usually yes, and it is often the most efficient thing to do. An offer is a set of three grades, so the weak subject is the binding constraint, and it is also the one absorbing the evenings. Fixing it frequently lifts the other two without anyone working on them, because the time and the worry get returned. We would rather put one focused hour where it counts than spread three thinly."
faq_2_q: "Should my child drop to three subjects?"
faq_2_a: "It depends on what the courses they want actually ask for, and that is worth checking before anything is dropped, because some courses name specific subjects. Where a fourth is genuinely surplus and is costing grades in the other three, dropping it is often the right call. Where it is a required subject, the answer is support rather than removal. It is a conversation to have with the sixth form as well as with us."
faq_3_q: "Is Year 12 or Year 13 the right time to start?"

Year 13 still works and plenty of families start there, but the plan narrows towards exam technique and there is less room to rebuild a subject from the beginning."
