  - q: "How can I tell where my child's losing marks in Science?"
    a: "Take a marked paper and sort the lost marks into three piles: straight recall, data and graph questions, and the longer answers where your child has to apply what they know. Most children are clearly weaker at one. Lost marks on recall mean gaps in what they've learned. On data, it's usually a method that hasn't had enough practice yet. And if the losses bunch up on unfamiliar questions, the knowledge is there but isn't transferring. That's the one most often missed, and often the quickest of the three to improve."

The last part is fresh questions on that topic, including some deliberately set somewhere unfamiliar. The tutor marks them against the real mark scheme while your child watches, so they can see the reasoning that earns the marks."
