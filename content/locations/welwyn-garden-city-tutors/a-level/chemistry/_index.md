faqs:
  - q: "Does it matter which exam board my child's school uses for Chemistry?"
    a: "Yes, more than most parents expect. The chemistry is much the same across AQA, Edexcel and OCR, but the papers aren't. Each board sets its calculations out in its own way, gives its own data booklet and puts a different weight on the 'explain why' questions. So a child who's strong on method can find one board's papers noticeably harder than another's. We check your child's board on the call and only suggest tutors who know it."
  - q: "Is it a tutor my child needs, or just more practice?"
    a: "Try asking them why a method works, rather than asking them to do it. If they can do it but can't explain it, more practice won't help much, because practice repeats the method and never reaches the reasoning. That's the gap one-to-one lessons are good at closing. If they can't do the method at all, that's a different problem, and usually an easier one to see."
