  - q: "Why has my child's GCSE English predicted grade come in lower than I expected?"
    a: "Because the prediction comes from a mock written in an hour and forty-five minutes, and English is the subject where what a student knows and what they get down in that time diverge most. A Bournemouth Year 11 who discusses the poetry brilliantly at home can still hand in two answers and a half, and the half is where the grade went. Teachers predict from the paper in front of them, which is fair, and fixable. The tutor's first job is to read the mock script rather than the grade, find whether the marks went to timing, structure or accuracy, and rebuild the under-pressure version week by week."
