faqs:
  - q: "What's the hardest part of GCSE Maths for Knutsford students?"
    a: "Usually the long problem-solving questions at the back of each paper. Your child may know every topic in them, but the question doesn't say which one to use, and that's where marks disappear. Close behind are algebra inside worded questions and the non-calculator paper, where shaky fractions or negatives from Year 8 cost marks all the way through. For students aiming at a 7 or above, it's the multi-step questions. For students chasing a 4 or 5, it's usually the basics wobbling under time pressure."
  - q: "How can I tell which Maths topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Look at a recent mock paper and where on the paper the marks went. If the early questions are mostly right and the damage is in the last third, it's usually exam technique and working out where to start. If marks go missing all the way through, there's probably a gap from earlier years. Our tutors do this properly in the first lesson, question by question, but even a quick look together at the kitchen table can tell you a lot."
  - q: "Should my child sit foundation or higher tier?"
    a: "It depends where they are now and where they need to be. Higher tier covers grades 4 to 9, and foundation covers 1 to 5. Knutsford Academy has asked for a 4 in Maths for its sixth form, which is possible on either paper. A steady 5 or 6 in mocks usually points to higher. Hovering around a 3 or 4, foundation can be the safer bet. The school makes the final call, and a tutor's first lesson gives you something solid to take into that conversation."
