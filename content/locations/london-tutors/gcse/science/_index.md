  - q: "Is Triple Science worth it, or is Combined enough?"
    a: "Triple if they're confident in all three sciences and thinking about A-Levels in them. Otherwise, Combined is often the better call. Triple gives three separate GCSEs and covers more. Combined gives two GCSEs from less content, and it's the right choice far more often than parents assume, because a strong Combined grade beats a stretched Triple one. If your child is borderline in Year 10, one lesson looking at their recent work is usually enough to say which way to go."
