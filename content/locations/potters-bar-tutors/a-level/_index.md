faq_1_q: "Does it matter which sixth form or college my child goes to, for tutoring?"
faq_1_a: "Not really, as long as we know the exam board. Owen's, Gracewood, Chancellor's, Woodhouse and Barnet and Southgate decide their own boards and courses, so two friends taking the same subject can sit quite different papers. If your child is at Chancellor's or Gracewood, check which school teaches each of their subjects too, because the two sixth forms have worked as a consortium. We'll ask about all of that on the call and pick tutors who know your child's papers."
faq_2_q: "My child is retaking an A-Level. Is tutoring different the second time?"
faq_2_a: "Yes, quite a bit. A retake doesn't mean starting the whole course again. The tutor looks at what happened last time, using the results and any marked papers, and works out whether the marks went on missing topics or on the exam itself: timing, longer answers, or questions that needed a method your child hadn't practised. Then the plan goes after that. Some retakers study at home and some at a college, and lessons fit round either. Check the exam entry deadlines early with the school or exam centre."
faq_3_q: "Will the tutor help with A-Level coursework or an EPQ?"
faq_3_a: "Up to a point, and the line matters. The rules the exam boards follow, set through the JCQ, limit the help anyone can give on coursework, so a tutor can't read or check a draft. What a tutor can do is explain what the mark scheme rewards, help your child plan their time and their research, and teach the skills underneath, like weighing up a source or writing up results clearly. The work itself has to be your child's own, start to finish."
faq_4_q: "Can we pause lessons for study leave or over the summer?"
faq_4_a: "Of course. There's no contract, so you can pause whenever you like and pick up again later. We'd just like a little notice, so the tutor can plan around it. Some Year 13s keep one lesson a week going through study leave, since that's when past papers matter most, and stop once the last exam is done. Others take the summer off after Year 12 and restart in September. You only ever pay for the lessons that actually happen."
