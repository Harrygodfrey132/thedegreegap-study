  - q: "Should my child focus on the calculator or non-calculator paper?"
    a: "Both. On AQA, Edexcel and OCR all three papers count equally, and two of them allow a calculator, so together they're worth twice as much as the non-calculator one. That paper still matters, because it's where shaky arithmetic and algebra show up first. The calculator papers lean more on problems with several steps, and the final questions of the third paper are where a lot of children run out of ideas or time. Your child's last mock will usually show which of those is costing them."
  - q: "Higher or Foundation: which is right for my child?"
    a: "Foundation papers go up to a grade 5. Higher papers cover 9 down to 4, with a 3 possible for a near miss. If your child is scraping 4s on Higher mocks, Foundation can feel safer, but it caps the grade, which matters if A-Level Maths is a possibility. The school makes the final decision. A tutor can work on the Higher-only topics, such as surds or harder algebraic fractions, and give you real evidence from your child's work to take into that conversation."
