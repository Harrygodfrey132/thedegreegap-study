  - q: "Does it matter which exam board my child's Science GCSE is with?"
    a: "It does, mostly in how questions are worded and marked. The science itself overlaps a lot, but each board names and groups its set experiments in its own way, and what the mark scheme looks for can vary. A tutor who knows your child's board can use real past questions from the first lesson. If you're not sure which board it is, a mock paper or the school's website will usually say, and we'll check on the call."
  - q: "Is it a tutor my child needs, or just a few more weeks of revision?"

If there are blanks, crossed-out starts or the same mistake in several places, that points to a gap in understanding, and revision on its own tends to go round in circles. If you're unsure, that's what the free call is for, and if we think practice at home will do, we'll tell you."
