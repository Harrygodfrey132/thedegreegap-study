Woodhouse College and the school sixth forms each publish their own entry requirements and revise them between years, so the first job is reading this year's version rather than what an older sibling remembers. From there the work is the subject grades the application rests on, plus any assessment or interview a school runs. Tell us the shortlist on the call and we will be honest about the distance."
faq_2_q: "Which exam boards do your Barnet tutors cover?"
faq_2_a: "AQA, Edexcel, OCR, WJEC, OCR MEI and the international specifications. Board is part of the match rather than a detail sorted out later, because the boards word questions differently, examine practicals differently and print different formulae. A tutor who knows your child's specification is working properly inside ten minutes. One who does not spends the first few sessions catching up at your expense."
