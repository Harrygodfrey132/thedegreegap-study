faq_2_q: "How should my child actually be using their free periods?"
faq_2_a: "Mostly on questions, not notes. Rereading feels productive but doesn't stick well, so a good free period might be half an hour of past-paper questions on this week's topic, marked against the scheme, then twenty minutes listing what went wrong. The tutor can set that work and check it in the next lesson, which gives the week a rhythm. Once it's a habit, most sixth formers find their evenings get lighter, not heavier."
