    a: "Usually the long essays rather than a single topic. Students learn the models and diagrams well, then struggle to apply them to the case in front of them and reach a real judgement. In macro, questions that link several ideas, such as interest rates feeding into exchange rates and inflation, cause the most trouble. A tutor practises the steps after the point: a real example, how big the effect is, and who wins or loses."
