faq_1_q: "My child sailed through GCSEs and is now struggling in Year 12. What's going on?"
faq_1_a: "It's very common, and it isn't a sign they've stopped trying. A-Levels ask for different things: fewer facts learnt and repeated, more applying ideas to questions they haven't seen, and longer answers that need a structure. Revising the night before often worked at GCSE and stops working around October of Year 12. A tutor spots which habit is letting your child down, whether that's note-taking, exam technique or a GCSE topic that was never quite secure, and starts there."
