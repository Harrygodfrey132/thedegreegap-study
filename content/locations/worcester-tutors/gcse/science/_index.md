  - q: "Should my child take Combined or Triple Science?"
    a: "Combined is worth two GCSEs graded from 9-9 down, Triple is worth three separate grades. Triple carries more content and more exam time, so it suits a student comfortable across all three who wants the Sciences to lead somewhere. Combined is not a lesser route and does not close A-Level Sciences at most colleges, though requirements vary and are worth checking on the current page. What matters more than the label is whether the weakest of the three is being carried by the other two."
  - q: "Can one tutor cover Biology, Chemistry and Physics?"
    a: "Sometimes, and we will be straight about when. Many tutors cover two of the three at real depth, usually Biology with Chemistry or Chemistry with Physics, and fewer cover all three. If whole topics are missing in Physics while the other two are solid, an hour split three ways does less than an hour aimed at the actual problem. For Triple Science students taking Maths as well, a Maths and Physics tutor gives a useful overlap."
