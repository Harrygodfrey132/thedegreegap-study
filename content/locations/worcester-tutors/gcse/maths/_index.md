  - q: "Is foundation tier giving up on GCSE Maths?"
    a: "On higher tier the available grades run from 4 to 9. On foundation they run from 1 to 5. Since most post-16 courses locally ask for a 4 or 5, foundation keeps the doors open. A Year 10 diagnostic answers this properly."
