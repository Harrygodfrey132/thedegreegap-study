  - q: "Is the GCSE English standard in Luton similar to St Albans?"
    a: "The difference is real at borough level and much smaller at desk level. Hertfordshire sits above the national picture and Luton a shade below it, which tells you something about two large areas and nothing about your child. English is also the subject where the comparison matters least, because the papers and the mark schemes are the same either side of the county line. A Luton student who can build an evaluation paragraph earns what anyone else earns for that paragraph. What differs is how much written work a big class can get marked."
  - q: "When is the best time to start GCSE English tutoring in Year 11?"
    a: "September to February, and the earlier half of that window is worth more. Winter mocks feed the predicted grades that go onto post-16 applications in Luton, so work landing before them counts twice over. English has a second reason. What a student understands has to become what a marker can award, and that habit gets built by writing something most weeks across a term, not by a fortnight of revision in May. A spring start still helps. It just narrows to technique on the questions carrying the most marks."
  - q: "My child is in Year 10. Is that too early to start English tutoring?"

Essay structure takes months to become automatic, and Year 10 has months. Sessions at this stage feel less like exam prep and more like planning, redrafting and reading work aloud. By the time the Year 11 mocks come round the student has a shape they trust rather than a blank page."
