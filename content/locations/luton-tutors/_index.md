faq_1_q: "How is a session actually structured for a Luton student?"
faq_1_a: "There is no fixed format, because the first session is a diagnostic. The tutor usually starts with a recent piece of school work or a marked mock and works backwards through the wrong answers until the understanding runs out. That point is normally earlier than anyone guessed. After that the hour splits between rebuilding whatever was missing and practising exam questions on it, and past papers arrive once there is something worth testing. The plan runs alongside school rather than repeating it."
faq_2_q: "Which exam boards do your tutors cover?"
faq_2_a: "AQA, Edexcel, OCR, WJEC and the international specifications, plus OCR MEI at A-Level Maths. We confirm the board on the consultation call before anyone is matched, because it is part of the criteria rather than a detail sorted out later. Schools across Luton do not all sit the same board, and the boards word questions differently, examine practicals differently and print different formulae. A tutor who knows your child's specification is working properly within ten minutes."
