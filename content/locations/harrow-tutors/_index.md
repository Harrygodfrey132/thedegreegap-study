faq_1_q: "Can you help with the Harrow Collegiate sixth form application?"
faq_1_a: "Yes, and the timing matters more here than in places where each sixth form is applied to on its own. The Collegiate form goes in during the autumn of Year 11, and the schools read predicted grades and mock results when they make conditional offers. Each sixth form publishes its own entry requirements and revises them between years, with the more competitive A-Level courses often asking for a grade in that subject. Work landing before the winter mocks counts twice over: once for the prediction, once for the exam itself."
