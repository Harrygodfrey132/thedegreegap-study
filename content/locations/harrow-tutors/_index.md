faq_1_q: "Will tutoring help with the Harrow Collegiate application?"
faq_1_a: "Yes, and the timing matters more here than in places where you apply to each sixth form separately. The Collegiate form goes in during the autumn of Year 11. The schools then look at predicted grades and mock results when they make conditional offers. Each sixth form publishes its own entry requirements and can change them between years. The more competitive A-Level courses often ask for a grade in that subject. So work that lands before the winter mocks counts twice: once for the prediction, and again in the exam itself."
