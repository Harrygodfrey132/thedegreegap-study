  - q: "Which Maths paper should my child practise most, calculator or non-calculator?"
    a: "Whichever one is costing more marks, and there's usually a clear answer. GCSE Maths has three papers, each worth a third of the grade. On the calculator papers, it's long multi-step problems and rounding slips near the end, which is where a lot of Harrow children quietly lose a grade. The first lesson shows which is doing more damage, and that one gets the practice first."
