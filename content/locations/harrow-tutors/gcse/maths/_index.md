  - q: "Should my child focus on the calculator or non-calculator GCSE Maths paper?"
    a: "Both, because they carry equal weight and fail for different reasons. The non-calculator paper punishes shaky arithmetic and slow algebraic manipulation. The calculator papers punish multi-step problem solving and rounding errors near the end, which is where a lot of Harrow students quietly lose a grade. The diagnostic settles which one is doing more damage, and usually one is clearly worse. Then that paper gets the drilling first and the other gets maintained rather than ignored."
