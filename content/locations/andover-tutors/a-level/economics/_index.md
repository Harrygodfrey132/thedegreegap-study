  - q: "Why do my child's Economics essays keep landing in the middle mark band?"
    a: "Usually because they explain well and judge too little. The higher bands reward evaluation: which effect matters more, over what time period, and what it depends on. Many students know that and still tack one sentence of it onto the end. A tutor shows your child how to weigh each point as they go, using the diagram as evidence rather than decoration. It's a habit, so it takes a few weeks of essays to settle."
