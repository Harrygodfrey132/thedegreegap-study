faqs:
  - q: "He passed the Kent Test and did well at GCSE. Why is A-Level Maths suddenly so hard?"
    a: "Because it's a different kind of test, and plenty of Sevenoaks families have asked us the same thing. The Kent Test was about speed and accuracy, and GCSE questions usually make it clear which topic they're on. A-Level questions often don't, and more of the marks go on choosing a method and setting out an argument. Children who've always found Maths easy can take that personally. It rarely has anything to do with ability, and a few weeks of the right practice usually shows him that."
  - q: "We're in Sevenoaks and he's already in Year 13. Is that too late to start?"
    a: "No, though Year 12 is easier. Much of the first year's Maths sits underneath the second, so starting early means fewer gaps to fill later. If an admissions test is planned, start sooner rather than later, because that preparation runs alongside the A-Level rather than instead of it."
