  - q: "Should my child focus on the calculator or non-calculator paper for GCSE Maths?"
    a: "Both count equally, so the real question is which is losing more marks. The non-calculator paper rewards arithmetic fluency and confident algebra, and it is where a student carrying an older gap usually suffers most, because there is nowhere to hide a shaky method. The calculator papers reward accuracy over multi-step problems and careful reading of the wordy questions near the back. Almost every student is clearly weaker on one of the two, and the first session identifies which, so the early weeks are spent where the marks actually are."
