faq_1_q: "Can one tutor help with English Language and English Literature?"
faq_1_a: "Usually, yes. They're two separate GCSEs that ask for different things: Language gives your child extracts they've never seen and a piece of their own writing to produce, while Literature tests set texts and the quotations to back up an argument. One English tutor can cover both, often giving most of the time to whichever is further behind. If your child is at Glyn, that means texts like Macbeth and An Inspector Calls on the Literature side."
faq_2_q: "My son has just started Year 10. Should we begin now or wait for the mocks?"
faq_2_a: "If a subject is already slipping, now is better. Year 10 gives the tutor time to fix older gaps, a Year 8 algebra method or a habit of retelling the plot in English, before Year 11 content piles on top. Lessons in Year 10 are more about understanding than exam drills. Wait for the mocks and you'll have a clearer picture, but less time to act on it."
