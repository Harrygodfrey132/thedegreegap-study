faq_2_q: "QE's applications close in February. When should my child start GCSE tutoring?"
faq_2_a: "Ideally in Year 10, or the autumn of Year 11 at the latest. The application goes in on predicted grades, but the offer depends on the real ones in August, so the months after February count for a lot. Starting earlier leaves room to fix older gaps, like a Year 8 algebra method or an essay structure that never settled. A later start still helps. The plan just narrows to past papers and the topics costing the most."
faq_3_q: "Some QE course pages mention an 'average GCSE score'. What does that mean for my child?"
faq_3_a: "It's an average of your child's GCSE grades, and several QE courses ask for one alongside grades in particular subjects. Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Maths and Economics currently ask for an average of 5.5, for instance. So a weaker grade in a subject your child isn't planning to continue can still pull the average down. Ask QE exactly how they work it out, and a tutor can help lift whichever subject is dragging."
