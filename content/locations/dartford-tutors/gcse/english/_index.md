faqs:
  - q: "Which GCSE English questions cost Dartford students the most marks?"
    a: "For a lot of them, it's any question that puts two texts side by side. The other common one is remembering exact quotations, since the Literature exams are closed book. A tutor can usually tell from one marked essay which of these is holding your child back."
  - q: "Does it matter whether my child sits AQA, Edexcel or OCR English?"
    a: "Yes, more than you'd think. The skills are much the same, but each board sets different texts, words its questions differently and splits the marks in its own way. Edexcel's Literature course, which Wilmington Grammar School for Boys follows, has its own conflict poetry collection, for example, and AQA's anthology has different poems. We check the board and set texts on the call and match a tutor who knows them."
