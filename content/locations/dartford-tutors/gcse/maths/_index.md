    a: "Both, though not equally to begin with. All three papers count equally, one non-calculator and two calculator, so the calculator papers carry two-thirds of the marks between them. The non-calculator paper tests number fluency and algebra, and it's where early marks often slip. The calculator papers reward multi-step problem-solving, and marks tend to drain away near the end of the final paper. A recent mock shows which is costing your child more, and that's where the tutor starts."
