faq_1_q: "Which exam boards do Dartford schools use for GCSE, and does it matter?"
faq_1_a: "It varies by school and by subject, and yes, it matters. Wilmington Grammar School for Boys, for example, sits AQA for GCSE Maths, OCR for Science and Edexcel for English Literature, and a school down the road may choose differently for every one. The topics overlap, but the papers are built differently and reward different things. So we check your child's board for each subject on the call and match a tutor who knows it."
faq_2_q: "My child spends hours on homework and the marks still aren't great. Does that mean they need a tutor?"
faq_2_a: "Often, though not always. Watch where the time goes. If your child sits staring at the first step of a question, a method is probably missing, and finding that is what a tutor does fastest. If the work gets done slowly but carefully, it may be more about organisation or confidence, and a few weeks of lessons can still help. If homework is quick and the marks are good, past papers may be all they need. The free call is a good place to talk it through."
