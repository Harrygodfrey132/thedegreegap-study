faq_1_q: "Can you help with the Sutton grammar test, or the Whitgift and Trinity entrance papers?"
faq_1_a: "Yes, and Croydon families ask us about them a lot. The Sutton test is sat in September, right at the start of Year 6, and you register over the summer before. So the useful preparation happens in Year 5. Whitgift and Trinity set their own entrance papers at several ages from 10 upwards, and both want GCSE grades and an exam for sixth form entry too. Tutors on our platform know how each one is put together, and they'll work on timing and technique as well as content. And if we think a test isn't the right route for your child, we'll say so on the call."
faq_2_q: "Two Croydon schools teach the same subject on different exam boards. Does that matter?"
faq_2_a: "Yes, quite a lot, and it's one of the first things we check. Schools across the borough follow different courses for the same subject, and AQA, Edexcel and OCR each have their own question style and wording. A tutor who already knows your child's board can get going from the first lesson, instead of spending weeks catching up. Just tell us the school and the board on the call. Not sure which it is? A photo of an exercise book or the front of a mock paper usually settles it."
faq_3_q: "The sixth forms will see my child's mock grades. Can a tutor help before then?"
faq_3_a: "Yes, and the earlier in the autumn you start, the better. For most Year 11s the mocks land somewhere between November and January, and Croydon's sixth forms and colleges mostly take applications over the winter, so they'll read those grades. That's why the weeks before the mocks are where a weekly hour does most. The tutor works through past papers from your child's board, marks them the way an examiner would and drills whatever's losing the most marks. Usually that's two or three types of question, not a whole topic. Starting after the mocks still helps. It just means a predicted grade's already been written once."

We'll ask about the year group, the school and the board, and where your child honestly is now. Then the grade they need, whether that's for a college course, a sixth form place or an apprenticeship. Within 24 hours you'll have two or three tutor profiles, and your child can meet the one you like on a free video call before you pay anything. There's no contract and no minimum term. And if tutoring isn't what your child needs right now, we'll tell you."
