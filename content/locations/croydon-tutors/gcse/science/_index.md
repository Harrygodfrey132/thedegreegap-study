  - q: "What is the difference between AQA, Edexcel and OCR GCSE Science, and does it matter for tutoring?"
    a: "It matters most near the grade boundaries. The core content overlaps heavily, but the boards differ on which equations are printed and which must be recalled, on the wording of the six-mark questions, and on the list of required practicals an examiner can ask about. A student revising the wrong practical list, or memorising an equation their board prints, is spending effort in the wrong place. So we confirm the board on the consultation call, and for a Croydon family that can mean a different answer for two children at two schools."

A student near the boundary in Year 10 mocks is often right on higher with a term of focused work on the topics that decide it, and a student sitting lower is often safer on foundation. A Year 10 diagnostic turns that into a decision with evidence, and school should be part of the conversation."
