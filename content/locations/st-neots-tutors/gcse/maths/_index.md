  - q: "Should my child focus on the calculator or the non-calculator Maths paper?"
    a: "Both, but the non-calculator paper is often where a tutor starts. On AQA, Edexcel and OCR your child sits three papers, one without a calculator, and all three count equally. Eduqas sets two. What we see a lot is marks lost early on the non-calculator paper, on fractions, decimals and long multiplication that a calculator would normally do. Then, on the last calculator paper, the losses come late, in longer problems where time runs short. Your child's last mock will usually show which pattern they've got, and the plan follows from that."
  - q: "How do I know if my child needs a Maths tutor, or just more revision?"

If they can't get started, or the same topic keeps going wrong test after test, that's the sort of gap that's hard to fix alone. A tutor can find where it began and rebuild from there.
