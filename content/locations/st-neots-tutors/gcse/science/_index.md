  - q: "Will my child be entered for Higher or Foundation Science?"
    a: "That's the school's decision, often made during Year 11, whether your child's at a St Neots school or one further out. It helps to know what's at stake. Foundation papers can't give more than a grade 5. Higher papers go from 9 down to 4, and a 3 is allowed for a near miss. So a child already scoring 6s has room on Higher that Foundation would take away, while one sitting near a 3 may pick up more marks on Foundation's gentler questions. Combined Science is one tier across every paper, giving a double grade such as 5-4. A tutor's view after a few lessons can be useful to share with the school."
