  - q: "What is the hardest part of GCSE English for Woking students?"
    a: "The closed-book memory load, and then the clock. Literature asks students to quote accurately from texts they cannot open, build an argument around a theme rather than retell a plot, and do it twice inside one paper. Unseen poetry is the other regular casualty, because there is no revision to fall back on, only method. Language Question 5 arrives last, when concentration has gone. None of that measures how well your child understands the books, which is why it moves so quickly with timed practice."
  - q: "What happens in a typical GCSE English session?"
    a: "An hour in three parts. The first fifteen minutes goes on last week's essay, marked against the real criteria, with the tutor showing where a band was reached and where it stopped. The last stretch is a timed question on that technique, done live on the whiteboard. Homework is usually one essay, because in English being marked properly beats volume."
