  - q: "What's the hardest thing about GCSE English for children in Woking?"
    a: "Remembering enough with the book closed, and then the clock. Literature asks your child to quote accurately from texts they can't open, build an argument about a theme rather than retell the plot, and do it twice in one paper. Unseen poetry is the other regular problem, because there's nothing to revise, only a method. And the Language writing question comes last, when concentration's gone. None of that measures how well your child understands the books. That's why timed practice moves it so quickly."

The last part is a timed question on that technique, done live on the whiteboard. Homework's usually one essay, because being marked properly beats writing lots."
