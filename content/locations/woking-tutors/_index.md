faq_1_q: "What grades will my child need for Woking College?"
faq_1_a: "It depends on the course, and the college publishes each one separately. To start an A-Level programme, it asks for at least five GCSE passes at grade 4 to 9, with subject requirements on top. The exact offer is settled at interview. A-Level Maths names at least a grade 6 in GCSE Maths, Further Maths asks for a 7, and Physics also wants a 6 in Maths. These get reviewed between years, so go by this year's course page rather than what an older sibling remembers."

faq_3_q: "Can a tutor help if my child is a year or more behind?"
faq_3_a: "Yes. A fair bit of our work starts right here. The tutor takes your child's wrong answers on a recent paper and follows each one back to where it first stopped making sense. That's usually earlier than anyone guessed. From there the plan builds forward alongside school, rather than repeating what's taught in class. It takes longer than fixing exam technique, and we'll tell you which of the two we think you're dealing with."
