  - q: "Does my child need a calculator paper tutor or a non-calculator one?"
    a: "The same tutor, but the two papers fail for different reasons and a good diagnostic separates them. Marks lost on the non-calculator paper are usually arithmetic fluency and algebraic manipulation, which need short daily practice rather than teaching. Marks lost on the calculator papers are more often multi-step reasoning, where the student knows every individual step and cannot see the order to put them in. Bring both papers to the first session if you have them, because a student fine on one and struggling on the other tells us more than an overall grade."
