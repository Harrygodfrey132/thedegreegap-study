  - q: "What is the difference between AQA, Edexcel and OCR GCSE Science, and does it matter for tutoring?"
    a: "It matters, and Science is where it matters most. The boards cover similar content but examine the required practicals differently, mark the extended six-mark answers to different structures, and give different amounts of equation support in the paper itself. A student who learned a practical one way can meet it framed another way and lose marks on material they genuinely know. We confirm the board before suggesting anyone, and for a student who has changed schools partway we look for a tutor who has taught both specifications."
  - q: "Should my child take foundation or higher tier GCSE Science?"
    a: "It depends on where your child sits now rather than on where you would like them to finish. Higher tier reaches the top grades but scores very little below its floor, and foundation caps lower while making the available marks far more reachable. A student sitting close to the boundary in Year 10 is often right on higher with focused work on the specific topics that decide it. A student well below is usually better served by foundation, where confidence builds on questions they can actually do. A Year 10 diagnostic turns that into a decision instead of a guess, and it is worth discussing with school as well."
  - q: "Can a Science tutor help a child with dyslexia, ADHD or processing differences?"
    a: "Yes, and several tutors in the network have real experience here. In Science a lot of the work is reducing how much has to be held in mind at once: a fixed layout for calculations so the steps do not get lost, planning frames for the six-mark answers, and breaking each required practical into named parts rather than learning it as a block of prose. One-to-one suits this better than a class because the pace belongs to the student. Tell us on the call what school has in place, including any exam access arrangements, and we match accordingly."
