  - q: "What is the hardest part of GCSE English for Ipswich students?"
    a: "Two things, and they are related. Unseen poetry is the first, because nothing about it can be learned in advance and most revision is built around learning things in advance. The comparison question is the second: holding two texts against each other, rather than writing about one and then the other, is a genuinely different skill and schools rarely have time to drill it. On the Language paper the equivalent is the evaluation question, where an opinion is asked for and a summary gets handed in."

The work is calmer then too, weighted towards reading widely and writing often rather than drilling papers against the clock. Year 11 still works and plenty of Ipswich families begin there, but the plan narrows to technique."
