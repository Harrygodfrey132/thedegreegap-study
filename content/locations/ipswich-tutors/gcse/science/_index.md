  - q: "Why is my child struggling with GCSE Science?"
    a: "Nine times out of ten it traces back to method rather than memory. Science at GCSE rewards students who can write a process down, and plenty of capable children have learned the content by heart without ever practising the writing. In Physics it shows as rearranging failing under pressure. In Chemistry it is moles and concentration. In Biology it is the six and nine-mark answers, where the facts are all there and the chain of reasoning between them is missing. Week one is about finding which of those it is."
  - q: "Are your GCSE Science tutors qualified teachers or university students?"

A student who needs a topic opening up from scratch usually does better with a qualified teacher who has taught it a hundred times and knows exactly where it goes wrong. A student whose content is fine but whose long answers are not often does better with a recent graduate who sat these papers and remembers the mark scheme in detail. Sometimes the deciding factor is just who your child will talk to honestly."
