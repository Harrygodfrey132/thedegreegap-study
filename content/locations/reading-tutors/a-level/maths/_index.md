faqs:
  - q: "How can I tell where my child is losing Maths marks?"
    a: "Mark one question line by line, not just the final answer. In calculus questions the derivative's often right, and the marks go in the tidying up before it or the factorising after it. That points at algebra, not at the topic being taught. Blank questions mean your child couldn't start. Lots of small, scattered slips mean accuracy. Each of those needs a different term of work."
  - q: "Could my child keep going with Maths over the holidays?"
    a: "Yes, and for getting algebra fluent the holidays are some of the most useful weeks of the year. The skill needs lots of practice close together, not spread thinly across a term. A half-term or Easter block can drill it over several days in a row while nothing else competes. It's all online, so if you're away from home your child can keep their slot."
