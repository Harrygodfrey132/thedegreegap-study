faqs:
  - q: "How can I tell which parts of A-Level Maths my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Mark one question line by line rather than looking at the final answer. In calculus questions the derivative is frequently correct and the loss happens in the simplification before it or the factorising after it, which points at algebra rather than at the topic being taught. Blank questions mean the student could not start. Scattered small errors mean accuracy. Those three need different terms of work."
  - q: "Can my child have Maths tutoring during the school holidays?"
    a: "Yes, and for rebuilding algebraic fluency the holidays are the best weeks of the year, because the skill needs repetition close together rather than spread across a term. A half-term or Easter block can take manipulation and drill it across consecutive days while nothing else competes. Everything runs online, so families away from home keep their slot."
