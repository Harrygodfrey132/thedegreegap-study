  - q: "What is the difference between AQA, Edexcel and OCR GCSE English, and does it matter for tutoring?"
    a: "It matters a great deal, more than most parents expect. The boards set different texts, weight context differently, and structure the Language papers differently, including whether an extract is printed with the question. A student prepared for one board's paper can be genuinely thrown by another's format even with identical knowledge. This is why we confirm the board and the set texts before suggesting anyone, and why a tutor who has taught your child's exact combination is worth holding out an extra day for."
  - q: "Why is my child struggling with GCSE English?"
    a: "Most often because the writing has never been taught as a skill in its own right. Schools cover the texts thoroughly, then set an essay that has rules of its own, and the distance between understanding a book and scoring on a question about it is where the marks disappear. The usual sign is a student who talks well about a text and writes a description of it. The other frequent cause is timing: one strong paragraph followed by two rushed ones. Both are habits, and habits shift faster than knowledge gaps do."
