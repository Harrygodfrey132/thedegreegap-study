  - q: "What is the difference between AQA, Edexcel and OCR GCSE Science, and does it matter for tutoring?"
    a: "Yes, and in Science it matters more than in most subjects. Content overlaps heavily while examining does not: the boards assess required practicals differently, mark six-mark answers to different structures, and print different amounts of equation support in the paper. A student who met a practical under one board's method can meet it framed another way and lose marks on material they genuinely know. We confirm the board on the consultation call, and where a student has changed schools we look for a tutor who has taught both."
