faq_1_q: "My child's friends at other schools seem further ahead. Is my child behind?"
faq_1_a: "Usually not, though it's worth checking properly. Each school decides the order it teaches GCSE topics in, so one class might do algebraic fractions in Year 9 and another in Year 10. By the exams, every board's papers cover the same core content. What matters is whether your child understands what they've been taught so far. A tutor finds that out from a recent test or mock, question by question. If there's a real gap, you'll know exactly where it is. If there isn't, that's worth hearing too."

faq_4_q: "How can I tell whether my child needs a tutor or just needs to revise?"
faq_4_a: "Sit down with a recent marked paper together. If the answers start well and then stop, or the same kind of question goes wrong every time, that's usually a gap a tutor can find quickly. If the marks go on slips made in a rush, a few weeks of timed practice at home might be enough. Sometimes revision really is the answer. And if it is, Harry or Joe will tell you that on the call."
