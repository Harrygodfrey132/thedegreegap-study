  - q: "Should my child focus on the calculator or the non-calculator paper?"
    a: "Both count equally, so the answer comes from the mocks rather than a rule. The non-calculator paper rewards fluent arithmetic and clean algebra, and students who lean on a calculator in class often lose marks there early. The calculator papers reward problem-solving and accuracy over several steps, and the damage tends to sit in the longer questions near the end. A tutor sorts a recent mock by paper and by question position, which shows which one is costing more."
