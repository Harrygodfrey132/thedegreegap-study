faq_1_q: "Does one weak GCSE really matter if the rest are fine?"
faq_1_a: "In Bishop's Stortford it can, because most sixth forms here add the grades together. A student on 6s in seven subjects and a 3 in the eighth lands on 45, only just over the 44 that the High School and Herts and Essex publish for A-Level entry. Lift that 3 to a 5 and there is breathing room. And if the weak subject is Maths or English, the separate minimum applies as well. That's why our first call looks at all eight grades, not just the one you rang about."
