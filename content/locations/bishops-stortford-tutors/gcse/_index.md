faq_1_q: "Does one weak GCSE really matter if the rest are fine?"
faq_1_a: "It can, because most sixth forms here add the grades together. Say your child gets 6s in seven subjects and a 3 in the eighth. That's 45, only just over the 44 the High School and Herts and Essex publish for A-Level entry. Lift that 3 to a 5 and there's some breathing room. And if the weak one is Maths or English, there's a separate minimum for those as well. That's why our first call looks at all eight grades, not just the one that's worrying you."
