    a: "Both matter, because all three papers count equally: one non-calculator and two calculator papers, so the calculator papers carry two-thirds of the marks between them. In practice, plenty of students drop marks early on the non-calculator paper, on fractions, negatives and written methods, and more towards the back of the last calculator paper on multi-step problems. A first lesson with a recent mock usually shows which paper is costing your child most."
