  - q: "Why is my child's GCSE English predicted grade lower than I expected?"
    a: "Predictions come from mock performance and teacher judgement, and English mocks punish timing harder than almost any other subject. A student who writes strong analysis slowly will be predicted on the unfinished paper, not on the quality of the paragraphs they did complete. That is the single most common cause we see. It is also one of the more fixable, since timing responds to practice under real conditions far faster than analysis does."
  - q: "What happens in a typical GCSE English tutoring session?"
