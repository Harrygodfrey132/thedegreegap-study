faqs:
  - q: "What is the hardest topic in GCSE Maths for Epsom students?"
    a: "Circle theorem proofs and the multi-step problem-solving questions at the end of Paper 3, year after year. Both fail for the same reason: they need reasoning written out in a form the mark scheme recognises, and strong students often see the answer without being able to evidence the route. Iterative methods sit just behind them. None of these are about ability. They are the questions that reward a taught method, which is why they move quickly once someone works on them properly."
  - q: "Should my child take foundation or higher tier GCSE Maths?"
    a: "Higher tier caps at grade 9 and floors at 4, sometimes 3. Foundation caps at 5. For most Epsom students the question does not arise, but where a child is sitting at a borderline 4 or 5 in Year 10, it is worth taking seriously rather than defaulting to higher because everyone else is. A wasted higher paper serves nobody. The honest version of this conversation needs a diagnostic first, which is what a tutor's first session provides."
