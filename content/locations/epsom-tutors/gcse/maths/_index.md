  - q: "Which GCSE Maths topics cost able Epsom children the most marks?"
    a: "Circle theorem proofs and the multi-step problems at the end of the last calculator paper, year after year. Both go wrong for the same reason. The reasoning has to be written out in a way the mark scheme recognises, and a strong child often sees the answer without being able to show the route. Iterative methods come just behind. None of this is about ability. These questions reward a taught method, which is why they tend to move quickly once someone works on them properly."
