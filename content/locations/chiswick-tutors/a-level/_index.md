faq_2_q: "My child got an A in one test and a D in the next. What does that usually mean?"
faq_2_a: "Usually that the gap is narrower than the D suggests. The common causes are a topic that later ones depend on, like moles in Chemistry or differentiation in Maths, or a type of question your child hasn't learned to plan, often the long ones that ask them to explain or evaluate. Sometimes it's simply timing. A tutor lines up recent tests and marks them as an examiner would, which tends to show which of these it is within a lesson or two."
