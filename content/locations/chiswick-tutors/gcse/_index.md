faq_2_q: "Foundation or Higher: who decides which tier my child sits, and can it still change?"

Higher covers grades 4 to 9 and Foundation goes up to a 5, which is why a child hovering around a 4 or 5 is the hardest call. A tutor can show you which topics separate the two papers for your child, so the conversation with school is about evidence rather than a feeling."
