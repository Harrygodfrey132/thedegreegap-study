  - q: "Should my child focus on the calculator or non-calculator paper for GCSE Maths?"
    a: "Both, because they are weighted equally and decide the grade together. The non-calculator paper rewards arithmetic fluency and clean algebra; the two calculator papers reward accuracy on multi-step, wordy problems, which is where most Manchester students we see lose marks at the back end. The first session marks a recent paper of each to find which is bleeding more, and the early lessons target that paper first."
  - q: "How can I tell which GCSE Maths topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Look at the question numbers on a recent mock, not just the total. On a higher AQA or Edexcel paper the early questions cover number and basic algebra, the middle runs through ratio and geometry, and the last third is where circle theorems, iterative methods and multi-step problem-solving live. If the marks keep going in that final block, it is exam technique, not subject knowledge, and it responds quickly to targeted practice."
