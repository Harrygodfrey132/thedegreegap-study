  - q: "We moved, and the new sixth form uses a different exam board. Will that cause gaps?"
    a: "It can, but usually small ones, and they're very fixable. Every board covers the same topics, apart from the set of real data each one uses in Statistics. What's different is how questions are worded and how the three papers are split. Edexcel puts Statistics and Mechanics together on its third paper, for example, while AQA pairs each of them with Pure. Schools also teach the topics in different orders, so a move mid-course can mean a topic that neither school got round to. Finding it is the tutor's first job."
