  - q: "How do I know if my child needs a Maths tutor or just more revision?"
    a: "Look at their working on a recent paper. Blank space on whole question types is a missing block, and no amount of revision fixes it because there is nothing there to revise. Clean working that loses marks near the end is an exam technique gap, which is the fastest thing a tutor fixes. Crossed-out attempts on multi-step questions is a method confidence gap. The three look identical on a report and need different terms of work."
  - q: "Should my child take foundation or higher tier GCSE Maths?"
    a: "Higher tier caps at grade 9 and floors at a 4, sometimes a 3. Foundation caps at 5 and floors at 1. A student sitting around a 4 or 5 in Year 10 mocks is usually better served by higher tier with targeted work on the early and middle questions, because that is where grades 4 to 6 actually live. Where a school move has left blocks missing, the honest thing is to find and fill those before the tier conversation, because the mock that decides it may not reflect what the student can do."
