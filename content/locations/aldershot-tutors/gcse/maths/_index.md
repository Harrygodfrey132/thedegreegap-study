  - q: "Is it more revision my child needs, or a tutor?"
    a: "A recent marked paper will usually tell you. Blank space across whole types of question means a missing topic, and revision can't fix that, because there's nothing there to revise. Tidy working that drops marks in the final questions is exam technique, which a tutor can sort quickly. Crossed-out attempts on long questions mean your child isn't sure of the method. All three look the same on a school report. But each needs different work."
  - q: "Should my child sit foundation or higher tier after a school move?"
    a: "Often higher, but fill any missing topics before you decide. Higher tier, the harder set of papers, runs from a 4 up to a 9, with a 3 as a safety net. Foundation tops out at a 5. If your child's on a 4 or 5 in Year 10 mocks, higher is often the better choice, with work on the early and middle questions, where grades 4 to 6 are won. But if a move has left gaps, the mock that decides the tier may not show what your child can really do."
