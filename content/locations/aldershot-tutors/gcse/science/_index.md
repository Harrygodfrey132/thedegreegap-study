  - q: "Does the exam board make a difference in GCSE Science?"
    a: "Yes, more than in most subjects, and it matters most to students who have changed schools. AQA, Edexcel and OCR cover overlapping content but differ in how the required practicals are examined, how the six-mark questions are marked and which formulae are given rather than learned. A student who covered a practical at one school under one board's method can meet a question about it framed the other way. We confirm the current board before suggesting anyone and look for tutors who have taught both where a move is involved."
  - q: "How much homework should my child do between Science sessions?"
    a: "Twenty to thirty minutes, and it should be specific rather than general. Exam questions on the topic just covered are worth more than re-reading notes, because Science marks are awarded for how an answer is written rather than for knowing the material. Where required practicals are the gap, short recall work on variables, apparatus and sources of error two or three times a week does more than one long session. Most tutors set the work at the end of the hour so it lines up with what was just taught."
