  - q: "How do we decide between Foundation and Higher?"
    a: "Look at where your child is working now and what they need next. If they're a secure 5 or above in Year 10, or need a 6 for A-Level Maths, Higher is usually right. If a 4 is the goal and number work still trips them up, Foundation lets them bank the marks without the hardest algebra. Schools tend to decide during Year 10 or early in Year 11, so a tutor's diagnostic before then gives you something solid to discuss with the Maths teacher."
  - q: "Should my child put more time into the calculator papers or the non-calculator one?"
    a: "There are three papers and all three count equally: one non-calculator and two calculator, so the calculator papers carry two-thirds of the grade between them. Even so, a lot of marks go early on the non-calculator paper, on fractions, negatives and mental methods. On the calculator papers, they tend to go on the long problem-solving questions at the back. The tutor checks which paper is bleeding more for your child and starts there."
