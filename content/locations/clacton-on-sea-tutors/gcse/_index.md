faq_1_q: "My child isn't at Clacton County High. Can they still apply to Sigma Sixth Tendring?"
faq_1_a: "Yes. Sigma Sixth Tendring welcomes students from any secondary school, with up to 250 places in Year 12. Year 11s at Clacton County High and the other Sigma Trust schools who meet the entry requirements are offered a place, and if the rest are oversubscribed, how well your child's GCSE grades suit their chosen courses counts for a lot. For A-Levels, that means six grade 5s, English and Maths included, plus a 6 in each subject. Check the current course guide, then the tutor works on whichever grade is furthest away."
faq_2_q: "My child sits Foundation Maths. Does that close any doors after Year 11?"
faq_2_a: "A few, so it's worth knowing early. Foundation tops out at a grade 5. That covers the Maths part of the general A-Level entry at Sigma Sixth Tendring, but not A-Level Maths itself, which asks for a 6, or Further Maths, which wants a 7. Vocational courses and the nursing T Level ask for a 4. If Higher looks like the better fit, a tutor can start on the algebra Foundation leaves out well before the school makes its final entries."
faq_3_q: "Is Year 10 too early to bring in a GCSE tutor?"
faq_3_a: "No, it's often the easiest time. In Year 10 there's room to go back over a topic from Year 8 or 9 without a mock looming, and a tutor can fix it before Year 11 piles new content on top. It also means the subject your child needs for their next course gets a full year of steady work rather than a scramble. Lessons in Year 10 tend to be about understanding. The exam technique comes later."
