  - q: "My child loves reading, so why is GCSE English such a struggle?"
    a: "Because reading well and writing exam answers are different skills. A keen reader often knows exactly what an extract means but hasn't been shown how to turn that into points about the writer's choices, with a quotation and a reason each time. Others never learnt to plan an essay, so the answer wanders. In the first lesson the tutor works out which it is for your child, and the lessons start from there."
  - q: "How is a GCSE English lesson with the tutor laid out?"
    a: "Most lessons run for an hour. The first part goes back over last week's practice answer, marked so your child can see what earned the marks. The middle half-hour is new technique on whatever is costing most, perhaps the comparison question or a Literature essay opening. Then your child writes something timed, and the tutor marks it with them before the lesson ends."
