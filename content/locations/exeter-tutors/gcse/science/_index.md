  - q: "Does it matter whether my child sits AQA, Edexcel or OCR Science?"
    a: "It matters at the top of the paper rather than at the bottom. The content overlaps heavily, so the science is the science whichever board sets it. What differs is the required practical list, how the long-answer questions are phrased, and how the calculation questions are laid out on the page. A student who has practised the wrong board's practicals is at a real disadvantage in one specific place. The board gets pinned down on the consultation call, and the match follows it."
  - q: "How much Science practice is worth setting between lessons?"
    a: "Sixty to ninety minutes, and split across the week rather than done in one block. The calculation work in Chemistry and Physics rewards short daily repetition, ten minutes at a time, in a way that a single long Sunday session does not. The long-answer practice is the opposite and needs one clear sitting with a timer. Most tutors set one of each. Beyond about ninety minutes a week the extra effort stops showing up in the marks."
