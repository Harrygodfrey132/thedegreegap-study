faq_1_q: "Does Exeter College want the same GCSE grades for every course?"
faq_1_a: "No. Entry grades vary from course to course, and some A-Level courses want a higher grade in that subject itself. So what your child needs depends on what they pick. The requirements can change from year to year too, which is why the college's current admissions page beats anything you've heard from another family. And if your child's one grade below, it's often a matter of twelve to sixteen weeks on the questions that cost them."
faq_2_q: "How do I work out which topics are costing my child marks?"
faq_2_a: "Start with where the lost marks sit on the paper, rather than the total at the top. Lots of crosses on the quick questions at the start usually mean some content hasn't stuck yet. If the start's fine and the marks go on six-mark questions and longer answers, chances are your child knows the material. It's how the answer's put together that's costing them, and that's the quicker fix. One subject slipping while the others hold steady? Then you've found the problem, and it's probably smaller than it looked."
