faq_1_q: "What grades does Exeter College want?"
faq_1_a: "It publishes requirements per course rather than one number across the board, and the more oversubscribed A-Levels ask for more in that subject itself. They are also revised between years. So the reliable version is the current admissions page for the specific course your child wants, not what was true two intakes ago. Where a student is one band under, that is usually twelve to sixteen weeks of focused work rather than a rebuild."
faq_2_q: "How do I know which topics are costing the marks?"
faq_2_a: "By where on the paper the marks go rather than by the total. Marks lost early on, across the short recall questions, point at content that never went in. Losses concentrated in the six-mark and extended questions mean the content is there but the answer is not built the way the scheme wants, which is far quicker to fix. Losses in one subject while the others hold up means you have found the problem and it is smaller than you feared."
