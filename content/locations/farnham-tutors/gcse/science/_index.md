  - q: "Does it matter which exam board my child's Science is on?"
    a: "Yes. On AQA, the experiments your child has to know are called required practicals, Edexcel calls them core practicals, and OCR groups them into practical activity groups, and each board asks about them in its own style. The long-answer questions are laid out differently too. Schools around Farnham choose their own boards, so we match each tutor to your child's. If you're not sure which board it is, your child's science teacher can tell you."
  - q: "Should my child sit Higher or Foundation in Science?"
    a: "The school decides, but it helps to know what's at stake. A Higher paper can earn anything from a 9 down to a 4, with a 3 for a near miss. Foundation tops out at a 5, or 5-5 on Combined Science. So a child getting 5s and 6s risks being capped on Foundation, while one scraping 3s may find Foundation gives them more questions they can actually start. A few lessons will show you which way your child is leaning, before you talk to their teacher."
