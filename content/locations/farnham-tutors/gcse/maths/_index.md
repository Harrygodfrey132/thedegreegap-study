  - q: "Which GCSE Maths paper should my child worry about most?"
    a: "Usually neither more than the other, since on AQA, Edexcel and OCR all three papers count equally. One is without a calculator and two are with, and Eduqas sets two papers instead. What changes is where the marks go. Without a calculator, they tend to go on slips in arithmetic and algebra in the first few questions. With one, they go on the long problems, especially when concentration fades near the end of the final paper. The crosses on your child's latest mock usually show which it is, and the tutor checks that in the first lesson."
