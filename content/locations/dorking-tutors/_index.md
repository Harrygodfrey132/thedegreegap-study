faq_2_q: "My child's at the Priory and their best friend's at the Ashcombe. Does the exam board really matter that much?"
faq_2_a: "It does, more than most parents expect. At GCSE the Ashcombe uses AQA for Maths and Combined Science, while the Priory uses Edexcel for Science and Edexcel or Eduqas for Maths. A lot of the content overlaps, but the wording of the longer questions and the way marks are shared out differ from board to board. Eduqas even sets two Maths papers instead of three. A tutor who knows your child's board can practise exactly the kind of question they'll meet in the hall."
faq_3_q: "My child is at RGS Surrey Hills and might take the IB Diploma. Can you help with that as well as A-Levels?"
faq_3_a: "Yes. We help with the IB Diploma as well as GCSEs and A-Levels, and RGS Surrey Hills is one of the few independent schools in Surrey to offer both. The IB is a different shape: six subjects, three at Higher Level and three at Standard, plus the Extended Essay and Theory of Knowledge. So we'd match by subject and level, and ask on the call which parts are worrying your child most. If they're still choosing between the two, Harry and Joe are happy to talk that through with you."
