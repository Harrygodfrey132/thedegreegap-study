  - q: "My child is doing Further Maths at the Ashcombe. Can a tutor help with Further Pure and Further Mechanics?"
    a: "Yes. The Ashcombe's guide says its Further Maths students take the Further Pure and Further Mechanics options on Edexcel, sitting an AS at the end of Year 12 and choosing whether to carry on. That's complex numbers, matrices and harder vectors on the Pure side, and collisions, momentum and work-energy problems on the Mechanics side. A tutor who knows those options can keep Further Maths and single Maths moving together, which matters, because a wobble in one usually shows up in the other."
  - q: "Does it matter which exam board my child's A-Level Maths is on?"
    a: "A little, mostly in how the papers are put together. Edexcel, AQA, OCR A and OCR MEI all teach the same core content, but the papers are put together differently. Edexcel, for one, sets a large data set for the statistics questions, and its specification says students must get to know it before the final exam, because questions assume they have. A tutor who knows your child's board practises with that data and with questions in the board's style."

You agree the length with the tutor, and you can change it later. We'll suggest what might suit your child on the free call."
