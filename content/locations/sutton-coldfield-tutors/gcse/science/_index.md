    a: "A common cause in Year 11 is that a revision method which used to work has stopped. Learning facts as separate items carries a student a long way, and then the questions start asking them to explain mechanisms, predict changes and evaluate methods, none of which a list of facts can answer. The other frequent cause is the required practicals, where a student who watched rather than did has nothing concrete to draw on. The first hour works out which of the two you are dealing with."
