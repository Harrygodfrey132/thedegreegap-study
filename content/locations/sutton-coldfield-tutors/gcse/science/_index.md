  - q: "Why is my child suddenly struggling with GCSE Science?"
    a: "Often it's because a way of revising that used to work has stopped working. Memorising got your child a long way. Now the questions want to know why things happen and what would change, and a list of facts can't answer that. The other common cause is the required practicals. If your child mostly watched while a lab partner did the practical, there's less to draw on in the exam. The tutor spots which of the two it is in the first lesson."
  - q: "How much Science homework should my child be doing between lessons?"
    a: "Twenty to thirty minutes, and it should be exam questions, not reading or flashcards. Recall apps build the feeling of knowing, which doesn't carry over to the longer questions. If practicals are the gap, a few short bursts a week on variables, equipment and sources of error do more than one long session. The tutor sets the work at the end of each lesson."
