Mechanics questions that describe a situation in words are the other big sticking point, for the same reason."
  - q: "How can I see where my child's Maths marks are slipping away?"
    a: "Look at which questions they started, not just which were wrong. A blank one means your child couldn't find a way in: a choosing problem, and a fixable one.
