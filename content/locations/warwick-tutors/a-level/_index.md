faq_1_q: "People keep saying 'super-curricular'. What is it, and does it really matter?"
faq_1_a: "It's anything your child does with their subject beyond what the A-Level asks: reading, a lecture, a project, a competition or an olympiad. For courses that turn most applicants away, it matters a lot, because it's the only real sign your child wants that subject rather than just a degree. For most courses it counts for far less than the grades. So it's worth being honest about which kind of course your child is aiming for."
