faq_1_q: "What counts as super-curricular, and does it really matter?"
faq_1_a: "It means engaging with the subject beyond what the syllabus requires: reading, lectures, a project, a competition, an olympiad. For the courses that reject most applicants it matters a good deal, because it is the only evidence that a student wants this subject rather than wants a degree. For most courses it matters much less than the grades. Worth being honest about which category the course your child wants falls into."
