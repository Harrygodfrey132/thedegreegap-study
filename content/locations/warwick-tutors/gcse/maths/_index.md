  - q: "How can I tell which GCSE Maths topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Get a marked paper and read the question numbers rather than the total. Papers run roughly in order of difficulty, so losses bunched at the end point to specific topics like algebra, circle theorems or the multi-step problems, while losses spread evenly usually mean accuracy or pace. Blank questions and abandoned working say different things too: a blank means no starting point, while working that stops means the method ran out partway. Five minutes of that beats a term of reports."
