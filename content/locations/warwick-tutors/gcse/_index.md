faq_1_q: "He has revised for months and the grade has not moved. What are we missing?"
faq_1_a: "Almost always what the hours are being spent on. Re-reading a topic already understood feels productive and changes very little, and it is where most students default because it is the comfortable part. A tutor marks a recent paper, finds the three or four question types genuinely losing marks, and puts the sessions there. The total hours often go down rather than up, which is usually a relief to everybody."
faq_2_q: "Is Year 10 or Year 11 the better time to start?"
faq_2_a: "Year 10 when you have the choice. Content steps up that year and the gaps that open are small enough to close quickly if somebody notices them. Left alone they compound for twelve months and then arrive all at once in a mock. Year 11 still works and plenty of families start there, the plan just narrows to exam technique and past papers rather than proper rebuilding."
faq_3_q: "Do you match on the exam board?"
faq_3_a: "Yes, and it is the first thing we confirm rather than a detail settled later.
