  - q: "How can I tell which parts of GCSE English my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Read a marked script and look at what the teacher has annotated rather than the grade. Comments asking for more analysis point at one assessment objective; comments asking for better evidence point at another; a note that context feels bolted on points at a third. If the final answer is thin or unfinished, the problem is pacing rather than any objective. Those four patterns cover most scripts, and each needs a genuinely different term of work, which is why the diagnosis comes first."
