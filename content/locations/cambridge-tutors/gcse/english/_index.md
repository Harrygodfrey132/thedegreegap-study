  - q: "Does it matter if my child's on AQA, Edexcel or OCR for English?"
    a: "Yes, and in Cambridge it matters more than usual, because iGCSE is in the mix too. The core skills barely change from board to board. What changes is the shape of the papers: which question carries the evaluation marks, how the unseen texts are tested, and how much of the grade rests on your child's own writing. A tutor working to the wrong paper can teach good English and still leave marks behind. So we check the board and texts with you on the call before suggesting anyone."
  - q: "How much should my child write between English lessons?"
    a: "About an hour to an hour and a half a week, and in English that usually means one timed piece, not an evening of reading notes. Writing is what gets marked, so writing is what needs practice. One essay done in exam conditions and brought to the next lesson beats three hours of highlighting. Beyond an hour and a half the gains drop off fast. And for a Year 11 with nine subjects on the go, more isn't realistic anyway."
