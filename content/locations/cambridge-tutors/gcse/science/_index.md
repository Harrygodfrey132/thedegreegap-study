  - q: "Foundation or higher tier, triple or combined: which matters more for my child?"
    a: "In Cambridge, triple or combined usually matters more. Higher tier goes up to grade 9 and starts around a 4, while foundation stops at 5, and in Science the school often settles that for you. Combined Science counts as two GCSEs and covers all three subjects in less depth. Triple is three separate GCSEs, and it's sometimes named in sixth-form Science requirements. So if your child's in Year 9 and might take a Science at A-Level, that choice deserves a proper conversation now, not in Year 11."
  - q: "How do I work out where my child is losing marks in GCSE Science?"
    a: "Look at where on the paper the marks go, not the total. If they're lost on the short recall questions at the front, the content hasn't gone in yet. If they're lost on the six-mark questions and the data questions, your child knows the science but isn't building the answer the way the mark scheme wants. And if one subject's paper is dropping marks while the other two hold up, you've found the problem. It's probably a third the size you feared."
