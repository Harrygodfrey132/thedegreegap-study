  - q: "Should my child take foundation or higher tier GCSE Science, and does Triple or Combined matter more?"
    a: "Higher tier caps at grade 9 and floors around a 4, while foundation caps at 5. For Science the tier question often resolves itself, and the one that matters more in Cambridge is Triple versus Combined. Combined Science is worth two GCSEs and covers all three subjects at less depth. Triple is three separate GCSEs and is sometimes named in sixth-form Science requirements. If your child is in Year 9 and Sciences are a possible A-Level, that decision deserves a proper conversation now rather than in Year 11."
  - q: "How can I tell which GCSE Science topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "The pattern shows in where on the paper the marks go, not in the total. Marks lost across the short recall questions at the front mean content has not been learned. Marks lost on the six-mark questions and the data-handling items mean the content is there but the answer is not being built the way the scheme wants it. Marks lost across one subject's paper while the other two hold up means you have found the problem, and it is a third the size you feared."
