faqs:
  - q: "What is the hardest part of GCSE Science for Guildford students?"
    a: "Three things, and they are the same across the Guildford schools because they are the same across the specifications. Moles and concentration calculations in Chemistry, where a student who understands the chemistry can still lose every mark on the arithmetic. Rearranging equations in Physics, which is really a Maths gap appearing in a Science lesson. And the required practicals across all three, which are examined as recall of method, variables and sources of error rather than as something you did in a lab a year ago. All three respond well to targeted work because they are narrow."
  - q: "Does the exam board make a difference in GCSE Science?"
    a: "Yes, more than in most subjects. AQA, Edexcel and OCR cover overlapping content but differ in how the required practicals are examined, how the longer six-mark questions are marked and which formulae are given rather than learned. The practical questions in particular are worth serious preparation and the shape of them is board-specific. We confirm the board before suggesting a tutor, and where a student has moved school mid-course we look for someone who has taught the subject on both boards involved."
  - q: "How long is a GCSE Science tutoring session?"
    a: "An hour, weekly, which for most students is the right amount. Science rewards frequency over length: an hour every week for a term does considerably more than three hours crammed into a Sunday before a mock, because the practice between sessions is where the method sticks. Some families add a second hour in the run-up to mocks or in the spring of Year 11, and where a student is covering all three sciences we sometimes suggest alternating focus week to week rather than trying to cover everything every session."
