faqs:
  - q: "What is the hardest topic in GCSE Maths for Norwich students?"
    a: "Algebra, treated not as one topic but as the thread through everything else. Rearranging equations, algebraic fractions and simultaneous equations appear inside geometry questions, ratio questions and the wordy problems at the end of the paper, so weakness there costs marks in places that do not look like algebra. Circle theorems and iterative methods come next, both of them method rather than understanding. A fourth, less discussed, is simply not knowing which formulae are given, which quietly costs time and accuracy on every paper."
  - q: "What is the difference between AQA, Edexcel and OCR GCSE Maths, and does it matter for tutoring?"
    a: "It matters more than most parents expect. The core content is close to identical, but the boards differ in how questions are worded, how multi-step problems are built, and which formulae are printed in the paper rather than memorised. A student prepared on one board's past papers can be thrown by another's phrasing while knowing exactly the same maths. We confirm the board on the consultation call before suggesting anyone, and tutors are matched to it, because familiarity with a board's habits is worth a surprising amount in the last two terms."

The last fifteen minutes are exam questions on that topic, marked against the real scheme in front of your child. The next week's work is set at the end so it follows directly."
