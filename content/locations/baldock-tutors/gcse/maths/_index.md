    a: "Worry about whichever one is losing more marks, because all three papers count the same. There's one non-calculator paper and two calculator papers. Without a calculator, fluent arithmetic and confident algebra matter most, so a child with an older gap usually struggles there, because a shaky method shows up straight away. The calculator papers reward accuracy across long, multi-step problems and careful reading of the wordy questions near the end. Nearly every child has one kind that's clearly worse. The first lesson finds which, so the early weeks go where the marks are."
