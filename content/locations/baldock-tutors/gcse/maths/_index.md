    a: "Both count the same, so the question is where more marks are going. The non-calculator paper rewards arithmetic fluency and confident algebra, and a student carrying an older gap usually struggles there because a shaky method has nowhere to hide. The calculator papers reward accuracy across multi-step problems and careful reading of the wordy questions at the back. Almost every student is clearly weaker on one of the two, and the first session identifies which, so the early weeks are spent where the marks actually are rather than spread evenly."
