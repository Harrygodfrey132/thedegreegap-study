  - q: "Why is my child struggling with GCSE English?"
    a: "Usually because writing about a text is a different skill from understanding it, and in a busy lesson there's rarely time to practise it one-to-one. Your child can know a book well and still lose marks on the essay, because the exam rewards an argument, not a description. The telltale sign is a teenager who talks well about a text and then writes a retelling of it. In Literature the other common cause is evidence: quotations learnt because they're famous, not because they can be argued with. Both are quicker to fix than a reading problem."
