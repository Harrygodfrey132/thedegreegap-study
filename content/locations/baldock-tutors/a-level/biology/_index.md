  - q: "Isn't the practical side of A-Level Biology marked separately?"
    a: "Partly, which is where the mix-up comes from. The practical endorsement, a separate pass for lab skills that your child's teachers assess, is reported on its own and doesn't change the grade. But the written papers ask about the practicals as well, and those marks count in full. That's what catches families out. Your child could pass the endorsement easily and still drop marks on a question about keeping the temperature steady in an enzyme experiment. So the tutor treats the practicals as exam content, not a box already ticked."
