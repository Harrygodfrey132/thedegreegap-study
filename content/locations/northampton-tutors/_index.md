faq_1_q: "What platform do the online sessions run on?"
faq_1_a: "Lessonspace. It is a shared interactive whiteboard built for one-to-one work, so the tutor and your child write on the same page rather than one of them talking at the other. faq_2_q: "Can you help a student who has fallen a long way behind?"
faq_2_a: "Yes, and it is a fair chunk of what we do. Some students come to us carrying gaps that go back a year or two, often from a period of poor attendance or a run of cover lessons. We do not pick up where school left off, because that is the part that is not working. The tutor works backwards through wrong answers until the understanding runs out, then rebuilds forward from there while school carries on. The two meet somewhere in the middle of the term."
faq_3_q: "Can a tutor help with getting into a Northampton sixth form or college?"
faq_3_a: "Yes, though the first job is reading what the place actually asks for. Sixth forms at Northampton School for Boys, Northampton School for Girls, Thomas Becket Catholic School and Weston Favell Academy each publish their own entry requirements, and so do Northampton College and Moulton College. Those get revised between years, so work from this year's published list rather than what an older sibling remembers. Once we know the target grades, the plan aims at them rather than at the subject in general."
