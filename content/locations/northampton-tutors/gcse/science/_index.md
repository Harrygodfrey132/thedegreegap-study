  - q: "Should my child take foundation or higher tier GCSE Science?"
    a: "It depends on where they are now, not where anyone hopes they will end up. Higher tier reaches the top grades and scores very little below its floor. Foundation caps lower and puts far more of the available marks genuinely within reach, which for a lot of students means a better grade rather than a worse one. Northampton schools often review the entry during Year 10, so a Science diagnostic then turns it into a decision made on evidence rather than a hunch. School should be part of that conversation."
  - q: "Can a Science tutor help a child with dyslexia, ADHD or processing differences?"
    a: "Yes, and several tutors do this regularly. The practical work is about lowering the load: one consistent layout for calculations so multi-step working does not get lost, six-mark answers broken into named stages the student can hold, and a single method drilled until it is automatic rather than three alternatives offered. Timed practice comes in gradually where exam conditions are the difficulty. Tell us on the call what school already has in place, including any access arrangements."
  - q: "What if my child does not want a tutor?"

The free 15-minute video call lets your child form their own view first and turn down a particular tutor without turning down the idea. If after a session or two they still do not want it, we pause. Their call."
