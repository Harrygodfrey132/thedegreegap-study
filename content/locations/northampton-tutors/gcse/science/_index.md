  - q: "Should my child sit the foundation or the higher Science papers?"
    a: "It depends on where your child is now, not where everyone hopes they'll end up. Higher papers go right up to the top grades, but a child who falls short of the lowest grade on them can end up with no grade at all. Foundation papers stop at a 5, and far more of the marks are within reach, which for plenty of children means a better grade, not a worse one. Northampton schools often review entries during Year 10, so a tutor's close look at a recent paper then lets you decide on evidence rather than a hunch. Keep school in that conversation too."
