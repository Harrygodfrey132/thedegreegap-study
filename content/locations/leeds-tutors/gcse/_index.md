faq_2_q: "My daughter's switched off from Maths completely. What would a tutor actually do?"
faq_2_a: "First, find out where the marks are going. In the first lesson the tutor goes through a recent paper with her. The trouble's usually in rearranging algebra, geometry or the long problem-solving questions near the end. Then they start on the oldest gap, even if her class moved on from it months ago."
