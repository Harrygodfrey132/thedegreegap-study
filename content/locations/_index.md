## How local tutoring works

All our lessons are online, so where you live never limits who can teach your child. What a town page adds is local knowledge: the schools we know in the area, and the GCSE and A-Level subject pages for that town.

Lessons are one to one on a shared online whiteboard, and every lesson is recorded, so your child can watch a method again before the exam. It also means we match tutors on what matters, the exam board and the papers your child will sit, rather than on who happens to live nearest.

If your town isn't listed, we can still help. Every family talks to Joe or Harry on a [free call](/book-a-call/) before we suggest a tutor.
