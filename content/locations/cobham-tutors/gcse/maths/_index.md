  - q: "Which GCSE Maths paper should we worry about most, calculator or non-calculator?"
    a: "Usually the non-calculator one, at least to begin with. On AQA, Edexcel and OCR there are three papers and all three count equally, so the two calculator papers make up two-thirds of the grade between them. But shaky fractions, negatives and algebra show up first on the non-calculator paper, with nothing to fall back on. On the calculator papers it's more about longer problems and getting every step accurate. Eduqas works differently, with two papers. Your child's mock results, paper by paper, usually show which one is costing more."
