  - q: "Higher or foundation tier for Science: how do we decide?"
    a: "It depends on where your child is now and what they want to do next. Higher tier is aimed at grades 4 to 9, while foundation tops out at a 5. If your child hopes to take a science at A-Level, higher's almost always needed, since those courses ask for a 6. For a child hovering around a 3 or 4, foundation can be the safer entry. Schools often review tier entries during Year 10, and a tutor's look at a recent paper around then gives you evidence for that conversation."
