  - q: "Should my child sit Foundation or Higher tier GCSE Science?"
    a: "It depends on where they are sitting now and what they want next. Higher tier runs from grade 4 up to 9, while Foundation tops out at a 5. For any student hoping to take a science at A-Level, Higher is almost always needed, since those courses ask for a 6. A student hovering at 3 or 4 may be better protected on Foundation. Tier entries are often reviewed during Year 10, and a diagnostic around then gives you evidence for that conversation with school."
  - q: "Is Year 10 too early to start GCSE Science tutoring?"

It also leaves room to settle triple versus combined or the tier question before it is fixed."
