faq_1_q: "Does a partner-school place at Reigate College mean my child can take any course?"
faq_1_a: "No, and it's worth getting clear on early. If your child's at a partner school and their reports show strong effort, attendance and punctuality, they'll be offered a place. But they still need five GCSEs at grade 4 or above, plus the minimum grades for each course they choose. Those vary. Maths, Physics, Chemistry and Biology all ask for a grade 6 in GCSE Maths, for example, and Further Maths asks for a 7. Check this year's course pages, though, as requirements can change from one intake to the next."
