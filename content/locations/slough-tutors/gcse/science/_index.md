faqs:
  - q: "What is the hardest topic in GCSE Science for Slough students?"
    a: "Not a topic so much as a type of question: the ones that turn a science into Maths. Moles and concentration calculations in Chemistry, rearranging and unit conversion in Physics, the magnification and percentage change questions in Biology. Students who revise well still drop these, because a revision guide teaches the fact and the paper asks for the working. Close behind are the six-mark extended answers and the required practical questions, where the method has to be described the way it was done, not the way it was remembered. Recall is rarely the problem."
  - q: "Should my child take foundation or higher tier GCSE Science?"
    a: "It depends on where your child is now, not where anyone hopes they will end up, and in combined Science it is one decision for all six papers. Higher reaches a 9-9 but scores little below its floor; foundation stops at a 5-5 and makes far more of the marks reachable. A student near the boundary in Year 10 is often right on higher with focused work on the calculation questions that decide it. A Year 10 diagnostic on a real paper turns that into a decision rather than a guess, and school should be part of the conversation."

Sessions are recorded, so nothing depends on notes taken under pressure. Tell us on the call what school already has in place, including any exam access arrangements, and we match on that first."
