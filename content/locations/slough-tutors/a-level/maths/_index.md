  - q: "How much homework should my child do between Maths lessons?"
    a: "Roughly an hour to 90 minutes a week, set by the tutor and marked before the next lesson. Usually that's two or three exam questions on the lesson's topic, done against the clock, plus one question from an older topic so nothing quietly slips away. Much more than that and you tend to get tired working rather than better working. The marking is what counts. A wrong answer nobody checks teaches the wrong method twice."
  - q: "Does my child need a Maths tutor, or would more revision do?"
    a: "It depends on the working on a recent paper, more than the score. If the working's clean but runs out of marks near the end of a question, that's exam technique, and a tutor's the quickest fix. Blank space or crossed-out starts on the longer questions mean your child isn't sure which method to use. More revision rarely helps there, because they don't know what to revise. If they're already scoring well across Pure, Mechanics and Statistics, they probably need practice papers and someone to mark them, not weekly lessons. We'll say so if that's the case."
