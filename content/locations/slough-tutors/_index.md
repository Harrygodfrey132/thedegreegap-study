faq_4_q: "How do I know whether my child needs a tutor or just a quieter term?"
faq_4_a: "Look at the last marked paper rather than the report. If the effort is there and the marks are going missing in the same few places every time, that is a gap a tutor can name inside an hour, and it is rarely the topic the class is on this week. If the marks are fine and the worry is really about the next school, say so on the call. We would rather tell you tutoring is not what your child needs than sell you a term of it."
