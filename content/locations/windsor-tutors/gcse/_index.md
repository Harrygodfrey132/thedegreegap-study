faq_3_q: "Should we start in Year 10, or wait and see how the Year 11 mocks go?"

Waiting for the mocks isn't wrong, but by then most of the course has been taught and sixth form applications are close. Some families start with one lesson a week in Year 10 and add a second only in the weeks before the exams."
