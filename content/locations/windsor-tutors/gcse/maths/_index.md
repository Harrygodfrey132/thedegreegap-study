  - q: "Which GCSE Maths paper should my child worry about most, calculator or non-calculator?"
    a: "Neither on its own. There are three papers and they all count equally, one without a calculator and two with, so the calculator papers carry two-thirds of the marks between them. The non-calculator paper catches arithmetic and algebra slips early on. The last calculator paper (Paper 3 on Edexcel and AQA) tends to cost marks near the end, when the problems get longer and time runs short. In the first lesson the tutor works out which one is costing your child more."
  - q: "How do I work out which Maths topics my child keeps losing marks on?"
    a: "Look at where the marks disappear on a recent mock. Higher papers start with shorter questions and build towards long, multi-step ones. If your child scores well early and then fades, it's usually technique and stamina rather than missing topics. If the dropped marks are scattered, there's probably a gap in one or two areas, often algebra. Bring the paper to the call, or share it with the tutor, and they'll map it out in the first lesson."
