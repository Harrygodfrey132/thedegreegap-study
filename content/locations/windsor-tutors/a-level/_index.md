faq_2_q: "When are predicted grades decided, and can tutoring still change them?"
faq_2_a: "Each school does it a little differently, but predictions usually lean on the end-of-Year 12 exams and work early in Year 13, before UCAS forms go off in the autumn. Tutoring doesn't change a prediction directly. It changes the work your child's teachers see: timed papers, marked essays, the mock. That's why Year 12 is the best time to start. A low mark in the summer exams is far easier to recover from with a few months of steady lessons behind it."
