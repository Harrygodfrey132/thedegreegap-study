  - q: "How do I know if my child needs a Chemistry tutor or just more revision?"
    a: "Ask them to explain why a mechanism goes the way it does, rather than to reproduce it. A student who can justify it understands the material and probably needs practice volume. A student who can draw it perfectly and cannot say why has memorised a picture, and no amount of further revision converts that into understanding, because they will keep rehearsing the same surface. The second case is exactly what one-to-one time is for."
