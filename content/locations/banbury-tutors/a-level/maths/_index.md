faqs:
  - q: "What is the difference between AQA, Edexcel and OCR A-Level Maths, and does it matter?"
    a: "It matters, and in ways that are easy to miss. The core mathematics is close to identical, but the boards differ in how questions are worded, how much working must be shown for method marks, which results appear in the formula booklet and how the large data set is used in Statistics. A student drilled on one board's past papers can lose marks on another's conventions while doing the same mathematics. We confirm the specification before proposing anyone."
  - q: "Can the same tutor help with Maths and Further Maths?"
    a: "Usually yes, and where a student takes both it is often the better arrangement, because the two interleave and a single tutor can sequence them sensibly rather than duplicating. It does narrow the pool, since not every A-Level Maths tutor is comfortable at Further Maths level. Tell us on the consultation call if Further Maths is part of the picture, because it changes who we shortlist rather than just what gets covered."

  - q: "What is the hardest part of A-Level Maths for Banbury students?"
