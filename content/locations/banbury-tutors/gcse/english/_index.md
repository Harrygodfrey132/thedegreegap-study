  - q: "Would it help to start English tutoring as early as Year 10?"
    a: "Yes, Year 10 is a good time to start, above all when the problem is how much your child writes. Producing several full answers in one sitting is about stamina as much as knowledge. That kind of habit builds through months of repetition, not a burst of effort just before the exams. Starting early also means the mocks aren't the first time your child has written answer after answer against the clock. And there's less pressure in Year 10, which suits a child who freezes when rushed."
  - q: "Why does my child struggle with GCSE English when they clearly understand it?"
    a: "There are two usual causes, and they need different work. The first is that writing has never been taught as its own skill, separate from reading. So a child who understands a text describes it instead of arguing about it. The second gets talked about less. Your child simply isn't getting enough words down in the time, usually because each sentence is being edited before it's written. The first lesson looks at a real timed answer, and it's usually obvious which of the two you're dealing with."
