  - q: "My child is in Year 10, is it too early to start GCSE English tutoring?"
    a: "Year 10 is the better starting point for English, above all where writing volume is the issue. Producing several developed answers in one sitting is an endurance habit as much as an academic skill, and habits build through repetition across months rather than intensity in the spring of Year 11. Starting in Year 10 means arriving at mocks with a paragraph structure that is automatic rather than still being thought about, and the sessions are calmer because nothing is imminent."
  - q: "Why is my child struggling with GCSE English?"
    a: "There are two common causes and they need different work. The first is that the writing has never been taught as a skill separate from the reading, so a student who understands a text writes a description of it rather than an argument. The second, less discussed, is simply not producing enough words in the time, usually because every sentence is being edited before it is written. The first hour looks at a real timed answer, which makes it obvious which of the two you are dealing with."
