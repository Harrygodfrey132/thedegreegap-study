  - q: "Should my child sit Higher or Foundation?"
    a: "It comes down to your child's marks now, on real papers. Higher reaches the top grades, but there's little to fall back on if a paper goes badly. Foundation tops out at a 5, and much more of that paper is within your child's reach. If your child's only just short of where Higher needs them in Year 10, staying on it and working on the harder questions is often right. Well below it, Foundation usually suits them better, and their confidence tends to come back once the questions feel winnable. The school makes the final call, and a marked paper in Year 10 turns it from a guess into a decision."
