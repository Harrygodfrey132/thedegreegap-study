  - q: "How do I work out which Maths topics my child's losing marks on?"
    a: "Look at where the marks went on the mock, not the total. Marks dropped early point to a gap in the basics, and that usually takes a term. Marks dropped only at the back, with the rest clean, are exam technique, and they'll move in weeks. And if the answers are right but the marks still aren't there? That's the pattern we see most in Kingston upon Thames. The working never made it onto the page."
