faqs:
  - q: "What is the hardest part of GCSE English for Bradford students?"
    a: "Two things, and they sit on different papers. On Language Paper 1 it is the evaluation question, where a student is asked how far they agree with a statement about the text and writes a description instead of an argument. On Literature it is the shift from knowing a text to building a case about it under timed conditions, which is a writing skill rather than a reading one. Add the Paper 2 comparison of viewpoints and you have most of the lost marks in one place."
  - q: "How many sessions does it usually take to move a GCSE English grade?"
    a: "Around eight to twelve weekly hours for a grade band, provided something is being written between sessions. It goes faster when the problem is technique, because a student who already reads well can learn what a paragraph has to contain in a few weeks and then apply it everywhere. It goes slower when the writing itself needs building, since that is a habit rather than a rule. The first session is a diagnostic and tells us honestly which of the two we are looking at."
  - q: "Does it matter which exam board my child's English is on?"
    a: "Yes, more than most parents expect. The boards word their questions differently, weight the reading and writing sections differently and set different anthologies for Literature, so a tutor working from the wrong one will be teaching a question style your child never sees. Schools across Bradford do not all sit the same board. We confirm it on the consultation call along with the set texts, then match on both rather than on English in general."
