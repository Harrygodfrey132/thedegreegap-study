  - q: "Is the GCSE Science standard in Bradford similar to Leeds or Harrogate?"

Results across the Bradford district do sit below the national picture, which tells you something about class sizes and pace and almost nothing about one child.   - q: "How do I know whether my child needs a Science tutor or just more revision?"
    a: "Look at a marked paper rather than the grade. Clean, sensible working that still loses marks at the back of the paper points at technique, and a tutor fixes that quickly. Blank space or crossed-out attempts on the multi-step calculation questions points at a method gap, and that needs teaching rather than revising. If the recall questions are fine and the six-mark answers are short, the problem is structure. More revision alone rarely touches any of those, because nobody is watching how the answer gets built."
  - q: "Can the same tutor cover GCSE Science and Maths?"
    a: "Some do, and where the trouble is the calculation side of Physics or Chemistry that pairing is genuinely useful, because one person sees the same weakness surface in two places. Most tutors specialise, though. A student who needs Biology recall rebuilt or English essays shaped is better off with a separate specialist than with a generalist covering both. Tell us on the consultation call what the pattern looks like across subjects and we will suggest accordingly rather than defaulting to one tutor."
  - q: "Can a Science tutor work with a child who has dyslexia, ADHD or processing differences?"

The whiteboard helps, because everything stays on screen and the session is recorded. Tell us at the consultation what school already has in place, including any exam access arrangements."
