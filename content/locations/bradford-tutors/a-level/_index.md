faq_1_q: "He coasted through Year 12 and Year 13 has gone sideways. What happened?"
faq_1_a: "Usually Year 12 got passed rather than learned. A fortnight of cramming before each assessment produces a reasonable grade and very little that lasts, and A-Level is built so the second year leans directly on the first. The repair is going back for the specific Year 12 topics this term's work depends on, which is faster than it sounds because the material is familiar even if it never stuck. That is what the first session is looking for, working backwards from a marked script."
faq_2_q: "How does tutoring change a predicted grade before UCAS?"
faq_2_a: "Not directly, and anyone who says otherwise is selling something. Predictions come from mock performance and a teacher's judgement, so the only honest route is lifting the work the judgement rests on. That means the autumn of Year 13 is the window, and Year 12 is better still. Tutors focus on the papers those mocks are drawn from and on the marks a student is leaving behind rather than the content they have not met. Then the conversation with school has evidence in it."
faq_3_q: "Is an apprenticeship application something a tutor can help with?"
faq_3_a: "Up to a point, and it is worth planning for early. Schemes at Bradford employers publish their own entry requirements and change them between intakes, and the deadlines often land before the UCAS one, so the autumn of Year 13 carries two pieces of work rather than one. Tutors help with the grades those schemes screen on and with the written application where that fits the match. Say on the consultation call if both routes are live, because it changes the order we work in."
faq_4_q: "Is it too late to start in Year 13?"
faq_4_a: "No, though the plan looks different. In Year 12 there is room to rebuild study habits and teach content properly. From Year 13 the work is narrower: find which earlier topics the current ones depend on, repair those, and drill exam technique alongside. Plenty of students move a full grade from a Year 13 start. What matters more is being underway before Christmas, because that is when the prediction gets written and the offers follow it."
