  - q: "Year 12 or Year 13, when should A-Level Maths tutoring start in Bradford?"
    a: "Year 12, and the gap between the two is wider than families expect. A Year 13 start still moves the final grade and plenty of students do it, but by then the offers have usually been made against a number set earlier."
  - q: "What actually happens in a typical A-Level Maths session?"
    a: "An hour in three parts. The first fifteen minutes go on last week's work, marked live, with the misconceptions pulled out rather than corrected silently. The middle half hour is the topic bleeding the most marks, taught with the working written on screen. The last fifteen minutes are exam questions on that topic against the mark scheme, so the student sees where the method marks sit. Homework is set from there, usually an hour's worth, and it comes back marked the following week."
