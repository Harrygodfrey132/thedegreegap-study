  - q: "Which Maths paper is costing my child more, calculator or non-calculator?"
    a: "It's usually one more than the other, and the first lesson finds out which. There are three papers and they all count the same. One's done without a calculator, the other two with. The non-calculator paper rewards quick arithmetic and confident algebra, and it's where an older gap tends to cost the most. The calculator papers reward accuracy over several steps, and careful reading of the wordy questions near the back. It's rare for a child to be equally strong on both. Once the tutor knows which, the early weeks go where the marks are."
  - q: "How can we tell whether Higher or Foundation is right for my child?"
    a: "Go by where your child is in Year 10, not where everyone hopes they'll finish. Higher runs from a 9 down to a 4, with a 3 allowed for a near miss and nothing below it. Foundation stops at a 5, but far more of its questions are within reach. On a 5 or 6 in Year 10 mocks, Higher with focused work is usually right. On a 3 or 4, Foundation's often safer, and confidence builds as your child starts getting questions right. School makes the final entry, usually in Year 11. A tutor's look at a Year 10 paper gives you real evidence to take to them."
