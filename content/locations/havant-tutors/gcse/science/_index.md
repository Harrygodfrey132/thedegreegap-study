  - q: "Does it matter whether my child's on AQA, Edexcel or OCR for Science?"
    a: "Yes, and more in Science than in most subjects. The content overlaps a lot, but the exams don't. The boards differ in how the practicals are examined, how the long answers are marked and how the topics are shared out between papers. So a child who learnt a practical one board's way can meet it framed another way and drop marks on something they know. We check the board on the free call. And if your child has moved schools mid-course, we'll look for a tutor who's taught both."
  - q: "Why is my child finding GCSE Science so hard?"
    a: "Usually it's one of two things, and each needs a different plan. The first is a writing problem. Your child knows the Science, but the long answers score badly because the points are listed, not linked, or because they've described when the question asked them to explain. The second goes further back: a skill like rearranging an equation that never quite landed and now turns up in every paper. The tutor spends the first hour working out which it is, because the next few weeks look completely different depending on the answer."
