  - q: "What is the difference between AQA, Edexcel and OCR GCSE Science, and does it matter for tutoring?"
    a: "It matters, and in Science more than anywhere. Content overlaps heavily while examining does not: the boards assess the required practicals differently, mark six-mark answers to different structures, and print different amounts of equation support in the paper itself. A student who met a practical under one board's approach can face it framed another way and lose marks on material they genuinely know. We confirm the board on the consultation call, and where a student changed schools mid-course we look for a tutor who has taught both."
  - q: "My child is in Year 10, is it too early to start GCSE Science tutoring?"

Year 10 work is also calmer: rebuilding method rather than drilling papers, which suits a student who has begun to decide Science is not for them.
