faq_1_q: "What GCSE grades will my child need for a Bicester sixth form?"
faq_1_a: "It varies, so each guide is worth reading closely. The Cooper School's current entry rules ask for five GCSEs at grade 5 or above, with at least a 4 in English and Maths. On top of that, it wants a 6 in the subjects that relate to what your child plans to study. The Bicester School asks for a 4 in English Language and Maths for every course, and each department adds its own requirement. Heyford Park School sets its own rules too. All of these can change from year to year, so check this year's figures for any sixth form on your list."
faq_2_q: "What if my child misses the grade they need in English or Maths?"
faq_2_a: "There's a second chance sooner than you might think. English Language and Maths are the only GCSEs your child can resit in November, so a near miss in the summer doesn't mean waiting a whole year. A tutor can use September and October to work on exactly what cost the marks. Often that's a handful of question types rather than the whole course. Sixth forms and colleges each have their own rules about fitting a resit in, so it's worth asking yours."
faq_3_q: "Is Year 9 too early to think about a GCSE tutor?"
faq_3_a: "Not if something's already wobbling. Plenty of schools start GCSE work in Year 9, and it's the calmest time to fix an old gap, because nothing's riding on it yet. If you've just moved to Bicester, it's also a good moment to check nothing from your child's old school slipped through in the move. Lots of families start in Year 10 or the autumn of Year 11 instead, and that works well too. As the exams get closer, lessons simply lean more on past papers."
faq_4_q: "Will the tutor follow what my child's doing in class, or go back over old topics?"
faq_4_a: "Usually a bit of both, and the balance shifts as the gaps close. Early on, more of each hour goes on topics your child missed or never quite got, because this term's work leans on them. Once those feel steady, lessons follow the class more closely, with exam questions on whatever's just been taught. You'll get a short note after every lesson, so you'll always know which it was that week. If you'd like more of one or the other, just tell us."
