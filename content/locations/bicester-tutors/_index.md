faq_1_q: "My child is joining a Bicester school partway through their GCSEs. Can a tutor help them catch up?"
faq_1_a: "Yes, and it's one of the most useful things a tutor can do. Schools don't all teach GCSE topics in the same order, and they don't always use the same exam board. So a move in Year 9 or 10 can leave a few holes and a few repeats. The tutor works out which topics are missing, then teaches them in a sensible order while your child keeps up with this term's lessons. Old exercise books and reports are worth digging out before the call."
