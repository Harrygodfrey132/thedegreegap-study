  - q: "Should my child do combined or triple Science?"
    a: "It depends on how secure your child is across all three. Combined Science is worth two GCSEs, while triple gives three, one for each science, with more content in each. Triple isn't automatically the better choice. It suits children who are comfortable in all three sciences and heading for Science A-Levels, and it can stretch a child who's shaky in one of them very thin. If your child is borderline, a look at their Year 10 work shows whether the weak science is a technique gap or a content gap, before the entry is fixed."
  - q: "How can I tell where my child is losing Science marks?"
    a: "Take a recent mock and read it by type of question rather than by topic. If the six-mark answers are losing marks while the short recall questions are clean, that's structure, not knowledge. If the practical questions are the weak spot, it's method. If the number questions go wrong after a sensible start, it's usually unit conversion or rearranging. Three different problems, three different plans. And you can often spot which one it is in about twenty minutes."
  - q: "What kind of progress do your Science tutors usually see?"
    a: "Where the gap is exam technique, about one grade band over a term of weekly lessons is a realistic aim, and it's the one we most often work towards. In Science, technique means six-mark structure, practical method and accurate calculations. Where content is missing from Year 9 or 10, it's slower and usually takes two terms. We'd rather tell you what's typical than promise anything. After the first lesson, you'll know which of the two it is."
  - q: "Are holiday lessons worth it for GCSE Science?"
    a: "Yes, and Science is one of the subjects where a holiday block really earns its keep. Half-term and Easter give enough clear time to work through a whole topic properly, which is hard in one weekly hour during term. Lots of Year 11 families keep a weekly lesson going all year and add a short, focused block at Easter on whichever paper is weakest. It's all online, so it's easy to fit in around holiday plans."
