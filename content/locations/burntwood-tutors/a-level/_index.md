faq_3_q: "Can an A-Level tutor help with coursework, like the History or English NEA?"
faq_3_a: "Yes, within the rules. The NEA has to be your child's own work, so the tutor won't write or rewrite any of it. What they can do is help your child pick a question that actually works, plan the argument, find and read sources, and understand what the mark scheme rewards. Coursework is often where marks slip away without anyone noticing, usually because it was left too late."
