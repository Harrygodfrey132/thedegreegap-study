  - q: "Why does my child do well in English lessons but not in the exam?"
    a: "Usually it's time and structure rather than ideas. In class there's discussion, a model answer and a second go. In the exam your child has to plan, find evidence and write about two texts in under two hours, alone and from memory. So the gap is often how quickly a good thought becomes a developed paragraph. A tutor practises exactly that under timed conditions, a little longer each week."
