We work backwards from whichever set your child is aiming at, and we will tell you honestly how far off it looks from here."
faq_3_q: "Can you help a student who has fallen a long way behind?"

From there the tutor works forward alongside what school is teaching, and the two meet somewhere around the middle of the term. Slower than a crammed week in April, and it lasts."
