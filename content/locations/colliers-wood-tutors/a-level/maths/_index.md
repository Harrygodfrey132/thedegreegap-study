Hypothesis tests in Statistics catch people out in a similar way, because the conclusion has to be written in the context of the question. A tutor practises the wording until it's automatic, using questions from your child's own board."
  - q: "My child's sixth form uses Pearson Edexcel for A-Level Maths. Does the board matter for tutoring?"
