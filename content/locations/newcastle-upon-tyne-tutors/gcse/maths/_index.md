  - q: "How do I work out which Maths topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Check where the marks stop on a marked mock, not the total. That's the quickest of the three to fix. Bring the paper to the call and we'll go through it with you."

If your child's already scoring highly in mocks, timed papers and someone to mark them may be all they need. Tell us on the call what you're seeing, and we'll say which it is."
