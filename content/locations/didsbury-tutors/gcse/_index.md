faq_1_q: "Parrs Wood or Didsbury Sixth Form: do the GCSE grades they ask for really differ?"
faq_1_a: "Yes, quite a bit, so it's worth knowing early. Parrs Wood's recent entry list wanted at least five GCSEs at grade 4 or above, English and Maths among them. On top of that, most A-Levels there asked for a 6 or so in a related subject. At Didsbury Sixth Form it was six grade 6s, again including English and Maths, and the likes of Chemistry and Physics needed a 7. Xaverian and Loreto have different requirements for each course. The numbers can change each year, so keep the current prospectus handy and we'll work back from there."
faq_2_q: "My child is at The Barlow, which stops at 16. When should we think about tutoring?"
faq_2_a: "Some time in Year 10 is ideal, while there's room to fix a gap before Year 11 piles on top of it. A Year 11 start can still work well. It just means more past papers and timing practice, and less going back."
