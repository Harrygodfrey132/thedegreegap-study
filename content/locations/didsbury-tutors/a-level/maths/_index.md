Others give a number in Statistics without saying what it means. A marked test shows quickly which it is for your child, and that's where the tutor starts."

Each board uses its own large data set, real-world figures that Statistics questions draw on. OCR MEI has a style all of its own. We'll confirm the board on the call, and every tutor we put forward will know it."
