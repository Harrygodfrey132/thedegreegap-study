  - q: "Higher tier or foundation: how do we know which is right?"
    a: "Your child's Year 10 mocks will usually point the way. Higher tier covers grades 9 to 4, plus a 3 if your child only just misses a 4. Foundation covers 1 to 5. Around a borderline 4 or 5, higher is usually right. But it needs work on the earlier questions, because that's where a 4 or 5 is earned on the higher paper. At a 3, foundation often gives the better result with less risk. The school decides the entry, and a tutor's read of a recent paper gives you something solid to discuss."
  - q: "Does my child need a Maths tutor, or just more revision?"
    a: "The working on a recent past paper will usually tell you, more than the score at the top does. If it's clean and confident but runs out of steam near the end, that's exam technique, and it's the quickest thing to fix. Blank space or crossed-out attempts on the multi-step questions point to shaky methods. That's where a tutor helps most. And if your child's already scoring over 80 per cent across mocks, practising the last few question types may well be enough."
