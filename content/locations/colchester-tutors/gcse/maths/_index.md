  - q: "Is higher tier the right entry for my child in Maths?"
    a: "It depends where the Year 10 mocks are landing. Higher tier runs from grade 4 up to 9, foundation from 1 up to 5. If your child is sitting around a borderline 4 or 5 in Year 10 mocks, higher tier is usually right but needs targeted preparation on the early questions, because that is where the grades 4 and 5 are actually earned on that paper. If they are at a 3, foundation often produces the better result by reducing risk. A Year 10 diagnostic is the honest way to make the call."
  - q: "Does my child need a Maths tutor or just more revision?"
    a: "Read the working on a recent past paper rather than the score at the top. Clean, confident working that runs out of steam at the back of the paper points to exam technique, which is the fastest thing to fix. Blank space or crossed-out attempts on multi-step questions points to method confidence, and that is where a tutor makes the biggest difference. If your child is already scoring above 80 per cent across mocks, targeted practice on the last few question types is likely enough."
