faq_3_q: "How can I tell which topics my child is losing marks on?"
faq_3_a: "By where on the paper the marks go rather than by the total. Losses spread across the short questions at the front usually mean content has not been learned. Losses concentrated in the six-mark and extended questions mean the content is there but the answer is not being built the way the mark scheme wants, which is faster to fix. Losses in one subject while the others hold up means you have found the problem and it is a third the size you feared. Send a recent paper and a tutor can read this in twenty minutes."
