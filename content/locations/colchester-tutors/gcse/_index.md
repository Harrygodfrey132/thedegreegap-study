faq_1_q: "What grades does Colchester Sixth Form College ask for?"

They can change from one year to the next, so go by the current admissions page rather than what was true two intakes ago. If your child is one grade short of what a course wants, that's often a twelve to sixteen week piece of work, not a rebuild."

faq_3_q: "How can I tell which topics my child is losing marks on?"
faq_3_a: "Go by where on the paper the marks are lost, not just the total. If they go across the short questions near the front, some content hasn't sunk in yet. If they go on the six-mark and longer questions, your child knows the content but isn't building the answer the way the mark scheme wants. That's quicker to fix. And if it's one subject while the others hold up, you've found the problem, and it's smaller than you feared. The tutor can usually read all this from one recent paper."
