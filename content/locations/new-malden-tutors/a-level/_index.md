faq_1_q: "Are predicted grades just a guess? How do teachers decide them?"
faq_1_a: "They're a judgement, but not a guess. Teachers base them on your child's work across Year 12 and the start of Year 13, and the end-of-year exams usually count for a lot. At Richard Challoner, for example, Year 12 Maths ends with two internal exams. A tutor can't change a prediction directly. What they can do is lift the marked work and exam results that teachers look at when they write one."
