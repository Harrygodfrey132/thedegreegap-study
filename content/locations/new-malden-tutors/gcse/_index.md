faq_2_q: "My child is in a Foundation set for Maths. Could they still move up to Higher?"
faq_2_a: "Often, yes. Foundation papers cover grades 1 to 5 and Higher covers 4 to 9, and many schools look at sets again during Year 10, as Holy Cross does. The difference is mostly algebra: rearranging, quadratics and the longer problems Foundation never reaches. A tutor works on those alongside the class, and after a few weeks you'll have a much clearer idea whether Higher is realistic. The school makes the final call, but your child goes into that conversation with evidence."
