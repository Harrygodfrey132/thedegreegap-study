Next comes Language Paper 2, where comparing two writers' views, one from the 1800s, takes a method most children haven't been shown. Both improve with steady practice, which is why a weekly lesson with a little learning between suits English well."

    a: "Usually not. Plenty of New Malden children grow up with two languages and read very well in English.
