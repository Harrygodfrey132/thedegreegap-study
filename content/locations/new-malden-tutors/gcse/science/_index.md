Coombe Sixth Form, Richard Challoner and Tiffin set their own lists, which can change from year to year.   - q: "School is talking about Foundation Science for my child. Should we push for Higher?"
    a: "If A-Level sciences are a possibility, Higher, because Foundation Combined Science tops out at a 5-5. Higher runs from 4-3 up to 9-9 but asks for more calculations and longer answers. If your child is scoring around a 4 or 5 in Year 10 tests, Higher is often within reach with steady work on the maths in Physics and Chemistry. School makes the final decision. A few weeks with a tutor gives you a clearer view going into that conversation."
  - q: "Do we need separate tutors for Biology, Chemistry and Physics?"

And if Maths is also a worry, a tutor who teaches Maths and Physics can link the two, since the equations and rearranging are the same skill."
