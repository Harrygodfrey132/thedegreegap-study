  - q: "How can I tell which parts of GCSE English my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Look at a marked essay rather than the grade, and look at where the teacher has left comments. If they keep asking for more analysis, it's the objective about language and structure. If they want more evidence or better-chosen quotations, it's the reading one. If they say the context feels bolted on, that's a third. And if the last answer is thin or unfinished, it's timing rather than any objective. Those four patterns cover most essays, and each needs quite different work."
  - q: "My child understands the books. Why is GCSE English still a struggle?"
