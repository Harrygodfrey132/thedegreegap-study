  - q: "How can I tell which GCSE English topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Look at a marked script rather than a grade, and look at which parts of it the teacher has annotated. If the comments cluster around wanting more analysis, the objective in question is about language and structure. If they ask for more evidence or better-chosen quotations, it is the reading objective. If they mention context feeling bolted on, that is a third. If the last answer is thin or unfinished, it is timing rather than any objective at all. Those four patterns cover most scripts and they need quite different work."
