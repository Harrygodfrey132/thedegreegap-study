  - q: "My child's in Year 10. Is it too early for GCSE Science tutoring?"
    a: "No, and in Science it's often the better time to start. The required practicals are spread across both years. A tutor in Year 10 can go over each one close to when your child does it in class, while it's still fresh. On triple, it also catches a weaker science before Year 11 speeds all three up at once. And the lessons are calmer. They're about rebuilding method rather than drilling papers, which suits a child who's started to switch off."
  - q: "How long is each GCSE Science lesson?"
    a: "For most families it's an hour, which in Science is roughly one topic's worth. Some families on triple book ninety minutes nearer the exams, so more than one science fits in a sitting. But a good hour beats a tired longer one. If your child is younger, or concentration's the real issue, forty-five minutes can work better, as long as it happens every week. It's worth mentioning on the call, so the length suits your child rather than the default."
  - q: "What sort of grade improvements do your Science tutors actually get?"
    a: "There's no single number that would mean much, and nobody can promise a grade. What we can say is that the size of the jump depends on the kind of problem. If Year 10 Chemistry never really sank in, it's a longer road, and one term shows less on paper. A good tutor will tell you which you're dealing with within the first few weeks."
