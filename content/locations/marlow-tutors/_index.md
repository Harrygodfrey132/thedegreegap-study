faq_1_q: "What do the sixth forms around Marlow ask for at GCSE?"
faq_1_a: "It depends on which one, and they ask in quite different ways. Sir William Borlase's sets an average points score across all of a student's GCSEs, plus a grade 5 in English and Maths and higher grades in the subjects they want to study. Great Marlow School asks for a grade 4 in English or Maths, with a resit alongside if the other one falls short. The Henley College does not let students resit alongside an A-Level programme. Requirements change year to year, so read this year's policy for each."
