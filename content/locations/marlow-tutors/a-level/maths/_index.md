faqs:
  - q: "What is the hardest part of A-Level Maths for Marlow students?"
    a: "Usually proof, Mechanics and multi-topic Pure problems. Proof asks for every logical step in writing, which feels unnatural to students who have always been rewarded for a quick correct answer. Mechanics goes wrong at the diagram stage, before any calculation. And the longer Pure questions mix trigonometry, calculus and algebra in one problem without saying which to start with. Statistics trips up students who calculate well but explain poorly. The good news is that all of these respond well to regular practice with feedback."
  - q: "How can I tell which A-Level Maths topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Look at a recent marked paper question by question, not just the total. If marks disappear on the early, shorter questions, the issue is often a core skill such as algebraic manipulation. If they go on the long questions at the end, it is more likely problem-solving and method choice. If one section, Mechanics or Statistics, is weaker every time, that strand needs its own attention. A tutor does this analysis in the first session and plans the following weeks from it."
