  - q: "My child reads all the time. Why are their English grades stuck?"
    a: "Because reading well and writing an exam answer well are different skills. Keen readers often understand a text deeply but have never been shown how to plan an essay, choose a quotation that supports a point, or write about a writer's methods instead of the story. Others lose marks on the writing tasks because they start without a plan and run out of time. A tutor's first job is working out which of those it is from a recent piece of marked work."
  - q: "Does my child need a tutor, or just more revision?"
    a: "Look at what the marked essays say. If your child writes clear, well-planned answers and just needs more quotations memorised, focused revision may be enough. If the essays drift, run short, or keep getting the same comment about analysis, more revision alone rarely fixes it, because the problem is how the answer is built. That is where one-to-one help moves things quickly. We will tell you honestly on the call which one we think it is."
