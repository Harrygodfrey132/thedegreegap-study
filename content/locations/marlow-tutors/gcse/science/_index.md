faqs:
  - q: "What is the hardest part of GCSE Science for Marlow students?"
    a: "Chemistry calculations come up most often, followed by six-mark extended answers and Physics equations. Mole calculations and concentration questions trip up students who are otherwise strong. Six-mark questions in all three sciences are marked on how well ideas connect, so a list of correct facts scores less than expected. In Physics, rearranging and units cost more marks than the concepts do. Required practicals are the fourth regular problem, because students remember doing them but not the reasoning behind each step."
