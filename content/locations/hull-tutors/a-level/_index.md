faq_2_q: "Is the first term of Year 12 too early to bring in an A-Level tutor?"
faq_2_a: "It's often the best time. The first term is when the gap between GCSE and A-Level opens, and small problems, like answers that list instead of explain, are quickest to fix before they turn into habits. Year 12 work also feeds the predicted grades on a UCAS form. Starting in Year 13 is common too; the plan just leans harder on past papers and timing."

faq_3_a: "Yes, within clear limits. The tutor can help your child narrow a question that's too big, plan the research, and see what a strong evaluation of sources looks like. Most of the help comes at the planning stage, which is where projects usually stall."
