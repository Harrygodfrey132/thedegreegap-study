  - q: "How can I tell which parts of GCSE English my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Read a marked script and look at what the teacher has annotated rather than the grade. Comments asking what the effect actually is, or writing that a point needs developing, point at imprecise analysis. Comments asking for better evidence point elsewhere, and a note that context feels bolted on points somewhere else again. If the last answer is thin, the issue is pacing rather than analysis. Each of those needs a different term, which is why diagnosis comes before any teaching."
  - q: "Why is my child struggling with GCSE English?"
    a: "A frequent cause among students who are otherwise doing well is imprecision. They understand the text, choose sensible quotations and then describe the effect in general terms that could apply to almost any writing. The mark scheme rewards saying what this particular choice does, and that needs vocabulary a student may simply not have yet. The other common cause is timing. Both are habits rather than knowledge gaps, and habits shift faster than comprehension problems do."
