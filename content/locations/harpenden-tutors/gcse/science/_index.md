    a: "Not at all. September to February is the best window, and earlier in it is better. Predicted grades lean heavily on the winter mocks, and they're what sixth forms see when they make offers.   - q: "Foundation or higher tier: which should my child sit in Science?"
    a: "Go by where your child is now, not by hopes for the summer. Higher tier runs from grade 9 down to 4, with a 3 allowed for a near miss. Foundation goes up to a 5, and the marks are easier to come by. On combined, there's one tier for all the papers. A child near the borderline often does best on higher, as long as the deciding topics get some focused work. A child well short of it usually does better on foundation. Schools review tier entries in Year 10 and usually settle them in Year 11, so a real paper, honestly marked, is good evidence to have."

If the gap runs deeper, that same term still shows real progress, just not always a full band. The tutor should be straight with you about that from the start."
