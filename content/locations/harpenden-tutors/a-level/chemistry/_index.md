Content overlaps heavily, examining does not: the boards differ on how calculations are structured, how much working earns method marks, which values appear in the data booklet and how the practical endorsement is evidenced. In energetics they also differ on which definitions are examined directly. We confirm the specification before proposing anyone rather than treating it as a detail."
  - q: "Year 12 or Year 13, when is the right time to start?"
