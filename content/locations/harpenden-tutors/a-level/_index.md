faq_1_q: "Should we wait and see how Year 13 starts?"
faq_1_a: "It is the common instinct and it usually costs the thing you care about. Predicted grades are built on Year 12 performance and reach universities early in Year 13, so a Year 13 mock arrives after the number that shaped the offers has gone. Starting in Year 12 means the work shows up in the prediction itself. Starting later still helps the final grade, but the application has already been made."
faq_2_q: "She understands it in lessons and then the mock comes back low. Why?"
faq_2_a: "Because lessons and exams test different things. In class the topic is named, an example is nearby and help is available. In an exam none of that is true and a clock is running. At A-Level the gap is usually that a student can follow a method when it is being driven and cannot select it themselves under pressure. That is practised rather than explained, which is why the sessions are quieter than parents expect."
