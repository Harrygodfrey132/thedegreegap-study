faq_1_q: "She works for hours and the grade will not move. What are we missing?"
faq_1_a: "Almost always what the hours are being spent on. Re-reading a topic already understood feels productive and changes very little, and it is where most students default because it is the comfortable part. A tutor marks a recent paper, finds the three or four question types genuinely losing marks, and puts the sessions there instead. The total hours often go down rather than up, which is usually a relief to everybody."
faq_2_q: "Is Year 10 or Year 11 the better time to start?"
faq_2_a: "Year 10 if you have the option. Content steps up that year and any gap that opens is small enough to close before it hardens, whereas left alone it compounds and lands all at once in a mock. Year 11 still works and many families start there, but the plan narrows to exam technique rather than rebuilding, and there is less room to repair confidence along the way."
