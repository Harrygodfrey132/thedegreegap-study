  - q: "What's the hardest part of A-Level Maths for Sutton sixth formers?"
    a: "Usually the first term of Year 12, when questions no longer tell your child which method to use. Children who did well at GCSE often find it hardest, because spotting the question type carried them a long way and suddenly there's nothing to spot. After that, marks tend to leak from whichever applied part gets less attention. That's often Mechanics for anyone who skips the diagram. Or it's Statistics, if the large data set (a set of real figures each exam board chooses for the course) never felt familiar. Proof is the other one. Building an argument isn't the same as finishing a calculation."
