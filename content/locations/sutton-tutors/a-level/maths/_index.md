    a: "The first term of Year 12, where a question stops saying which method it wants. Strong GCSE performers often find that hardest, because pattern matching carried them a long way and then has nothing left to match against. After that the losses gather in whichever applied strand gets neglected, usually Mechanics for students who avoid drawing diagrams and Statistics for those who never got comfortable with the large data set. Proof is the other one, since building an argument is not the same act as finishing a calculation."
