faq_2_q: "Do you work with students preparing for grammar or sixth form entry in Sutton?"
faq_2_a: "Yes. Some families come to us for the 11+ and the Selective Eligibility Test the borough's grammar schools share. faq_3_q: "What exam boards do your tutors cover?"

The board is part of the match rather than an afterthought, because question wording and the marking of longer answers differ between them. We confirm the specification on the first call. If your child sits iGCSE, say so, since the paper structure isn't the same."
