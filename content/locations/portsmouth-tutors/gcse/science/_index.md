  - q: "Who decides the tier, and can it still change?"
    a: "Your child's school decides, usually using mock results, and entries are normally settled during Year 11, so there's more room to talk it through earlier on. For a child around a 5 in Year 10 mocks, higher is usually right, as long as the gaps get worked on. For a child sitting at a 3, foundation can be the safer choice, because falling short of the lowest grade on higher means no grade at all. A tutor can show you, with real papers, where your child stands."
