  - q: "Is it a tutor my child needs for Maths, or just more revision at home?"
    a: "Their working on a recent paper usually tells you. If it's neat and mostly right but marks disappear on the last few questions, that's exam technique, and a tutor can often sort it quickly. If the working stops halfway, or there's a lot crossed out on multi-step questions, your child isn't sure of the method, and more revision alone rarely fixes that. If they're already scoring well across mocks, targeted past papers may be enough. We'll tell you honestly on the call."
