  - q: "My child does well in other subjects. Why is GCSE English such a struggle?"
    a: "Often because nobody has shown them what a good answer is built from. In Maths or Science your child can check whether they're right. In English the mark scheme rewards things that feel vague, like 'developed' or 'perceptive' comments, and a child who likes certainty can find that maddening. Usually the gap goes back to never being taught how to plan an essay, so every answer starts from nothing. The first few lessons find where it breaks down: choosing evidence, or explaining it."
