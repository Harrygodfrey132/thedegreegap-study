  - q: "How can I tell which Maths topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "The easiest clue is a recent mock paper, looked at by question number. Early questions are usually quick arithmetic and single topics, and the later ones get longer and mix topics together. If the marks disappear near the start, it's often a gap in basics like fractions or negatives. If they go in the last third, it's usually about spotting the first step. A tutor does this sort in the first lesson, so you'll know where the hour is going."
  - q: "Should my child sit Foundation or Higher tier?"
    a: "It depends where they're working now. Higher tier covers grades 4 to 9 and Foundation covers 1 to 5, so a grade 4 or 5 is possible on either. A steady 5 or better in mocks usually points to Higher. If your child is hovering around a 3 or 4, Foundation can be the calmer route. The school makes the final call, and a tutor's first few lessons give you something solid to take to that conversation."
