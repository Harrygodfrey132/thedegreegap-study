  - q: "How do I work out which bits of Maths are costing my child marks?"
    a: "Start with a recent mock and look at which question numbers lost marks. The early questions are usually short and test one thing, and the later ones get longer and mix topics together. Marks dropped near the start often point to basics like fractions or negatives. Marks lost in the last third are more often about seeing where to begin. The tutor does this sorting properly in the first lesson, so you'll know exactly where the hour's going."
  - q: "Is Higher tier the right choice for my child?"
    a: "Mock grades are the best guide. Higher runs from 9 to 4, with a 3 for a narrow miss. Foundation goes up to a 5, so a 4 or 5 is possible on either. Schools review tiers in Year 10 and usually settle them in Year 11, and the final call is theirs. A tutor's view helps you talk it through with them."
