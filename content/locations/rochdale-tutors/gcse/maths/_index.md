  - q: "My child's been entered for Foundation Maths. Is that the right tier?"
    a: "It might be, and it's worth checking rather than assuming. Foundation tops out at a grade 5, while Higher runs from around a 4 up to a 9. If your child sits around a 5 or 6 in Year 10, Higher is usually right, with some focused preparation. Nearer a 3 or 4, Foundation can be the steadier route to a pass. Schools usually decide after the Year 10 or early Year 11 mocks, and a tutor can give you a second opinion."
