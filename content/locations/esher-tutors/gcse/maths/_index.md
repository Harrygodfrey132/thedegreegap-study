  - q: "Should my child practise more for the calculator or the non-calculator paper?"
    a: "Both, because all three papers count the same. On AQA, Edexcel and OCR, your child sits one non-calculator paper and two calculator ones, so the calculator is allowed for two-thirds of the marks. Eduqas sets two papers instead. The non-calculator paper rewards quick, accurate work by hand: fractions, negatives, expanding and factorising. The calculator papers lean more on long problems with several steps. A common pattern is lost marks on basics in the non-calculator paper, then running out of time near the end of the final paper. Going through your child's recent papers in the first lesson usually settles which needs the work."
