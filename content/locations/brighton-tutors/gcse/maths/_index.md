  - q: "What grades do BHASVIC and Varndean ask for?"
    a: "Both publish their own requirements and revise them between years, so the current admissions page is the only version worth planning around. They are standalone colleges rather than school sixth forms, both are regularly oversubscribed, and under the local Accord system a student applies to one or the other. The general entry ask is usually a set of grade 4s, with a higher requirement in the subjects a student wants to carry on. For Maths and the Sciences that subject-specific number is normally the one that decides it."
  - q: "When is the best time to start Maths tutoring in Year 11?"
    a: "September to February, and earlier inside that window is better here than in most places, because the college applications and the mock that informs them arrive close together. A start in the autumn gives a full term of weekly work before anything is decided. April and May starts still help, and we take them often, but by then the sensible programme is narrower: pick the two or three topics bleeding the most marks and drill those rather than trying to rebuild anything."
