  - q: "Should my child take Combined or Triple Science?"
    a: "Combined is worth two GCSEs graded from 9-9 down, Triple is worth three separate grades. Triple carries more content and more exam time, so it suits a student comfortable across all three. Combined is not a lesser route and does not close A-Level Sciences at BHASVIC or Varndean, though the subject-specific entry requirements vary and are worth checking on the current page rather than assumed. What matters more is whether the weakest of the three is being carried by the other two."
  - q: "Can one tutor cover Biology, Chemistry and Physics?"
    a: "Sometimes. Many tutors cover two of the three at real depth, usually Biology with Chemistry or Chemistry with Physics, and fewer cover all three. If a student needs exam technique across the board, one tutor is efficient and works well. If Physics has been sliding since Year 10 while the other two are solid, an hour split three ways achieves less than an hour aimed at the problem. For Triple Science students taking Maths too, a Maths and Physics tutor gives a useful overlap."
  - q: "How do you vet your Science tutors?"
    a: "Roughly three in every hundred applicants make it onto the platform. Written application, a subject knowledge stage, then a founder-led interview built around explaining something difficult while a student is stuck. References are checked before any match. For Science we look closely at whether a tutor can teach a required practical as an exam question rather than as a memory of doing it, because that is where a lot of reachable marks sit unclaimed."
  - q: "What if my child misses their Science grade on results day?"
    a: "We work with families through results day and the resit window. An eight-week block in September and October is the usual shape, most often aimed at moving a 3 to a 4 or a 4 to a 5, which is where most college entry points sit. If the shortfall was against a specific course at BHASVIC or Varndean, ring them before assuming the place is gone. Both handle near-misses case by case, and Greater Brighton Metropolitan College runs resits alongside Level 3 courses."
