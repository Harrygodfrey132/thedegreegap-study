It's the writing against the clock: essay structure that drifts, or a good point that never gets tied back to the question. They'll mark her recent work the way the exam board would, then rebuild the technique on past papers. And English Language and English Literature really are two different exams, so we'll match a tutor who knows the one she's dropping marks in."
