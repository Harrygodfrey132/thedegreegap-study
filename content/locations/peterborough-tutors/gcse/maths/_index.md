  - q: "How much Maths should my child do between lessons?"
    a: "About an hour to an hour and a half across the week, in short bursts rather than one long sitting. Maths rewards little and often. Three twenty-minute goes at the same type of question do more than one long evening, because the method has time to settle in between. It should be exam questions on the topic just covered, not rereading notes or watching videos. Past ninety minutes the returns drop off fast. And a tired child practising a method wrongly is worse off than one who stopped."
  - q: "Should my child take higher or foundation tier?"
    a: "It depends where your child is now, not where anyone hopes they'll end up. On higher tier the grades run from 9 to 4, a near miss can still earn a 3, and below that it's ungraded. Foundation tops out at a 5, but far more of the paper is within your child's reach. If Year 10 mocks are landing around a 5 or 6, higher is often right, with focused work on the topics that decide it. Well below that, foundation usually works better, because more of the questions are winnable. The school makes the final call, and a tutor's look at a recent paper gives you evidence to bring."
