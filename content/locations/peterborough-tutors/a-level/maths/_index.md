    a: "Yes, though not in the way most families expect. The Maths is almost the same on every board. What changes is how questions are worded, what's in the formula booklet, and the large data set, the real-world data each board picks for its Statistics questions. If your child practises on another board's papers, they can drop marks on their own board's, even when they know the Maths. We'll ask which board it is on the call, before we suggest a tutor."

A quick glance and a tick is how a teenager convinces themselves a method's secure when it isn't. The tutor sets the amount and checks how your child's marking it."
