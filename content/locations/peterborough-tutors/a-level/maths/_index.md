  - q: "What is the difference between AQA, Edexcel and OCR A-Level Maths, and does it matter?"
    a: "It matters in ways that are easy to miss. The core mathematics is close to identical, but the boards differ in how questions are worded, how much working must be shown for method marks, which results appear in the formula booklet and how the large data set is used. A student drilled on one board's papers can lose marks on another's conventions while doing the same mathematics. We confirm the specification before proposing anyone."
  - q: "How much A-Level Maths practice should my child do between sessions?"
    a: "Two to four hours across the week for a student aiming near the top of the band, split into short sittings rather than one long one. Marking it honestly matters as much as doing it: glancing at an answer and calling it right is how a student convinces themselves a method is secure when it is not. The tutor sets the volume and checks how the marking is being done."
