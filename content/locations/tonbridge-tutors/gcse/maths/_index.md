  - q: "How can I tell which Maths topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Look at where the marks go on a recent mock, not just the total. Higher tier papers usually start with shorter number questions, move into ratio, geometry and algebra, and finish with longer multi-step problems. If the marks all disappear near the end, it's often exam technique and timing. If they're scattered from early on, there's usually a gap from Year 8 or 9 underneath. A tutor can sort this out in the first lesson."

Lessons are more about understanding than exam practice at that stage. Year 11 is still a good time too. The work just gets more focused on past papers as the exams get closer."
