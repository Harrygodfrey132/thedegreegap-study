  - q: "Is there a way to see where my child is dropping Maths marks?"
    a: "Yes, and a recent mock is the place to look. Check where on the paper the marks went, rather than the total. Higher papers usually open with shorter number questions, move through ratio, geometry and algebra, and finish with longer multi-step problems. If everything goes wrong near the end, it's often exam technique and timing. If the lost marks are scattered from early on, there's usually a gap from Year 8 or 9 underneath. That's what the tutor sorts out in the first lesson."
  - q: "Is Year 10 too early to start with a GCSE Maths tutor?"
    a: "Not at all, and it's often the easiest time to start. In Year 10 there's room to go back and fix a shaky topic, like algebra or negative numbers, before Year 11 work lands on top of it. Lessons lean more towards understanding than exam practice at that stage. Year 11 is still a good time too. The work just gets more focused on past papers as the exams get closer."
