  - q: "Is the GCSE Maths standard in Tonbridge similar to Tunbridge Wells?"
    a: "Close enough that the comparison is not much use. Both towns sit inside the Kent selective system, both send students to grammar and to other secondaries, and the sixth forms in both publish their own entry requirements. What really differs is school by school, not town by town. The number worth knowing is the Maths requirement published this year by the sixth form your child actually wants, and how far the current paper sits from it. A marked past paper answers that in about twenty minutes."
  - q: "How can I tell which GCSE Maths topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Look at question numbers rather than the total. On a higher tier paper the early questions test arithmetic and ratio, the middle runs through geometry and graphs, and the last stretch is algebra, proof and multi-step problem solving. Marks lost evenly across the whole paper point at pace and accuracy. Marks lost almost entirely in the back third point at question style, which shifts faster than content does. Ten minutes with a marked script tells you more than a term of general revision."
