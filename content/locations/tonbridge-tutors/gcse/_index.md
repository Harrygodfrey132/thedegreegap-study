faq_1_q: "What GCSE grades do Tonbridge sixth forms ask for?"
faq_1_a: "It depends a lot on which one. The Judd School works from an average across a student's best ten qualifications, with at least a 4 in Maths and English Language. Weald of Kent uses an average across the best eight plus a 5 in English. Hillview asks for 4s in Maths and English Language and three other subjects. Most also set their own grade for each A-Level subject. These change from year to year, so check the current guide for the sixth form your child likes."
faq_2_q: "Is Year 10 too early to start GCSE tutoring?"
faq_2_a: "Not at all. Year 10 is often the easiest time, because there's room to go back and fix a wobbly topic before Year 11 piles more on top. Lessons then are more about understanding than exam practice. Year 11 is still a good time to start, and plenty of Tonbridge families do, usually in the autumn before the mocks. The work just gets more focused on past papers and timing as the exams get closer."
faq_3_q: "Our children sit different exam boards. Does that matter for tutoring?"
faq_3_a: "Yes, and it's worth knowing early. AQA, Edexcel and OCR cover a lot of the same content, but they ask questions in different ways and mark them differently. A tutor who knows your child's exact board can start on real past questions from the first lesson. That's why we match each child separately. Not sure which board it is? It's usually printed on the front of a mock paper, or we can work it out together on the consultation call."
