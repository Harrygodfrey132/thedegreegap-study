  - q: "Does my child need Triple Science to take a science A-Level at Esher Sixth Form College or Southborough?"
    a: "No. Both currently accept Combined Science. Esher Sixth Form College asks for 6-6 in Combined Science, or 6s in the separate sciences, plus a 6 in GCSE Maths on the Higher paper, for A-Level Biology, Chemistry and Physics. Southborough asks for a 6 in Combined Science or the separate GCSE, and prefers a 6 in Maths. Triple can make the first term a little easier, because less is new, but it isn't required. Check the current course pages for your child's year of entry."
  - q: "How can I tell which science is pulling my child's grade down?"
    a: "Look at the papers, not the overall grade. Combined Science gives one double grade, like 5-5 or 6-5, but there are six papers underneath, two each for Biology, Chemistry and Physics. Ask school for the paper-by-paper marks from the last mock, or check the marked scripts if they came home. One weak science, often Chemistry or Physics calculations, can hold the whole grade down. A tutor's first lesson confirms which, then puts the time there."

If school homework is heavy one week, tell the tutor and they'll lighten it."
