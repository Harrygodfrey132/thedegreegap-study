  - q: "How much maths is there in A-Level Biology?"
    a: "More than most students expect. On the Edexcel course at Esher, maths skills make up 10% of the marks, and other boards are similar. It's things like percentage change, rates from graphs, standard deviation and statistical tests such as chi-squared. None of it is hard maths, but it's easy to drop marks by rounding badly or forgetting units. If your child stopped Maths after GCSE, a few lessons on these skills early in Year 12 can save a lot of marks later."
