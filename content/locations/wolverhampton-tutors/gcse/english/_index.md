  - q: "Why is my child's English predicted grade lower than I expected?"
    a: "Predictions come from mock performance and teacher judgement, and English mocks punish timing more harshly than almost any other subject. A student who writes strong analysis slowly gets predicted on the unfinished paper rather than on the quality of the paragraphs they did complete. That is the single most common cause we see, and one of the more fixable, because timing responds to practice under real conditions far faster than analytical depth does."
  - q: "How much English work should my child do between sessions?"
    a: "Sixty to ninety minutes, and in English that usually means one timed piece rather than an hour of reading notes. Writing is what is assessed, so writing is what has to be practised. One essay written to time and handed in beats three hours with a highlighter. Past ninety minutes the returns fall away, and for a Year 11 carrying nine subjects it stops being realistic anyway."
