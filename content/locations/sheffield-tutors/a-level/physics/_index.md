angle_body_1: "Plenty of students get through mechanics and electricity by forming a mental picture of what is happening, and it works well, because those topics describe things that can be imagined. Fields do not cooperate. A gravitational field has no appearance, potential is defined relative to infinity, and the sign conventions run against intuition, with potential energy negative and increasing towards zero. Students who have relied on visualising reach this unit and find the technique has stopped working, usually somewhere in the middle of Year 13 when there is little time to develop another."
angle_body_2: "What replaces it is working from definitions and relationships rather than pictures: knowing precisely what each quantity is defined as, which graph relates to which by gradient or area, and trusting the algebra over intuition when the two disagree. It is a genuine change of method and worth naming as one rather than treating the student as suddenly weaker. The first session establishes how your child currently approaches an unfamiliar topic. Sheffield sixth forms and colleges each publish their own requirements."
