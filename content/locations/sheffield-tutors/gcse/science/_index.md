  - q: "Should my child take Combined or Triple Science?"
    a: "Combined Science is worth two GCSEs and covers all three subjects, Triple is worth three and goes deeper in each. Triple suits a student who is comfortably at a grade 6 or above and heading for a Science A-Level, because the extra content overlaps with what Year 12 assumes. Combined is not a lesser route and it does not close off A-Level Sciences at most sixth forms, though some set a higher Combined grade as the equivalent. It's a decision worth making with a marked Year 10 paper in front of you, not on instinct."
  - q: "What happens in a typical GCSE Science tutoring session?"
    a: "Most run 60 minutes in three parts. The first fifteen goes over the week's homework and surfaces whatever the marking exposed. The middle thirty works the topic bleeding the most marks, usually a required practical or an extended-response question, with the method broken down into steps the student can repeat. The last fifteen is exam-paper practice on that same topic with a mark-scheme walkthrough, so they see how the marks are awarded rather than just whether the answer was right."
