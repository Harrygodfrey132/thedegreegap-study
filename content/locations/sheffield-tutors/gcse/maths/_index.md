faqs:
  - q: "What grade do Sheffield sixth forms want for A-Level Maths?"
    a: "Each one publishes its own and they vary by course and by year, so check the current page for the school your child is applying to rather than relying on what a friend's older child needed. King Edward VII School, Silverdale School and High Storrs all set a subject-specific minimum for Maths that sits above their general entry bar, and it is usually higher than the requirement for the humanities. The practical point is that Maths is judged on its own, so a strong overall set of results will not make up for the Maths grade itself."
  - q: "How can I tell which GCSE Maths topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Look at question numbers on a recent mock rather than the total mark. Higher-tier papers build in difficulty, so marks lost across the early questions point at arithmetic and method, while marks lost from question 17 onwards usually point at exam technique rather than knowledge. If the paper is mostly right until the last third and then falls away, the problem is often timing and stamina, which is a faster fix than most parents expect."
  - q: "Does my child need a tutor or just more revision?"
    a: "The answer is in what their working looks like on a recent past paper, not in how many hours they've put in. Clean, correct working that still loses marks at the top of the paper is an exam technique gap, and that is the fastest thing a tutor fixes. Blank space or crossed-out attempts on multi-step questions is a method confidence gap, and that one rarely resolves through revision alone because the student avoids the questions they most need to practise. Already scoring above 80% across mocks and it's targeted practice you need, not a full programme."
