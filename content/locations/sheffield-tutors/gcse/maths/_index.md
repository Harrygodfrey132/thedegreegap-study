  - q: "Should my child focus on the calculator or non-calculator paper for GCSE Maths?"
    a: "Both, because they are weighted equally and decide the grade together. The non-calculator paper rewards arithmetic fluency and clean algebra; the two calculator papers reward accuracy on multi-step, wordy problems, which is where most Sheffield students we see lose marks at the back end. The first session marks a recent paper of each to find which is bleeding more, and the early lessons target that paper first."
  - q: "How do I know if my child needs a GCSE Maths tutor or just more revision?"

Blank or crossed-out working on the multi-step questions points to a method-confidence gap, where a tutor is essential. If a student is already scoring above 80% across mocks, targeted practice on one topic block is usually enough, not a full programme."
