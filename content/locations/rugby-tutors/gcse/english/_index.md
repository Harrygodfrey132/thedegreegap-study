  - q: "My child reads a lot. Why are they struggling with GCSE English?"
    a: "Wide reading helps, but the exam asks for something more particular: an argument about how a writer creates an effect, backed up by short, well-chosen evidence. Many keen readers have never been taught to plan that kind of answer, so they write everything they notice and run out of time. A tutor teaches a simple structure for a paragraph, then builds speed until it holds up under exam conditions."
  - q: "Will my child have English homework from the tutor as well as from school?"

More than that tends to tip into copying out notes, which feels busy and does little. The tutor marks whatever comes back at the start of the next lesson, so the effort never goes unread."
