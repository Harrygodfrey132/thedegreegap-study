faq_2_q: "When do teachers decide the grades that go on UCAS forms?"
faq_2_a: "Mostly from what your child does in Year 12. They're sent with UCAS applications in the autumn of Year 13, so the end-of-Year-12 exams and the work around them carry real weight. Tutoring won't change a prediction by itself. What it changes is the work your child hands in and the marks they get, and that's what teachers look at when they decide."
