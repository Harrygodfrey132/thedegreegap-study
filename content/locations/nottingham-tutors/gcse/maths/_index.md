  - q: "Why is my child struggling with GCSE Maths?"
    a: "Usually because of something that happened two or three years before the topic they are stuck on. Maths stacks. Fractions hold up ratio, ratio holds up proportion, negatives hold up algebra, algebra holds up almost everything after it. Pull one out low down and everything above it wobbles, no matter how hard the student works on the thing in front of them. This is why more revision on the current topic often changes nothing, and why the first session is spent finding the real floor rather than teaching."
  - q: "How do you vet your Maths tutors?"
    a: "Roughly three in every hundred applicants make it onto the platform. There is a written application, a subject knowledge stage, then a founder-led interview that is mostly about how someone explains a hard idea to a student who is stuck and embarrassed about it. References get checked before any match. Every tutor is a graduate, many are qualified teachers or examiners, and the degree by itself has never been what gets someone through."
  - q: "Is GCSE Maths tutoring actually worth the money?"
    a: "It depends on the gap, and we will tell you honestly on the call. Where a student is already at the grade they need, the marginal gain is small and a short block aimed at one topic is often better value than a full programme."
