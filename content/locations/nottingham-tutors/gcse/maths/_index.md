  - q: "Should my child focus on the calculator or non-calculator paper for GCSE Maths?"
    a: "Both, because they are weighted equally and decide the grade together. The non-calculator paper rewards arithmetic fluency and clean algebra; the two calculator papers reward accuracy on multi-step, wordy problems, which is where most Nottingham students we see lose marks at the back end. The first session marks a recent paper of each to find which is bleeding more, and the early lessons target that paper first."
  - q: "How can I tell which GCSE Maths topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Look at the question numbers on a recent mock, not just the total. Higher papers build from number and basic algebra, through ratio and geometry, to the problem-solving and circle-theorem questions at the end. If the marks are going in that final block, it is exam technique. If they go missing from the start, it is more likely a foundation from an earlier year, and the tutor rebuilds that first."
