  - q: "Why is my child struggling with Science when they seem to understand it?"
    a: "Often it isn't the science at all. If your child can explain a topic out loud but still loses marks, look at the calculations and graphs. A shaky step in rearranging an equation, or a unit that never got converted, costs the mark even when the idea is right. The other usual reason is how answers are written: a six-mark question needs points linked together, not a list. Both can be fixed, and the first lesson with a tutor shows which one it is."
  - q: "Foundation or Higher: which Science paper should my child sit?"
    a: "It depends where your child is now, and the school makes the final decision. Higher tier is aimed at grades 4 to 9 and Foundation at 1 to 5, so a Foundation paper can't give more than a 5. On Combined Science, that's 4-4 to 9-9 on Higher and 1-1 to 5-5 on Foundation. If your child is working around a 4 or 5, it's a real choice, so ask the school which tier they're planning. A tutor can then prepare your child for the paper they'll actually sit."
