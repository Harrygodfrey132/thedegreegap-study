  - q: "Should my child take Combined or Triple Science?"
    a: "Combined is worth two GCSEs graded from 9-9 down, Triple is worth three separate grades. Triple carries more content and more exam time, so it suits a student who is comfortable across all three and wants the Sciences to lead somewhere. Combined is not a lesser route and it does not close A-Level Sciences at most colleges, though entry requirements vary and are worth checking on the current page. What matters more than the label is whether the weakest of the three is being carried by the other two, and that is what a diagnostic session shows."

If Physics has been sliding since Year 10 while Biology is solid, one specialist on the actual problem does more than a generalist spreading an hour across everything. We would rather say that than sell a tidier arrangement."
