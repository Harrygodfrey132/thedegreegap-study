  - q: "How can I tell which A-Level Maths topics my child is losing marks on?"
    a: "Compare textbook work against exam questions on the same topic. If the textbook exercises are fine and the past paper questions are not, the gap is interpretation rather than technique, and that is both the most common problem and the least often named. If the working starts and collapses partway, the gap is algebraic fluency. If the page is blank, the question was not recognised at all. Those three need different plans and look identical on a report."
