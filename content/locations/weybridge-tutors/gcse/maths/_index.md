  - q: "Calculator or non-calculator: which paper needs more practice?"
    a: "Whichever one's losing more marks. AQA, Edexcel and OCR each set three papers that count the same: one non-calculator and two calculator. The non-calculator paper shows up weak arithmetic and algebra, especially with fractions and surds (square roots left in an exact answer). The calculator papers reward problem-solving and accuracy over a long chain of working. Lots of children drop more marks late in the non-calculator paper than they'd expect. A tutor compares recent papers of both kinds and puts the hours where the gap is widest."
  - q: "Does my child need higher tier for A-Level Maths?"
    a: "Yes, if A-Level Maths is the aim, because foundation doesn't go above a 5. Higher tier runs from a 9 down to a 4, with a 3 allowed for a near miss. For a child sitting at a 3 or 4 in Year 10, foundation can be the safer route to a secure pass. The school has the final say. A tutor can help you make the case in Year 10 by looking at which questions your child's getting right every time, not just the overall mark."
