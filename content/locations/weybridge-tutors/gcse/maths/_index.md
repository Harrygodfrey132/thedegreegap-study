  - q: "Should my child focus on the calculator or non-calculator paper?"
    a: "Both count equally, so the answer depends on which one is losing more marks. The non-calculator paper exposes weak arithmetic and algebraic manipulation, especially with fractions and surds. The calculator papers reward multi-step problem-solving and accuracy over a long chain of working. Many students drop more marks late in the non-calculator paper than they expect. A tutor compares recent scripts from both and puts the hours where the gap is widest."
  - q: "Should my child sit foundation or higher tier GCSE Maths?"
    a: "Higher tier is usually right for a student aiming at A-Level Maths, since foundation caps at grade 5. Higher allows a 4 at the bottom, with a safety net of a 3 in some series, and goes to a 9. For a student sitting at a 3 or 4 in Year 10, foundation can be the safer route to a secure pass. A tutor can help make the call in Year 10 by looking at which questions your child can answer reliably, not just the overall mark."
