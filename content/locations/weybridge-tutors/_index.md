faq_1_q: "Should my child stay at Heathside for sixth form or go to Esher?"
faq_1_a: "It depends on the subjects, and the grade asked for each one can differ. Heathside Weybridge Sixth Form publishes a general requirement plus a subject list, and for A-Level Maths it asks for a grade 7. Esher Sixth Form College asks for five GCSEs at grade 4 or above, then a grade 6 in the subject for A-Level Maths and the Sciences. Both review their figures between years, so read the current course directory. A tutor's part is making sure the grade is there for whichever door your child picks."
