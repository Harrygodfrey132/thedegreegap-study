faq_2_q: "When are predicted grades worked out, and can a tutor still make a difference?"
faq_2_a: "Teachers base predicted grades mostly on Year 12 work, and they're sent with university applications in the autumn of Year 13. So Year 12 is when tutoring has the most room to help, because the work it improves is the work the prediction rests on. A tutor can't change a predicted grade directly. What they can change is your child's tests and homework. Starting in Year 13 still helps with the final exams, which are what the offer depends on in the end."
