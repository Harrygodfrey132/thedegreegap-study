  - q: "My child's last Science mock was disappointing. How do I see what went wrong?"
    a: "Ask to see the marked paper itself, because the total won't tell you much. Go through it with your child and notice what kind of question lost marks. Short recall questions point to revision. Questions about a practical or a graph usually point to method instead. And blanks next to the calculations often mean an equation your child couldn't rearrange, or didn't know how to start. Those last two are skills, and they respond well to one-to-one practice. Tell us what you spot on the call, and we'll pick tutors with it in mind."
  - q: "Why do you ask which exam board my child's Science is on?"
    a: "Because the boards differ, mostly in how questions are worded and how the practicals are named and asked about. AQA calls them required practicals, Edexcel says core practicals, and OCR has practical activity groups. The science underneath is much the same, but each board lays out its papers differently and rewards answers in its own way. Schools around Letchworth choose their own boards, so we ask which one your child's on before suggesting anyone. If you're not sure, we'll help you find out on the call."
