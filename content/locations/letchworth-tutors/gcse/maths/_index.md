  - q: "Should my child focus on the calculator or the non-calculator paper?"
    a: "Neither should be left out, because all three papers count equally. On AQA, Edexcel and OCR there's one non-calculator paper and two calculator ones, so a calculator's allowed for two thirds of the marks. Eduqas is different, with two papers. Without a calculator, it's quick, accurate number work that scores, like fractions and rearranging. With one, it's multi-step problems done carefully. Most children drop more early marks on the non-calculator paper, and more near the end of the last calculator paper. A tutor looks at a recent mock to see which is costing your child more."
  - q: "Should my child sit Higher or Foundation?"
    a: "It depends on how your child's doing now, and what the grade is for. Higher covers grades 9 to 4, and a 3 can be awarded if your child just misses a 4. Foundation goes up to a 5. If A-Level Maths is the plan, Higher is usually the route, since grades 6 to 9 are only on those papers. If your child's sitting around a 3 or 4 in mocks, Foundation can make a pass more likely. Teachers make the final call, often after a mock. A tutor can tell you which side of the line your child's work sits on, and why."
