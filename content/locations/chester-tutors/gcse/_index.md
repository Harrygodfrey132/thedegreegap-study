faq_1_q: "How do I find out which exam board my child is on?"
faq_1_a: "Ask the subject teacher, or look at the top of a past paper your child has been given, where the board is printed. It matters more than parents expect, because AQA, Edexcel, OCR and, for some students around Chester, WJEC and Eduqas word questions differently, mark extended answers to different structures and print different amounts of formula support. We confirm it on the consultation call before proposing a tutor, and we will help you work it out if nobody at home is sure."
