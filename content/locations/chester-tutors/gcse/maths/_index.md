The non-calculator paper rewards quick arithmetic and confident algebra, and it's where an older gap tends to show, because there's no calculator to cover for it. The calculator papers are about accuracy over several steps and careful reading of the wordy questions at the back. Most children are noticeably weaker on one of them. The first lesson shows which."
