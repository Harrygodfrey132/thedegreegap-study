faq_3_q: "My child sailed through GCSEs. Why is A-Level suddenly so hard?"
faq_3_a: "Because it's a different kind of course, not because they've stopped being able. GCSE rewarded knowing things. A-Level rewards using them on questions your child hasn't seen before, and there's far more to cover with far less hand-holding. Bright children often hit this hardest, since they've never had to practise the way A-Level needs. A tutor helps them build that habit early, usually through past questions marked the way the board marks them."
