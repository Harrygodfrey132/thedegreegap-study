faqs:
  - q: "What is the hardest part of GCSE English for Aylesbury students?"
    a: "Two things, every year. The unseen poetry comparison, because it asks a student to build an argument about a text they have never seen, under time, with no revision to fall back on. And the Question 5 writing task, which carries a large share of the Language paper and routinely gets the leftover minutes. Neither is about how much a student knows. Both respond quickly to being taught as a method, which is why they are usually where we start."
  - q: "When is the best time to start GCSE English tutoring in Year 11?"
    a: "September to February, and ideally before the mocks that shape sixth-form predicted grades. English rewards a run of weeks rather than a burst, because essay writing improves through repetition and feedback rather than revision. Starting in April is still worth doing, but the work narrows to exam technique and timing rather than building the analysis that lifts a band. The autumn gives room for both."
