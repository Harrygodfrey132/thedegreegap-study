faq_4_q: "My son spends his free periods revising and his marks aren't moving. What's going wrong?"
faq_4_a: "Usually the method, not the effort. Rereading notes and copying them out feels like work, but A-Level questions ask him to use what he knows on something unfamiliar, and rereading doesn't practise that. A tutor swaps some of those hours for past-paper questions, marked against the real scheme, so he can see exactly where the marks go. The same hours tend to count for far more once they're spent on questions."
