  - q: "What is the hardest part of GCSE English for Leamington students?"
    a: "The writing question, though almost nobody names it. Students find unseen poetry hardest in the moment, but the writing question is where the most marks go unclaimed, because technical accuracy is treated as a fixed trait rather than something that can be practised. Comparison comes next, because it wants a genuinely different paragraph shape, one that holds two texts at once rather than walking through them in turn."
  - q: "How much English work should my child do between sessions?"
    a: "Sixty to ninety minutes, and it should be writing rather than reading. One timed paragraph or one full question, done under the clock, is worth more than an evening spent re-reading notes and highlighting quotations. Re-reading feels productive and changes very little, which is why a student can revise for hours and watch the grade stay put. Beyond ninety minutes the returns fall away sharply."
