faq_1_q: "My daughter's already getting As. What would a tutor actually add?"
faq_1_a: "Less than you might think, and sometimes it isn't worth it. Where it helps is when her goal really needs the top grade. Getting from an A to an A* is about judgement, not knowing more, and a busy classroom rarely has time for one-to-one feedback on judgement. For someone already on As, a short block of lessons before the mocks often does more than a whole year. And if we think she doesn't need us at all, we'll say so on the call."
faq_2_q: "How does my child's school actually decide predicted grades?"
faq_2_a: "Mostly from class work and mocks, plus the teacher's judgement, checked across the department so predictions stay consistent. There's no national formula. In most sixth forms the grade is set towards the end of Year 12, from that year's work and exams, and some review it after an early mock in Year 13. So the time to change what goes to UCAS is before those papers, not after."
