  - q: "What is the difference between AQA, Edexcel and OCR GCSE Science, and does it matter for tutoring?"

A tutor drilling Physics equations from memory that your child's board prints on the paper is spending your money in the wrong place. Maidstone schools do not all sit the same board for GCSE Science, so we confirm the exact specification on the consultation call and match to it."
