  - q: "Why is my child struggling with GCSE English when they read so much?"
    a: "Usually because nobody taught them to plan an answer, and reading a lot hides that for years. A strong reader in Maidstone can understand a text completely and still write a summary of it, because understanding and arguing are different skills and only one of them is marked. The moment it goes wrong is often Year 9, when the extract questions start asking why a writer made a choice and the student keeps answering what the choice was. A GCSE English tutor's first job is finding which of the four papers shows that most, and starting there."

The last fifteen minutes are a timed go at a fresh question from a past paper, so the habit gets built under the clock and not only in conversation. Everything happens on Lessonspace, our shared whiteboard, and the recording is there afterwards."
