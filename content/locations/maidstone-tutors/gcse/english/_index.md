  - q: "My child reads loads. Why are they struggling with GCSE English?"
    a: "Usually because planning an answer hasn't clicked yet, and reading a lot can hide that for years. A keen reader can understand a text completely and still write a summary of it. Understanding and arguing are different skills, and only one gets marked. It often starts to go wrong in Year 9, when extract questions begin asking why a writer made a choice and your child keeps explaining what the choice was. The tutor's first job is finding which of the four papers shows it most, and starting there."
