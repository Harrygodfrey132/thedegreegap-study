The work is to read this year's list, find the gap between it and the current grades, and close that gap subject by subject. A Year 10 start gives the most room. faq_3_q: "My son got a 3 in GCSE Maths. Can you help with the resit?"
faq_3_a: "Yes. A student who starts at MidKent College or a sixth form without a 4 in Maths or English keeps studying it until they have one, so the resit is coming either way and it's better planned than drifted into. Resit work is different from first-time tutoring: we start from the summer paper and find the two or three topics that cost the most, rather than working through the whole specification again. Eight weekly hours from September is usually enough to move a 3 to a 4, and the November sitting is the first chance to do it."
