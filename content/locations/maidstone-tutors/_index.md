faq_2_q: "Can a tutor help my child move to a grammar sixth form from another school?"
faq_2_a: "Yes. We hear this one a lot in Maidstone. Maidstone Grammar, MGGS, Invicta and Oakwood Park each publish their own GCSE requirements for joining in Year 12, and change them from year to year. The subjects your child wants to carry on usually need more than the general entry grade. So the job is to read this year's list, find the gap between it and your child's current grades, and close it one subject at a time. Starting in Year 10 gives the most room. It's the same for the sixth forms at Maplesden Noakes, Valley Park and St Simon Stock, and for courses at MidKent College."
faq_3_q: "My son got a 3 in GCSE Maths. Can you help with the resit?"
faq_3_a: "Yes. If your son starts at MidKent College or a sixth form without a 4 in Maths, he'll normally keep studying it after sixteen. So the resit's coming either way, and it's better planned than drifted into. Resit lessons aren't the same as first-time tutoring. The tutor starts from what went wrong in the summer. They find the two or three topics that cost the most, rather than working through the whole course again. Eight weekly lessons from September is often enough to move a 3 to a 4, and the November sitting is the first chance to do it. Nobody can promise the grade, but it's a realistic plan."
