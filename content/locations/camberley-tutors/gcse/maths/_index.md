  - q: "Does the Maths grade matter for sixth form around Camberley if my child isn't taking A-Level Maths?"
    a: "Yes, more than most people expect. Collingwood asks for a 6 in GCSE Maths for A-Level Physics. At The Sixth Form College Farnborough, Physics asks for a 7, and your child would take A-Level Maths alongside it. Gordon's School's current guide asks for at least a 5 in Maths before any A-Level at all. So the grade can matter even for a child who's dropping Maths after Year 11. Figures like these get revised, so it's worth looking at this year's guides before you settle on a target."
  - q: "Should my child sit Higher or Foundation Maths?"
    a: "It comes down to the grade your child needs. Higher papers cover grades 4 to 9, and a 3 can be given just below a 4. Foundation stops at a 5. That makes Higher the only route to the 7 that A-Level Maths needs at Collingwood or the Farnborough sixth form college. If a 4 is the real goal and Higher mocks keep going badly, Foundation can take some of the pressure off. Entries are the school's decision in the end, so it's worth asking your child's Maths teacher what they think."
  - q: "How much should my child practise between Maths lessons?"

Year 11 is busy, with mocks and college open evenings landing in the same few months, so tell the tutor if it's too much and they'll scale it back."
