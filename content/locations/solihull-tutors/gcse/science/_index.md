  - q: "Should my child take triple or combined Science?"
    a: "Triple gives three separate grades and more content, combined gives two grades covering the same three subjects in less depth. Neither shuts the door on A-Level Science, though some sixth forms ask for a higher combined grade than they would ask for in the separate subject, so check the Tudor Grange, Arden or Solihull Sixth Form College course page before deciding. The honest test is Year 9 performance plus whether the extra content will get revised or resented. A diagnostic before the option forms go in beats a general impression."
  - q: "What happens in a typical Science session?"
    a: "An hour in three parts. The first fifteen minutes go on last week's work, marked with the reasons said out loud rather than written in a margin. The middle half hour takes one thing and rebuilds it, often a required practical drawn out with the variables labelled, or a calculation method worked step by step. The last fifteen minutes are exam questions on that one thing, marked against the real scheme there and then, so the student sees what the marks were actually for."
  - q: "How do you vet your Science tutors?"

Every tutor sits down with Harry or Joe before we put them in front of a family."
