  - q: "The school's predicted a lower Maths grade than we thought. Why?"
    a: "Because a prediction reflects what happens against the clock. It's built from the mocks plus the teacher's judgement, and there can be a big difference between what your child does in a quiet room and in ninety minutes of exam. A child who understands every topic but finishes two thirds of the paper gets predicted on the finishing, not the understanding. That's why a tutor should read the actual mock, not just the grade. The two often tell different stories."
