  - q: "Why is my child's Maths predicted grade lower than we expected?"
    a: "Predictions come from mock performance plus teacher judgement, and the difference parents notice most is between what a student can do in a quiet room and what they can do in ninety minutes with a clock running. A child who understands every topic and finishes two thirds of the paper will be predicted on the finishing, not the understanding. The first thing a tutor should do is read the actual mock script rather than the grade, because the two tell different stories."
