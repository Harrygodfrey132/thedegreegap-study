faq_3_a: "Partly, yes. The tutor's main job is the subject itself, but a lesson in the same slot each week, with set work in between, gives a Year 12 a fixed point to plan the rest around. What they can't do is read or check coursework drafts, because exam board rules limit the help anyone can give on those. If organising is the main worry, mention it on the call."
