  - q: "Is it the non-calculator paper we should be worrying about?"
    a: "Often it's a mix. On AQA, Edexcel and OCR there are three papers, and all three count equally: one without a calculator and two with one. Eduqas sets two. The non-calculator paper finds out fast whether fractions, negatives and algebra are secure, so gaps from Year 8 tend to show up there first. The calculator papers lean more on problem-solving over several steps. Lots of children lose marks early on the non-calculator paper and late on the last calculator one, when they're tired. Your child's latest mock will show which pattern is theirs."

And if mocks are already coming back strong, a few lessons before the summer may be all that's needed. On the call, Harry or Joe will tell you plainly which of these it sounds like."
