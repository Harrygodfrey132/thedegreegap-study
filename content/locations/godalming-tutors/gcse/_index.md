faq_1_q: "What does Godalming College look for at GCSE?"
faq_1_a: "At least five GCSEs at grade 4 or above, according to the college's current guide. Then each course adds its own, like a 6 in Maths for A-Level Maths or a 5 in English Literature for the Literature A-Level. English Language matters more than most, because the college says anything below a 4 there narrows the choice of courses a lot. These details can change, so check this year's course page for each subject your child likes the look of. Once you know the targets, a tutor can start on whichever grade is furthest off."
faq_2_q: "Should we wait for the Course Advice Session before finding a tutor?"
faq_2_a: "You don't need to. The session is a conversation about likely grades and course choices, and it helps if your child walks in already knowing where they stand. If a subject is worrying you now, that's reason enough to start. Omo's son began in Year 10, which gave his tutor six months on English before he'd even reached Year 11. Starting in Year 11 is common too."
faq_3_q: "Will the tutor know the papers my child's school has chosen?"
faq_3_a: "Yes. Tutors on our platform work with AQA, Edexcel, OCR and WJEC Eduqas, at both tiers where a subject has them. Schools choose their boards subject by subject, so a child at Rodborough and a friend at Charterhouse might be sitting quite different English papers. Your child can usually tell you the board for each subject, and the school's exams page may list them too. Every tutor we then put forward will know that board."
