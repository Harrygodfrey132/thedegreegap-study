  - q: "How do I know if my child needs a Maths tutor or just more revision?"
    a: "Look at their working on a recent paper. Clean, sensible working that still loses marks near the end is an exam technique gap, and that is the fastest thing a tutor fixes. Blank space or crossed-out attempts on multi-step questions is a method confidence gap, and that one genuinely needs a person rather than more hours. If a student is scoring well across mocks already, targeted practice on two or three topics beats a full weekly programme, and we will say so."
