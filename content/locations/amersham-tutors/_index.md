faq_2_q: "What happens in the first session with a tutor?"
faq_2_a: "Mostly diagnosis. The tutor works through a recent marked paper with your child, asking them to talk through the answers that went wrong, and listens for the moment the explanation runs dry. That point shows whether the problem is content from an earlier year, the way answers get written down, or simply running out of time. The plan for the following weeks comes out of that hour. You get a short note afterwards saying what was found and what happens next."
