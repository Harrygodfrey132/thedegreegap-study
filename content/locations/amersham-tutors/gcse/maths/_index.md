  - q: "Is my child better off on Higher or Foundation?"
    a: "That comes down to where their mocks sit and what they want to do next. Foundation tops out at a grade 5, so if your child needs a 6 or more for a sixth form course, it has to be Higher. Higher covers grades 4 to 9, and a child who only just misses the 4 can be given a 3. That makes it a risk for a child hovering around a 3 or 4. Schools usually look at tiers in Year 10 and settle them in Year 11. So a tutor's check-up in Year 10 gives the school better evidence, while there's still time to talk it through."
  - q: "Can I tell from a mock which Maths topics are costing my child?"
    a: "Yes, and you don't need to know any Maths to start. Forget the total for a minute and find the questions that lost marks. The first few are routine methods, so marks gone there mean a gap in what your child knows. The middle mixes ratio, geometry and algebra. The last third is longer problem solving and proof. A child who's fine until then usually knows the methods but struggles to pick one, or to set out the reasoning. And on any show-that question, check the working. That's nearly always where the marks went."

With dyslexia, the wordy problem questions get broken into labelled parts. Tell us about any access arrangements, like extra time, so timed practice can match them."
