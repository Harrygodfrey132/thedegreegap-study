  - q: "My child loves books. Why doesn't that show in their English grade?"
    a: "Because the exam rewards a different skill from reading. Reading widely builds vocabulary and instinct, but a GCSE answer needs your child to name a method, quote briefly, explain the effect and link it to the question, all against the clock. Planning under pressure takes its own practice, and in a busy class there's rarely time to go through each child's plan. So the best idea often turns up last. The tutor finds the missing step and rebuilds it, usually starting with planning."

But the weekly load stays modest, so it fits around your child's other subjects."
