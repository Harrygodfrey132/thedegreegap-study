  - q: "What's the difference between English Language and English Literature?"
    a: "Language tests reading unseen texts and writing your own, under time pressure. Literature tests what your child knows about the set texts and poems, and how well they can argue about them. Most children are stronger in one. The tutor splits the time between them according to where the marks are being lost."
  - q: "How can I help with English at home?"
    a: "Talk about the set texts. Ask what your child thinks of a character and why, and get them to back it up with a line from the book. That's most of a Literature answer. Reading anything regularly helps with Language. The tutor will also set short timed writing tasks between lessons."
