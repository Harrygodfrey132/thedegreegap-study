## Common questions

**Who is it for?** Year 12 and Year 13 students and their parents. Teachers and careers staff are welcome too, and can register to receive the replay for use in school.

**Does a student have to speak or appear on camera?** No. Questions go in the chat, and nobody is asked to speak or switch a camera on.

**Is it recorded?** Yes. Everyone who registers gets the replay and the slides by email, whether or not they attend live.

**Will you contact my child?** No. We only get in touch about tutoring if a parent ticks the box asking us to. Students are never asked, and never contacted about tutoring.

**Is there a cost, now or later?** No. The session, the slides and the replay are free, and nothing has to be bought at any point.

**I work in a school. Can I share it?** Yes, please do. The wording above is ready to paste, and there is nothing to book or host. Email us if you would like a newsletter image.
