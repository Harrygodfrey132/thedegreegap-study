## Common questions

**Who is it for?** Parents and guardians of students in Year 10 or Year 11, whatever grade their child is working at right now. Parents of Year 9 students planning ahead are welcome too.

**Who runs it?** Harry Godfrey and Joe Clark, the co-founders of The Degree Gap. They have run this session for parents before, and the reviews above come from those evenings.

**Do I have to speak or appear on camera?** No. Questions go in the chat, and nobody is asked to speak or switch a camera on.

**I can't make the evening. Should I still register?** Yes. Everyone who registers gets the slides and the free resources by email afterwards.

**Is there a cost, now or later?** No. The session and the resources are free, and nothing has to be bought at any point.

**Will you call me about tutoring?** Only if you tick the box asking us to. Otherwise you will just hear from us about the webinar.
