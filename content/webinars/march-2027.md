## Who's on the panel

Three of our specialist tutors, each taking one core subject:

- **Maths, Emily.** A qualified Maths teacher with four years in London schools, including a grammar school and students with English as an additional language.
- **English, Jess.** An English Language and Literature specialist with three years of one-to-one and small-group tutoring.
- **Science, Callum.** A Cambridge natural sciences graduate (biochemistry specialism), now a graduate medical student, with over six years of tutoring behind him.

## What each specialist covers

**Maths.** Why exam questions increasingly ask students to work backwards or apply a method in an unfamiliar context, how to build a web of the crossover topics that appear together, and how to make past-paper practice active rather than passive.

**English.** The language and structural devices the mark scheme actually tests, how to signal genuine analysis to the examiner, and a clear plan for the Question 5 writing task that carries half the marks on each Language paper.

**Science.** Why understanding the content beats drilling past papers too early, what "understanding" really means when a question drops your child into an unfamiliar scenario, and the precise scientific language that turns a roughly-right answer into full marks.

## Who it's for

Parents of students in Year 10 or Year 11, across all exam boards, whatever grade your child is working at right now. Come with questions, there is time for live Q&A at the end.
