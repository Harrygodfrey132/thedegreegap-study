## How to choose the right subject tutor

**Start with the exam board.** AQA, Edexcel, OCR and the other boards set different papers, even in the same subject. A tutor who knows your child's board knows which questions come up and how they are marked. The school can tell you which board each subject uses.

**Check the tier in Maths and the sciences.** Foundation and Higher papers lead to different grades, and the decision usually gets made during Year 11. Our guide to [foundation or higher tier](/blog/foundation-or-higher-tier-gcse-year-11/) explains what is at stake.

**Decide what the lessons are for.** A gap in the content needs teaching. Marks lost on answers your child finished usually come down to exam technique, which is often quicker to fix. A marked mock paper shows you which it is, and our guide to [reading a mock result](/blog/do-mock-exams-matter/) shows you how.

**Talk to us before you commit.** Every family talks to Joe or Harry on a free call before we suggest a tutor, so the match is made around what your child needs.
