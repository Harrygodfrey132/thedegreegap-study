assess_heading: "How Edexcel A-Level Maths is examined, paper by paper"
assess_intro: "Edexcel (spec 9MA0) is fully linear: three papers at the end of Year 13, each 2 hours and 100 marks, with no coursework and nothing banked from Year 12. What makes Edexcel different is the split. Two whole papers are pure, and all the applied content sits together in the third. So the pure carries two thirds of the grade."
papers:
  - name: "Paper 1: Pure Mathematics 1"
    detail: "2 hours, 100 marks, a third of the whole A-Level. Proof, algebra, functions, coordinate geometry, sequences, trigonometry, differentiation and integration. Pure only."
  - name: "Paper 2: Pure Mathematics 2"
    detail: "2 hours, 100 marks, more pure across the same content. Because Papers 1 and 2 are both pure, a slow start with Year 12 pure quietly costs marks on two thirds of the grade."
  - name: "Paper 3: Statistics and Mechanics"
    detail: "2 hours, 100 marks, split into a statistics section and a mechanics section of about 50 marks each. The statistics half draws on the large data set released in advance, so your child needs to have met it before exam day."
  - name: "The formula booklet"
    detail: "Edexcel gives students a formula booklet and allows a graphical or scientific calculator. The booklet is long, and some formulae aren't in it, so knowing your way around it matters."
