Pearson doesn't grade the papers one by one."
papers:
  - name: "Paper 1: Pure Mathematics 1 (9MA0/01)"
    detail: "Any pure topic can come up: proof, algebra and functions, coordinate geometry, sequences and series, trigonometry, exponentials and logarithms, differentiation, integration, numerical methods and vectors. The questions tend to get harder as the paper goes on."
  - name: "Paper 2: Pure Mathematics 2 (9MA0/02)"
    detail: "The same pure content as Paper 1. There's no separate Paper 2 topic list, so a topic can turn up on either paper, or both. On both pure papers, watch for questions marked 'Solutions relying on calculator technology are not acceptable', where every step has to be written down."
  - name: "Paper 3: Statistics and Mechanics (9MA0/03)"
    detail: "Two booklets in one two-hour sitting. Paper 31, Statistics (50 marks): sampling, data presentation, probability, the binomial and normal distributions, and hypothesis testing, with some questions on the large data set. Paper 32, Mechanics (50 marks): units, kinematics, forces and Newton's laws, and moments."
  - name: "The formula booklet"
    detail: "Edexcel's green 'Mathematical Formulae and Statistical Tables' booklet is given out in every paper. It's one booklet for AS and A-Level Maths and Further Maths, so your child needs to know which pages are theirs. Those pages include the binomial series, series sums, compound-angle identities, the quotient rule, integration by parts and the suvat equations, plus binomial and normal tables."

    detail: "Met Office weather data for May to October 1987 and May to October 2015, from Camborne, Heathrow, Hurn, Leeming and Leuchars in the UK, and Beijing, Jacksonville and Perth abroad. # ───────────── FAQ ─────────────
faq_items:
  - q: "How do I know if my child sits Edexcel A-Level Maths?"
    a: "The papers say Pearson Edexcel and carry the codes 9MA0/01, 9MA0/02, 9MA0/31 and 9MA0/32, and the front asks for the green formulae booklet. Edexcel and Pearson Edexcel are the same board, so either name on the timetable means the same thing. If you're not sure, we'll check with you on the free call before matching anyone."
  - q: "What's on Edexcel A-Level Maths Paper 1, Paper 2 and Paper 3?"
    a: "Papers 1 and 2 are both pure, and either can test any pure topic, from proof and algebra to calculus, numerical methods and vectors. Pearson doesn't split the topics between them, so revising for one is revising for the other. Paper 3 is the applied paper: a 50-mark statistics booklet (9MA0/31), with questions on the large data set, and a 50-mark mechanics booklet (9MA0/32), sat together in two hours."
  - q: "What's in the Edexcel A-Level Maths formula booklet?"
    a: "The green booklet holds the harder formulae and the statistical tables, not the basics. The A-Level Maths pages give the binomial series, series sums, compound-angle identities, small-angle approximations, the quotient rule, integration by parts, Newton-Raphson, the trapezium rule and the suvat equations. Pearson lists what isn't in it, including the quadratic formula, laws of logs, double-angle formulae, sine and cosine rules, and the chain and product rules."
  - q: "What is the Edexcel large data set?"
    a: "It's real Met Office weather data, May to October in 1987 and 2015, from five UK weather stations and three overseas. Students study it during the course, and some Paper 3 statistics questions assume they know it. In June 2025 one used sunshine and rainfall at Leeming in 2015, and the examiners noted that some students didn't know the data's units."
  - q: "Where do I get Edexcel A-Level Maths past papers and mark schemes?"
    a: "On Pearson's qualifications website, on the A-Level Mathematics (2017) pages, with mark schemes and examiners' reports alongside. The June 2025 papers are there now. The examiners' reports are worth reading too, because they say where students lost marks. A tutor can mark a past paper with your child the way an examiner would and show where each mark went."
  - q: "Where can I see the Edexcel A-Level Maths grade boundaries for 2026?"
    a: "On Pearson's grade boundaries page, where the June 2026 GCE boundaries were published on results day, 13 August 2026. For Maths the grade is set on the total out of 300 across the three papers. The boundaries move a little each year with how hard the papers were, so an old boundary is a rough guide rather than a target."
  - q: "Is Edexcel A-Level Maths harder than AQA or OCR?"
    a: "No. Every board teaches the same Department for Education content, and an A is an A whichever board awarded it. Edexcel just arranges it differently, with two all-pure papers and the applied maths together in Paper 3, where AQA and OCR put a pure section in front of the applied one. It's a different shape, not a harder exam."
  - q: "How much does an Edexcel A-Level Maths tutor cost?"
    a: "Lessons are from £37 an hour, and the price for your tutor is agreed with you before anything is booked. You don't sign a contract or pay anything upfront, and both the consultation call and the video meeting are free. After that it's simply the lessons your child has, and you can stop at any point."
  - q: "What if my child doesn't get on with the tutor?"
