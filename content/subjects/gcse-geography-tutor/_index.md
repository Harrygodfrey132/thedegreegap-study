faq_items:
  - q: "My child memorises the case studies but still loses marks. Why?"
    a: "It's the thing we hear most about Geography, and it's almost never a memory problem. The exam wants the handful of facts that fit the question, then a judgement on top. That's a different skill from recall, and it's the one a tutor can teach quickly, because it's mostly technique."
  - q: "What are the 9-mark questions, and why do they matter so much?"
    a: "They're the long answers on AQA, and they're where the top marks are won or lost. Your child has to use case study detail to answer the exact question, then weigh it up and reach a view. The tutor sets real 9-markers, marks them the way an examiner would and shows your child where the marks went."
  - q: "Do the tutors know my child's exam board?"
    a: "Yes, and it matters more in Geography than parents expect. AQA, Edexcel A, Edexcel B and the OCR routes set different case studies and mark the long answers differently, 9 marks on AQA and 8 on Edexcel. Some boards also end on a paper built around a pre-release booklet. We pick tutors who know your child's board. Not sure which it is? We can work it out on the call."
  - q: "Can a tutor help with the fieldwork and skills questions?"
    a: "Yes. These trip a lot of students up because they're about applying skills under pressure, not learned content. Reading an OS map, giving a six-figure grid reference, pulling data off a graph, writing about their own fieldwork. A tutor can rehearse all of it one-to-one, which is hard to find time for in a full class."
  - q: "How much does it cost?"
    a: "Lessons start from £37 an hour. The exact price depends on the tutor, and we'll always agree it with you before anything is booked. There's no contract, no joining fee and nothing to pay upfront. You just pay for the lessons you have, and the first chat with us and the video meeting with your tutor are free."
  - q: "What if we don't click with the tutor?"
    a: "Just let us know and we'll find someone else, with no charge and no awkward conversation. It happens, and we'd much rather sort it than have your child sit through lessons that aren't working. It's also why you hear from two or three tutors before choosing, rather than being handed one."
  - q: "Does online tutoring actually work for Geography?"
    a: "It does. On Lessonspace the tutor can put an OS map or a graph on the shared whiteboard, annotate it with your child and mark a long answer line by line. Every lesson has a replay too, which is really handy for going back over a case study the night before a test."
  - q: "My child has ADHD, dyslexia or anxiety. Can you still help?"
    a: "Yes. We'll ask about this on the call, and lots of tutors on our platform work with children who learn differently or find school stressful. One-to-one lessons take away the pressure of a classroom, and the tutor can break a long answer into small steps. Tell us what works for your child and we'll pick tutors with that in mind."
  - q: "Do you cover where we live?"
    a: "Yes, we cover the whole of the UK. All our lessons are online, so it doesn't matter whether you're in a city or a village. It also means we can pick the tutor who's the best fit for your child, rather than whoever happens to live nearby."
