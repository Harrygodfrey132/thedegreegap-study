faq_items:
  - q: "My child did well in GCSE History. Why are they struggling at A-Level?"
    a: "It's one of the most common calls we get. GCSE rewards knowing what happened and explaining it clearly. A-Level asks your child to weigh evidence, judge how convincing a historian is and hold an argument all the way to a conclusion. That step from telling the story to arguing about it catches out lots of students who did well at 16. And it can be taught."
  - q: "They know the content, so why do the essays keep losing marks?"
    a: "Usually the essay is describing when it should be arguing. In lessons the tutor marks real essays against the levels and works on the shape of the answer: an argument set out in the opening, paragraphs that take a position, and evidence used to prove a point rather than fill space. That's where the top-band marks sit."
  - q: "Can a tutor help with the source and interpretations questions?"
    a: "Yes, and it's often the quickest area to improve. Source questions reward working out who wrote something, when and why, and what that does to its value. Interpretations questions reward weighing one historian's view against another's rather than agreeing with both. The tutor practises each one until your child knows what the mark scheme wants."
  - q: "Do the tutors know my child's exam board and options?"
    a: "Yes. AQA, Edexcel and OCR mark in different ways, and each board has its own options, from the Tudors to Russia, Germany and Civil Rights. We'll pick tutors who know your child's board and periods. Not sure which it is? We can work it out together on the call."
  - q: "Can a tutor help with the coursework?"
    a: "Yes, within the exam board's rules. The tutor can help your child choose a workable question, find and weigh the historians, and structure the argument so it holds together. Everything handed in stays your child's own work. But a tutor who knows how it's marked makes a big difference to how it comes together."
  - q: "How much does it cost?"
    a: "Lessons start from £37 an hour. The exact price depends on the tutor, and we'll always agree it with you before anything is booked. There's no contract, no joining fee and nothing to pay upfront. The consultation call with us and the video meeting with your tutor are both free."
  - q: "What if we don't click with the tutor?"
    a: "Just let us know and we'll find someone else, with no charge and no awkward conversation. It happens, and we'd much rather sort it than have your child sit through lessons that aren't working. It's also why you see two or three tutors before choosing, rather than being handed one."
  - q: "Does online tutoring actually work for History?"
    a: "It does. On Lessonspace the tutor and your child can both write on the same essay or source, so the tutor can show exactly where a paragraph stops arguing and starts retelling. Some tutors also set a practice essay between lessons and mark it. And every lesson has a replay to look back on."
  - q: "Do you cover where we live?"
    a: "Yes, we cover the whole of the UK. All our lessons are online, so it doesn't matter where you are. It also means we can pick the tutor who knows your child's periods best, rather than whoever happens to live nearby."
