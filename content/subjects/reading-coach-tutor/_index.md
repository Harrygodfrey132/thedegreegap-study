# ───────────── FAQ ─────────────
faqs:
  - q: "How do I know if my child needs a reading coach?"
    a: "A few things tend to give it away. Reading aloud that is slow or full of guesses. A reading-age letter from school that came back lower than you hoped. Or a child who used to love stories and now finds every excuse not to open a book. Any of those is a good reason to talk to us, and we will tell you honestly on the call if we do not think a coach is what your child needs."
  - q: "My child has decided they hate reading. Can that really change?"
    a: "It is one of the most common reasons families come to us, and yes, it usually can. A child who says they hate reading has often just found it hard for a long time, so avoiding it feels easier than failing at it. A patient coach starts below the point of struggle, finds books that are actually worth finishing, and lets small wins do the rest. We cannot promise a bookworm, but the dread tends to lift."
  - q: "What does a reading coach actually do in a session?"
    a: "The first session is mostly listening, hearing your child read and working out where it comes unstuck. After that it depends on what they found, maybe the sounds they never quite got, maybe the meaning slipping past them. Sessions stay light and one-to-one, with reading your child can manage and then a small stretch, so it never tips back into the thing they dread."
  - q: "Do you do reading sessions in person, or only online?"
    a: "Everything runs online, through Lessonspace, our shared whiteboard. The coach and your child read off the same screen, and the coach can highlight a word or bring up a new page in a second. Most children settle into it quickly, and plenty concentrate better one-to-one online than in a busy classroom. We do not do in-person."
  - q: "What age is a reading coach right for?"
    a: "Mostly primary and lower-secondary, the years where reading is meant to become automatic and school moves on assuming it has. That said, age matters less than where your child actually is with reading. Tell Joe what is going on and he will tell you honestly whether it is a good fit."
  - q: "Are the consultation and the trial calls really free?"
    a: "Yes. The 40-minute consultation with Joe is free. The trial calls with the coaches he suggests are free too. You only start paying once you have chosen a coach and weekly sessions begin. No catch, no card details up front."
