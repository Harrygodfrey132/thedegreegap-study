faq_items:
  - q: "My child knows the theory. Why are the marks low?"
    a: "Usually it's application. A-Level Business answers have to be about the business in the question, using its figures and its situation. A general answer that would fit any company scores lower. Then there's evaluation, because the long questions want a balanced argument and a clear, justified decision. A tutor practises both with your child, question by question."
  - q: "How does a tutor help with the long evaluative questions?"
    a: "By going through real answers against the mark scheme and showing your child where the marks are. Often the analysis is fine but the judgement is missing, or it's one sentence tacked on at the end. The tutor teaches a simple way to weigh up both sides and say what the decision depends on, then sets practice questions to mark together."
  - q: "Do your tutors cover the calculations?"
    a: "Yes. Break-even, ratio analysis, investment appraisal and the other number topics come up across the boards, and with practice they're some of the most dependable marks on the paper. Just as important is explaining what the figure means for the business, which is where many answers stop short."
  - q: "Which exam boards do your tutors know?"
    a: "AQA, Edexcel, OCR and Eduqas. Each sets its papers and case studies differently, so we'll match your child with a tutor who knows their board. With Edexcel, for instance, the context for one paper is released before the exam, so preparing for it is part of the work. Not sure which board it is? It'll be on a mock paper."
  - q: "My child didn't take GCSE Business. Will they be behind?"
    a: "Not really. Lots of students start Business for the first time in Year 12, and the course doesn't assume they've studied it before. Early lessons can cover the key terms and how exam answers are built, so your child feels on solid ground before the harder material arrives."
