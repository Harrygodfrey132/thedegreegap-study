faq_items:
  - q: "Which modules can you help with?"
    a: "The core undergraduate ones: microeconomics, macroeconomics, econometrics, and the maths and statistics for economics modules that sit underneath them. That covers things like constrained optimisation, game theory, growth models and regression. We also help with options such as development, labour or financial economics, depending on the tutor. Tell us on the call what your course covers and we'll match you to it."
  - q: "I did well in A-Level Economics. Why am I struggling now?"
    a: "Because it's a different subject in a lot of ways. A-Level rewards diagrams and evaluation, while a degree rewards being able to set up a model and solve it. Many students also come in without Further Maths, so calculus and matrices feel like a second course on top of the first. It's a very common gap, and it's one a tutor can close quickly once they see where it starts."
  - q: "Can a tutor help with econometrics and the software?"
    a: "Yes. A lot of students can run a regression but aren't sure what the output is telling them. A tutor goes through it with you: what the coefficients mean, when the standard errors can't be trusted and how to write up the results. Some of our tutors also help with the software courses use, such as Stata, R or Python, so it's worth mentioning which one yours uses."
  - q: "Will the tutor do my coursework or problem sets for me?"
    a: "No. A tutor will teach you the method, go through similar questions with you and give you feedback on your understanding. Assessed work has to be your own, and your university's rules on that matter. The aim is that you can do the next problem set without help, and that's what lifts the exam marks too."
  - q: "Can you help with a dissertation?"
    a: "We can help you plan it, choose a method that fits your question, understand the econometrics and make sense of your results. The research and the writing stay yours. Students usually find a few sessions early on, while the question and data are still being decided, save the most time."
