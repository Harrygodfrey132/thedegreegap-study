faq_items:
  - q: "How is A-Level Further Maths set out?"
    a: "On every board, half the course is compulsory core pure. That's topics like complex numbers, matrices, proof by induction and more calculus. The other half is made up of options, usually picked by the school, such as further mechanics, further statistics, discrete or decision maths, or extra pure. Tell us which options your child sits and we'll find a tutor who knows them."
  - q: "Which exam boards do your tutors cover?"
    a: "AQA, Edexcel, OCR A and OCR B (MEI). The core pure is much the same across them, but the papers and the options aren't. Edexcel puts the options on separate papers, for example, and MEI has its own set of options. If you're not sure of the board, check the front of a mock paper or ask us on the call."
  - q: "My child copes with A-Level Maths but not Further Maths. Why?"
    a: "It's a really common pattern. Further Maths assumes every A-Level Maths method is already second nature, then builds on it straight away. Proof by induction and complex numbers also ask your child to think in a new way, and there's no easy topic to coast through. A tutor can find the one or two ideas that are holding the rest up, and fix those first."
