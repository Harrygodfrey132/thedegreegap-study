  - q: "What topics are on OCR GCSE Maths Paper 1?"
    a: "Paper 1 can test any topic on the Foundation course, and it's a calculator paper. OCR's specification says any topic area may be assessed on any paper for that tier, so Papers 2 and 3 draw on the same list. The Higher equivalent of Paper 1 is Paper 4. If a revision list says 'Paper 1 topics', check it's an OCR one, because AQA's and Edexcel's Paper 1 is the non-calculator paper."
