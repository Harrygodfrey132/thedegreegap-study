  - name: "Paper 1 (1PH0/1)"
    detail: "1 hour 45 minutes, 100 marks. Motion and forces, conservation of energy, waves, light and the electromagnetic spectrum, radioactivity and astronomy. Speed-time graphs, wave speed and half-life calculations, all of which reward clear working."
  - name: "Paper 2 (1PH0/2)"
    detail: "1 hour 45 minutes, 100 marks. Energy and forces doing work, electricity and circuits, static electricity, magnetism and the motor effect, electromagnetic induction, the particle model, and forces and matter. This is the paper full of circuit and F = BIL calculations."
  - name: "The core practicals"
    detail: "Tested in the written papers. Your child investigates things like the resistance of a wire, force and extension, wave speed and specific heat capacity in class, then gets asked about the method, the variables and the data in the exam."
  - name: "The equations in the exam"
    detail: "Edexcel gives students the equations, so the marks depend on picking the right one for a situation your child hasn't seen before, then working a multi-step calculation through without missing a line. That choosing step is where a tutor helps most."
