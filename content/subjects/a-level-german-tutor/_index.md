faq_items:
  - q: "The speaking exam and the research project scare my child. Can a tutor help?"
    a: "Yes, and it's the part most students dread. In the speaking exam your child discusses topics from the course, then presents their Individual Research Project and answers questions on it. The IRP is a subject they choose and research themselves, linked to a German-speaking country or community. A tutor helps them pick a topic with enough depth, find German sources to read and practise answering questions they haven't seen. The research and the opinions stay your child's own."
  - q: "Why are the case endings still going wrong?"
    a: "Because A-Level German grammar asks a lot. Adjective endings, the four cases, verbs moving to the end of a clause and the subjunctive all have to be right at speed. Most students half know the rules. A tutor finds which ones slip under pressure and drills them in short, regular bursts until they stick. Both the essays and the translation into German reward that accuracy."
  - q: "Can a tutor help with the translation?"
    a: "Yes. Translating into English is mostly about reading the German closely and then writing English that sounds natural, not a word-for-word copy. Translating into German is a grammar test, as one wrong ending can cost the mark. The tutor works through past translations with your child and shows them where the marks tend to go."
  - q: "My child's essay on the film just retells the plot. What helps?"
    a: "That's the most common way these essays lose marks. The examiner wants an argument about the work, its characters and themes, backed up with precise evidence. The tutor works from the film or book your child is studying, helps them plan an answer to the actual question, and marks practice essays written in German."
  - q: "Do the tutors know my child's exam board?"
    a: "Yes. AQA, Edexcel and Eduqas all set A-Level German, and each has its own list of films and books and its own way of laying out the papers. Your child will be matched with someone who has taught that board's course. If you're not sure which board it is, a mock paper will say, or we can check together on the call."
