# ───────────── FAQ ─────────────
faq_items:
  - q: "What's the spec code for Edexcel GCSE Biology?"
    a: "It's 1BI0, Pearson Edexcel's GCSE Biology. The papers are coded 1BI0/1F and 1BI0/2F at Foundation, or 1BI0/1H and 1BI0/2H at Higher, and the code is printed on the front of every paper, so a mock is the quickest way to check. Edexcel International GCSE Biology is a different qualification with its own papers. We'll confirm which one with you on the call."
  - q: "What topics are on Edexcel GCSE Biology Paper 1?"
    a: "Paper 1 covers Topics 1 to 5: key concepts in biology, cells and control, genetics, natural selection and genetic modification, and health, disease and the development of medicines. So cells, microscopy and enzymes, mitosis, stem cells and the nervous system, DNA and inheritance, evolution and genetic engineering, then pathogens, immunity, antibiotics and heart disease. It's ten questions and 100 marks in 1 hour 45 minutes."
  - q: "What's on Edexcel GCSE Biology Paper 2?"
    a: "Paper 2 covers Topic 1 again, plus Topics 6 to 9: plant structures and their functions, animal coordination, control and homeostasis, exchange and transport in animals, and ecosystems and material cycles. That means photosynthesis and plant transport, hormones, the menstrual cycle, blood glucose and the kidneys, the heart, blood and respiration, then ecosystems and the carbon, water and nitrogen cycles."
  - q: "Does my child get an equation sheet in Edexcel GCSE Biology?"
    a: "No. Ofqual's equation sheets for the 2025 to 2027 exams are only for GCSE Maths, Physics and Combined Science, so there's no sheet in the summer 2027 Biology papers. Calculators are allowed. But the equations named in the specification have to be learned, including cardiac output = stroke volume × heart rate and BMI = mass ÷ height², along with magnification and percentage change in mass."
  - q: "Where can we find Edexcel GCSE Biology past papers and mark schemes?"
    a: "On Pearson's own past papers page: choose GCSE, then Biology, then the exam series. Question papers, mark schemes and examiner reports are free there, though Pearson keeps the most recent papers for teachers only, so schools can use them as mocks. A tutor works through the rest with your child and marks each answer against Pearson's scheme, the way an examiner would."
  - q: "Where are the Edexcel GCSE Biology grade boundaries for 2026?"
    a: "On Pearson's grade boundaries page. The June 2026 GCSE document went up on results day, 20 August 2026, with Biology listed for Foundation and Higher. Each boundary is the lowest combined mark across the two papers, out of 200, that earns a grade. They shift with how hard the papers turned out, so last summer's numbers are a guide, not a promise for next year."
  - q: "Is Edexcel Biology harder than AQA?"
    a: "No. Both are set to the same Ofqual rules, graded 9 to 1, with the same weighting on knowing, applying and analysing. What actually differs is where topics sit. Edexcel puts genetics on Paper 1 and photosynthesis and respiration on Paper 2, the other way round from AQA, and tests Topic 1 on both. So a revision guide for the wrong board can have your child revising the right topic for the wrong paper."
