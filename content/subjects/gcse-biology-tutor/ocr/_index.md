faq_items:
  - q: "Is my child doing OCR Gateway or Twenty First Century Biology?"
    a: "Check the code on a mock paper. J247 is Gateway Science Biology A, the course this page covers, split into topics B1 to B7. J257 is Twenty First Century Science Biology B, where both papers, Breadth in biology and Depth in biology, test all eight chapters. Tutors on our platform teach both routes, and we'll confirm which one with you on the call."
  - q: "What's on OCR Gateway Biology Paper 1?"
    a: "Paper 1 at Foundation, or Paper 3 at Higher, covers B1 to B3 plus B7 practical skills. That's cells and microscopy, DNA, enzymes, respiration and photosynthesis (B1), then diffusion, osmosis, mitosis, stem cells, the heart and transport in plants (B2), then the nervous system, hormones and homeostasis, including diabetes and the kidneys (B3). It's 90 marks in 1 hour 45 minutes."
  - q: "What's on OCR Gateway Biology Paper 2?"
    a: "Paper 2 at Foundation, or Paper 4 at Higher, covers B4 to B6 plus practical skills, and assumes your child still knows B1 to B3. B4 is ecosystems, decomposition and the carbon and water cycles. B5 is inheritance, natural selection and evolution. B6 is global challenges: biodiversity, feeding the human race, and disease, immunity and treatments. Some of its marks are synoptic, joining topics together."
  - q: "Does OCR give an equation sheet for GCSE Biology?"
    a: "No. Ofqual's formula and equation sheets for the 2025 to 2027 exams cover GCSE Maths, Physics and Combined Science only, so there isn't one in the summer 2027 Biology papers. A scientific or graphical calculator is allowed, and at least 10% of the marks test maths, from magnification and percentages to means and graphs. Those are skills to practise, not things to look up."
  - q: "Where can we find OCR Gateway Biology past papers?"
    a: "On OCR's J247 qualification page, under Assessment, where question papers, mark schemes and examiner reports go back to 2018 for both tiers.
