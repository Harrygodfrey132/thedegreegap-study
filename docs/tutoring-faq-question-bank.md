# Tutoring FAQ question bank — 100 commercial-intent questions

A writing brief for the outer-ring questions parents ask *before* they hire anyone: what it costs, whether it works, how to pick someone, what they are signing up to. None of these are subject-content questions. Every one of them is a question a parent types into Google or asks in a Facebook group with their card half out of their pocket.

Machine-readable version, with slugs and priorities, is in `Content Plan - Tutoring FAQs.csv` in this folder — hand that to a writer or VA.

## How to use this

- **Facebook:** one question per post. Post the question as the hook, answer it in 3–5 short lines in the post itself, then link the full piece. Questions answered only by a link get throttled by the algorithm and skipped by parents.
- **Blog:** each question is one post, titled as the question. Answer it in the first paragraph, then earn the rest of the read — same pattern as the existing cost pieces.
- **Priority column:** High = write and post first; these carry buying intent or get asked weekly. Low = fill-in material for quiet weeks.
- **Funnel stage:** Bottom = the parent is choosing a tutor this month. Middle = researching. Top = still deciding whether to bother at all. A healthy Facebook month mixes all three; don't post ten price questions in a row.
- **UK figures date fast.** Anything with a price in it needs a year stamp and an annual refresh, like the existing 2026 cost pieces.
